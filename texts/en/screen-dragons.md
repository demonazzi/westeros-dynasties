### vhagar · Vhagar ✎
House of the Dragon gives Vhagar two deaths with different circumstances from Fire & Blood: Laena asks her to end her suffering after childbirth, and Aemond loses control of her during the pursuit of Lucerys. The book has Laena die of childbed fever and leaves Aemond's intentions during the chase uncertain.

### sheepstealer · Sheepstealer ✎
The adaptation gives Sheepstealer's story to Rhaena. In Fire & Blood, Nettles earns his trust by bringing him freshly killed sheep, then fights and flies with him during the Dance. Rhaena instead remains in the Vale, where her own dragon, Morning, hatches.

## Драконы Дейенерис

### drogon · Drogon ✎
The television finale has Drogon destroy King's Landing, melt the Iron Throne after Daenerys's murder and carry her body away. The published novels end much earlier in his story: he and Daenerys are in the Dothraki sea, with their return to Westeros still unwritten.

### rhaegal · Rhaegal ✎
Jon rides Rhaegal in Game of Thrones. After surviving the battle at Winterfell, the dragon is killed by scorpion bolts from Euron's fleet near Dragonstone. In the published novels Rhaegal remains alive and has no established rider.

### viserion · Viserion ✎
The show has the Night King kill Viserion, raise him as an undead dragon and use him to breach the Wall. He falls when the Night King is destroyed. In the novels Viserion is alive near Meereen; that history with the Others has not occurred.
