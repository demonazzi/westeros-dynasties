## Бракены при Мейгоре и в Танец драконов

### lyle_b · Lyle Bracken ✎
One of the Warrior’s Sons who faced Maegor in his trial of seven. None survived.

### olyver_b · Olyver Bracken ✎
He abandoned Maegor’s Kingsguard for Jaehaerys, who sent him to the Wall. His subsequent rebellion ended with Walton Stark executing him with Ice.

### humfrey_b · Humfrey Bracken ✎
Stone Hedge initially backed Aegon II. Daemon’s capture of the castle forced Humfrey to submit; he later joined the army marching for Aegon III.

### amos_b · Amos Bracken ✎
He killed Samwell Blackwood at the Burning Mill, then fell to an arrow. The shot was credited to Black Aly, without certainty.

### humfrey_kids · Другие дети Хамфри Бракена ✎
Daemon held them hostage after taking Stone Hedge.

### raylon · Raylon Rivers ✎
He brought the Burning Mill survivors home and surrendered when he discovered Daemon held the Bracken family.

## Стоунхедж в конце третьего века

### brk_lord18 · Лорд Бракен, отец Джоноса ✎
The unnamed Bracken shown here as Jonos's father. No name or separate history is recorded for him; Jonos himself holds Stone Hedge during the War of the Five Kings.

### jonos_b · Jonos Bracken ✎
Robb’s death brought him back to the Iron Throne. He then besieged Raventree Hall, using royal service to pursue Bracken claims against the Blackwoods. Jaime negotiated a surrender and reduced the lands Jonos demanded. He also required Jonos to send a daughter to court, making the settlement a restraint on both houses.

### harry_r · Harry Rivers ✎
The brotherhood reported his death at Lannister hands. Tytos Blackwood disputed Jonos’s claim to be his father.

### barbara_b · Barbara Bracken ✎
Barbara is Jonos Bracken's eldest daughter, born to his first wife. During the burning of Stone Hedge, Gregor Clegane raped one of Jonos's daughters. The victim is not identified, so it is uncertain whether she was Barbara.

### jayne_b · Jayne Bracken ✎
Jayne is Jonos Bracken's second daughter by his first wife. One of the five sisters was raped by Gregor Clegane when Stone Hedge was burned, but the account does not name her.

### catelyn_b · Catelyn Bracken ✎
Catelyn is Jonos Bracken's third daughter and the eldest born to his third wife. Gregor Clegane raped one of Jonos's five daughters during the burning of Stone Hedge; which sister suffered the assault is unknown.

### bess_b · Bess Bracken ✎
Bess is the fourth of Jonos Bracken's daughters, the second born to his third wife. She or one of her sisters was raped by Gregor Clegane during the burning of Stone Hedge; the victim's identity is left unspecified.

### alysanne_b · Alysanne Bracken ✎
Alysanne is Jonos Bracken's youngest daughter, born to his third wife. The burning of Stone Hedge included Gregor Clegane's rape of one of the sisters, but the account does not establish that Alysanne was the victim.

### jonos_sib · Брат или сестра Джоноса Бракена ✎
Jonos Bracken's unnamed sibling, the parent of Hendry. Neither a name nor a gender is established. Hendry was killed by Lannister forces during the liberation of Stone Hedge in 299 AC.

### hendry · Hendry Bracken ✎
He died in the fighting to retake Stone Hedge.

### brk_dau · Дочь лорда Бракена, возможно сестра Джоноса ✎
Possibly the proposed bride whom Brynden Tully refused; her identity remains uncertain.
