## Баттервеллы при Мейгоре и Джейехерисе I

### alton_b · Alton Butterwell ✎
Maegor’s master of coin proposed his widowed sister as a royal bride, pointing to her seven children as evidence of fertility.

### alton_sis · Сестра Алтона Баттервелла ✎
Considered as a possible wife for Maegor because she had already borne seven children. He chose other brides.

### beatrice · Beatrice Butterwell ✎

## Белые Стены

### btw_gf · Лорд Баттервелл, десница Эйгона IV ✎
Aegon IV’s Hand and the first lord of Whitewalls. The dragon egg he received from the king later became a tourney prize.

### btw_gf_daus · Дочери лорда Баттервелла, деда Амброза ✎
Court history claimed Aegon IV slept with all three during a visit to their father.

### btw_gf_son · Сын лорда Баттервелла, отец Амброза ✎
Ambrose Butterwell's unnamed father. Both Ambrose and his paternal grandfather served as Hand of the King; no separate account of the intervening generation survives.

### ambrose · Ambrose Butterwell ✎
A cautious courtier whose caution failed him at Whitewalls. He had served Aegon IV as master of coin and Daeron II as Hand, and lost sons on both sides of the first Blackfyre rising. His second wedding supplied the occasion for another conspiracy. Bloodraven exposed it, took most of his lands and had Whitewalls dismantled. Ambrose retained only a fraction of his former estate.

### ambrose_sons · Сыновья Амброза Баттервелла ✎
Two died fighting on opposite sides at the Redgrass Field; the third fell to the Great Spring Sickness.

### ambrose_daus · Дочери Амброза Баттервелла ✎
Both were married before Ambrose’s second wedding. The elder married Whitewalls’ captain of guards.
