## Завоевание и Джейехерис I

### loren · Loren the Last
Defeat at the Field of Fire ended his kingship. Submission to Aegon preserved Casterly Rock and made him Warden of the West.

### lyman · Lyman Lannister
He sheltered Aegon the Uncrowned and Rhaena from Maegor. The Shivers killed him in 59 AC.

### loren_sons · Другие сыновья Лорена Ланнистера
Loren Lannister had several sons after the Conquest. The younger sons are not named. Lyman Lannister is generally believed to have been his son, although their relationship has not been explicitly confirmed.

### tymond · Tymond Lannister
Daella rejected him as a husband, disliking his reputation with women.

### lyman_sons · Законные сыновья Лаймана Ланнистера
One courted Jeyne Westerling before Maegor married her.

### tyler_hill · Tyler Hill
He led five hundred men for Aegon the Uncrowned and later proposed marriage to Rhaena.

## Танец драконов

### lan_lord10 · Лорд Ланнистер, отец Джейсона и Тиланда
Lord of Casterly Rock, otherwise unnamed.

### jason_l · Jason Lannister
He led the western army for Aegon II and died at the Red Fork.

### tyland · Tyland Lannister
Moving the treasury beyond Rhaenyra’s reach helped cripple her government. Torture left him blind and mutilated but did not reveal its location. After the war, the former green served Aegon III loyally as Hand, directing reconstruction and setting aside the old factional division. Winter fever killed him in 133 AC.

### cerelle_l · Cerelle Lannister
In 133 AC, Johanna Lannister put forward Cerelle and her sister Tyshara as possible brides for Aegon III. Both were unmarried and of suitable noble birth. Aegon eventually married Daenaera Velaryon.

### tyshara · Tyshara Lannister
Tyshara was considered as a bride for Aegon III. After the Maiden's Day Ball, she was found in bed with a groom and said he had climbed through her window. The scandal ended her prospects of becoming queen. Rumour blamed Unwin Peake, who wanted to remove rivals to his own daughter, but his involvement was never established.

### jason_l_daus · Три другие дочери Джейсона Ланнистера
Jason and Johanna Lannister had three other daughters besides Cerelle and Tyshara. Their names are unknown. Along with their brother Loreon, they belonged to the legitimate family of the Lord of Casterly Rock.

### loreon · Loreon Lannister
He inherited as a small child while his mother confronted ironborn attacks on the west.

### jason_l_bast · Внебрачные дочери Джейсона Ланнистера
Dalton’s raiders captured him and his mother at Lannisport.

## Род Тайвина

### tya · Tya Lannister
Her only known son died in infancy.

### gerold · Gerold Lannister
The Golden maintained the house’s wealth and authority. Death took his elder sons Tywald and Tion before him, leaving the inheritance to Tytos. His second marriage to Rohanne Webber connected Casterly Rock with a woman whose earlier life had centred on Coldmoat.

### tywald · Tywald Lannister
He died from wounds received in the Peake Uprising.

### tion_l · Tion Lannister
Killed at the Wendwater during the fourth Blackfyre rising.

### tytos_l · Tytos Lannister
The Laughing Lion wanted affection more than confrontation. Loans went unrepaid and bannermen learned to disregard his authority. Tywin answered the weakness while his father still lived, destroying the Reynes and Tarbecks and making obedience a matter of fear.

### jason_g · Jason Lannister
He commanded western troops in the War of the Ninepenny Kings. Contemporary testimony attributes his death to illness, despite later claims that Maelys killed him.

### damon_l · Damon Lannister
Damon was Jason Lannister's only child by his first wife, Alys Stackspear, who died giving birth to him in 244 or 245 AC. He married Ella Lannister of Lannisport. Their son Damion later became castellan of Casterly Rock.

### joanna · Joanna Lannister
Tywin’s severity relaxed with her. Their marriage ended when she died giving birth to Tyrion, a loss that shaped the family’s later relationships.

### stafford · Stafford Lannister
Robb’s surprise attack destroyed his unready army at Oxcross.

### jason_g_sons · Двое младших сыновей Джейсона Ланнистера
These two unnamed sons of Jason Lannister and Marla Prester were brothers to Stafford and Joanna. The couple had three sons and three daughters altogether.

### jason_g_daus · Две младшие дочери Джейсона Ланнистера
Jason Lannister and Marla Prester had two unnamed daughters besides Joanna. They were sisters to Stafford and belonged to a younger branch of the family, their father being Gerold Lannister's younger son.

### tywin · Tywin Lannister
He restored Lannister authority during his father's weak rule. He served for years as Aerys II's Hand, then resigned as their relationship deteriorated. At the end of Robert's Rebellion, he captured and sacked the capital, securing his house's place in the new regime through Cersei's marriage. He later directed the war against the Starks and became his grandson's Hand. Tyrion killed him after escaping from prison.

### kevan · Kevan Lannister
He spent years organising armies and government beside Tywin, then tried to steady Tommen’s reign as regent after Cersei’s arrest. That competence made him a target: Varys murdered him to prevent the crown recovering its strength before the arrival of Aegon’s cause.

### genna · Genna Lannister
Plain-spoken and politically observant, she understood her brothers without idealising them. Emmon’s grant of Riverrun brought her into its disputed inheritance. Her conversations with Jaime weigh what Tywin’s death has cost the family and how precarious the Freys’ new possession remains.

### tygett · Tygett Lannister
A capable warrior who struggled with Tywin’s dominance. He died of the pox.

### gerion · Gerion Lannister
His humour made him especially welcome to Tyrion. He sailed east to recover Brightroar and disappeared on the voyage towards Valyria. No return or recovered remains established his fate.

### tyrek · Tyrek Lannister
Robert’s young squire vanished in the King’s Landing riot; his fate remains unknown.

### joy_hill · Joy Hill
Born in 288 AC to Gerion Lannister and a common woman named Briony, Joy was still a small child when her father disappeared at sea. Jaime thinks of her as a sweet, lonely girl. As part of his agreement with the Freys, Tywin promised her in marriage to one of Walder Frey's natural sons when she came of age.

### cersei · Cersei Lannister
All three of her known children were fathered by Jaime; protecting that secret drives many of her decisions. After Robert's death, she seeks to rule through her sons and remove rivals. Her restoration of the Faith Militant turns against her: she is arrested, subjected to a humiliating penance and left awaiting trial.

### jaime · Jaime Lannister
He joined the Kingsguard while still a youth. He killed Aerys II to prevent the destruction of the capital, earning the name Kingslayer while leaving his motives unexplained for years. Losing his right hand forces him to reconsider his life and duties. In the books, he works to end the war in the riverlands, while his bond with Cersei deteriorates.

### tyrion · Tyrion Lannister
Educated and quick-witted, he nevertheless faces contempt because of his stature. He served as acting Hand and played a major part in defending the capital on the Blackwater. Accused of Joffrey's murder, he escaped after killing Shae and his father. In Essos he seeks a place in the next struggle for power, but has yet to meet Daenerys by the end of the published novels.

### lancel · Lancel Lannister
At Cersei’s direction he supplied Robert with strong wine during the fatal hunt. He later became her lover, but injury and guilt drove him towards the Faith. Abandoning his marriage and lands for the Warrior’s Sons made him a witness whose knowledge threatened the queen.

### willem_l · Willem Lannister
Rickard Karstark murdered the captive boy at Riverrun.

### martyn_l · Martyn Lannister
A squire taken captive by northern forces.

### janei · Janei Lannister
Janei is Kevan Lannister and Dorna Swyft's youngest child and only daughter, born in 296 or 297 AC. Her older brothers are Lancel, Willem and Martyn. She is still a small child during the War of the Five Kings.
