### samwell_bw · Samwell Blackwood
Lord of Raventree Hall at the start of the Dance of the Dragons and a supporter of Rhaenyra. The feud between the Blackwoods and Brackens quickly became part of the wider conflict between blacks and greens. Amos Bracken killed him at the Battle of the Burning Mill. His eleven-year-old son Benjicot then became head of the house.

### alysanne_bw · Alysanne Blackwood
Black Aly. A skilled archer, she fought for the blacks and was credited with killing Amos Bracken with an arrow. After the war, she helped secure a pardon for Corlys Velaryon and negotiated with Cregan Stark over the fate of the northerners who had marched south. She became Cregan's second wife and bore four daughters.

### benjicot · Benjicot Blackwood
He inherited Raventree Hall at eleven. Despite his age, he fought in several decisive battles of the Dance for Rhaenyra and earned the name Bloody Ben. With the young Tullys, he was among the commanders known as the Lads. He survived the war and helped defeat Borros Baratheon's army.

## Любовница Эйгона IV

### melissa_bw · Melissa Blackwood
One of Aegon IV's mistresses. She bore Mya, Gwenys and Brynden Rivers, the future Bloodraven. Despite her position, she was well liked at court, including by Naerys.

## Жёны Старков и королева

### melantha · Melantha Blackwood
Melantha became Lady of Winterfell as Willam Stark's second wife, following Lyanne Glover's death. Their children were Edwyle and Jocelyn.

### betha · Betha Blackwood
She married Aegon for love before he was chosen as king. Her strong character earned her the name Black Betha. She later tried to stop her children from marrying in defiance of the crown's agreements with the great houses.

## Время книг

### tytos_bw · Tytos Blackwood
Lord of Raventree Hall during the War of the Five Kings and a supporter of the Tullys and Robb Stark. He helped free Edmure and supported Robb's proclamation as king. After the northern alliance was defeated, he continued to hold his castle until Jaime secured its surrender. The peace terms required him to give his son Hoster as a hostage and make concessions to the Brackens.

### brynden_bw · Brynden Blackwood
Heir to Raventree Hall.

### lucas_bw · Lucas Blackwood
He served in Robb Stark's army and died at the Red Wedding.

### hoster_bw · Hoster Blackwood
Named after Hoster Tully. A tall young man who loved books and knew the history of the riverlands well. After Raventree Hall surrendered, he was handed over to Jaime Lannister as a hostage. In their conversations, he explained the origins of the old feud between the Blackwoods and Brackens.

### edmund_bw · Edmund Blackwood
Known to his family as Ben, Edmund is adventurous, according to his father. During the settlement at Raventree, Tytos offered him as Jaime Lannister's hostage and suggested he become Jaime's squire. Jaime already had enough squires and chose Hoster instead, leaving Edmund at home.

### alyn_bw · Alyn Blackwood
Alyn is Tytos Blackwood's fifth son of Raventree Hall, younger than Edmund and older than Bethany. He was born between 288 and 291 AC.

### bethany_bw · Bethany Blackwood
Bethany is a gentle, often laughing girl who has never traveled more than a day's ride from Raventree Hall. Jonos Bracken urged Jaime to take her hostage because Tytos doted on his only daughter. Her father resisted, and Jaime took Hoster instead.

### robert_bw · Robert Blackwood
He died of illness during the siege of Raventree Hall.
