## Айронвуды

### gyles_y · Gyles Yronwood ✎
A mob killed him while he helped retrieve Joffrey’s body during the King’s Landing riots.

### edgar_y · Edgar Yronwood ✎
Wounds from his duel with young Oberyn became infected and killed him. Poison was suspected, never proved.

### ormond_y · Ormond Yronwood ✎
After Edgar’s death, reconciliation with the Martells brought Quentyn to Yronwood as a ward.

### anders · Anders Yronwood ✎
Quentyn’s foster father knighted him and sent Cletus on his eastern mission. Arianne suspected ambitions against her own inheritance.

### yrn_bro · Брат Андерса Айронвуда, отец Арчибальда ✎
Anders Yronwood's unnamed brother was the father of Ser Archibald, who accompanied Quentyn Martell to Essos. His descent from Ormond Yronwood is not established.

### ynys · Ynys Yronwood ✎
Quentyn Martell fell for the blonde, blue-eyed Ynys when he first arrived at Yronwood as a ward, but never told her. She later married Ryon Allyrion, heir to Godsgrace, and they have at least two sons.

### ynys_sons · Сыновья Инис Айронвуд и Риона Аллириона ✎
Ynys Yronwood and Ryon Allyrion have at least two sons whose names and ages are not given. Their paternal half-brother is Ser Daemon Sand, Ryon's bastard son.

### cletus · Cletus Yronwood ✎
Quentyn’s close friend died when corsairs attacked their ship near the Disputed Lands.

### gwyneth · Gwyneth Yronwood ✎
At twelve, Gwyneth was a slight, dark-haired, dark-eyed girl in a family of fair-haired, blue-eyed relatives. Clever with her hands and quick with words, she hoped to marry Quentyn Martell when she grew older. Quentyn imagined returning to marry her, but died in Meereen after trying to take Daenerys's dragons.

### archibald · Archibald Yronwood ✎
He accompanied Quentyn to Meereen and burned his hands trying to extinguish the prince’s flames. After Quentyn’s death, Barristan gave him a mission to regain the Windblown’s support.
