## Мормонты

### mor_lord · Лорд Мормонт, отец Джиора и Мейдж
Longclaw’s former owner taught Jeor that a man would not lie before a heart tree.

### jeor · Jeor Mormont
He left Bear Island for the Night’s Watch and became its Lord Commander. Jon’s rescue of him from a wight earned the gift of Longclaw. His great ranging ended in disaster at the Fist of the First Men; survivors retreated to Craster’s Keep. There, mutineers killed him. His final wish was that Jorah return to Westeros and take the black.

### jorah · Jorah Mormont
Debt drove him to sell poachers into slavery and flee Ned Stark’s judgment. In exile he served Daenerys while informing Varys, then abandoned the spying too late to save her trust. Banished, he seized Tyrion as a hoped-for peace offering, only for both to become slaves. They escaped and joined the Second Sons. Published Winds of Winter chapters show him participating in the company’s move back to Daenerys’s side.

### maege · Maege Mormont
Robb sent her and Galbart Glover into the Neck to contact Howland Reed before the Red Wedding. Her subsequent whereabouts are unknown.

### dacey · Dacey Mormont
She tried to fight her way out of the Red Wedding. Ryman Frey’s axe killed her.

### alysane · Alysane Mormont
She helped recover Deepwood Motte for Stannis. A published Winds of Winter chapter sends her north with Justin Massey and Jeyne Poole.

### alysane_dau · Дочь Алисаны Мормонт
Alysane Mormont's elder child is a nine-year-old girl in A Dance with Dragons. Her father is unknown. Alysane, who is unmarried, jokes that Mormont women turn into bears and find mates in the woods; the children's actual parentage remains unexplained.

### alysane_son · Сын Алисаны Мормонт
Alysane Mormont's younger child is a two-year-old boy in A Dance with Dragons. He has a nine-year-old sister, and his father is not identified. His mother's tale of bear mates leaves the question of his parentage open.

### lyra_m · Lyra Mormont
Maege Mormont's third daughter accompanied her mother and Jorelle into the Neck to seek the crannogmen before the Red Wedding. Alysane later reports that both sisters are still with Maege.

### jorelle · Jorelle Mormont
Jorelle, called Jory, is Maege Mormont's fourth daughter. She and Lyra went with their mother into the Neck to find the crannogmen before the Red Wedding. Alysane subsequently says the sisters remain with Maege.

### lyanna_m · Lyanna Mormont
Her reply to Stannis insisted that Bear Island recognised only a King in the North named Stark.
