## Гудбразеры

### gunthor_g · Gunthor Goodbrother ✎
After Dalton’s murder, a quarrel over Lysa Farman ended with him killing Alester Wynch. Their occupation forces faced revolt on Fair Isle.

### arthur_g · Arthur Goodbrother ✎
He killed the Reach’s lord admiral Leo Costayne during the invasion of the Iron Islands.

### gorold · Gorold Goodbrother ✎
The Lord of Hammerhorn could muster a substantial fleet. He accepted Euron at the kingsmoot and joined the attack on the Shields.

### greydon · Greydon Goodbrother ✎
Sent to inform the family’s other branches of Balon Greyjoy’s death.

### gormond · Gormond Goodbrother ✎
He brought Aeron the news that Balon had died.

### gran_g · Gran Goodbrother ✎
Gran is tall and difficult to distinguish from his identical brothers, Greydon and Gormond. He admits Aeron Greyjoy to Hammerhorn when the priest comes to speak with Gorold after Balon's death.

### gysella · Gysella Goodbrother ✎
Gysella is one of Gorold Goodbrother's twelve daughters. She is at Hammerhorn when Aeron Greyjoy arrives, but her father sends her and her sister Gwin away before speaking with the Damphair.

### gwin · Gwin Goodbrother ✎
Gwin is present at Hammerhorn when Aeron visits Gorold to discuss Balon's death and Euron's return. Gorold dismisses her and Gysella from the room before their conversation.
