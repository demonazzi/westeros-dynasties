## Сигард в конце третьего века

### mal_lord · Лорд Маллистер, отец Дениса ✎
Lord of Seagard, otherwise unnamed in the surviving references.

### denys_mal · Denys Mallister ✎
The experienced commander of the Shadow Tower withdrew from the election after Sam persuaded him to support Jon Snow.

### mal_sib · Брат или сестра Дениса Маллистера, родитель Джейсона ✎
Jason Mallister's unnamed parent was a sibling of Denys Mallister. This branch connects the Lord of Seagard to his uncle, the long-serving commander of the Shadow Tower.

### jason_mal · Jason Mallister ✎
He killed Rodrik Greyjoy defending Seagard, then fought for Robb. Threats to execute his captive son finally forced him to surrender his castle to the Freys.

### patrek · Patrek Mallister ✎
He rode with Robb’s companions in the Whispering Wood. Captivity after the Red Wedding made him the Freys’ leverage against Seagard.

### jeffory · Jeffory Mallister ✎
Aerys executed him after he accompanied Brandon Stark to King’s Landing.
