## Мандерли при Джейехерисе I и в Танец драконов

### theomore · Theomore Manderly
He entertained Queen Alysanne during her northern progress and had four marriages.

### theomore_sons · Сыновья Теомора Мандерли
Theomore Manderly had at least three sons. During her visit to White Harbor in 58 AC, Queen Alysanne arranged betrothals between his two younger sons and two of her ladies-in-waiting. The young men are not named.

### mara_m · Mara Manderly
She attended Alysanne on Dragonstone early in Jaehaerys’s reign.

### jessamyn_m · Jessamyn Manderly
Alysanne’s cupbearer during the queen’s visit to the North.

### desmond · Desmond Manderly
He sent men to fight for Rhaenyra and negotiated a marriage to Joffrey Velaryon that the prince’s death prevented.

### medrick · Medrick Manderly
He marched south with the northern forces supporting Rhaenyra.

### torrhen_m · Torrhen Manderly
Service to the blacks led to a regency seat and later the Handship. Aegon III dismissed him on coming of age.

### desmond_daus · Дочери Десмонда Мандерли
Joffrey Velaryon’s death ended the promised marriage between him and one of these daughters.

### torrhen_m_daus · Дочери Торрхена Мандерли
Torrhen Manderly had several unnamed daughters. Jeyne Manderly, who married Rickon Stark, may have been one of them, but her precise place in the family has not been established.

## Мандерли — жёны Старков

### jeyne_m · Jeyne Manderly
Jeyne married Rickon Stark, Cregan's eldest son and heir, and had two daughters, Sansa and Serena. Her parentage within House Manderly is unknown; the suggestion that Torrhen Manderly was her father remains uncertain.

### myriame · Myriame Manderly
Myriame was Lady of Winterfell as the wife of Rodwell Stark. Their marriage was childless, and Rodwell was succeeded by his younger brother Beron.

## Белая Гавань в конце третьего века

### wyman · Wyman Manderly
His appetites and outward submission disguised a careful effort to restore the Starks. Once Wylis was safely home, Wyman sent Davos to find Rickon on Skagos. Three Frey guests vanished on the journey to Ramsay’s wedding, where Wyman served pies suggestive of revenge for the Red Wedding. The implication is strong, but their contents are not explicitly confirmed. He survived Hosteen Frey cutting his throat during a quarrel at Winterfell.

### wylis · Wylis Manderly
His release from captivity freed Wyman to act against the Boltons without endangering his surviving son.

### wynafryd · Wynafryd Manderly
Her betrothed, Rhaegar Frey, vanished after leaving White Harbor for Winterfell.

### wylla_m · Wylla Manderly
Her green braids stood out at court. She openly defended the family’s Stark allegiance when the Freys demanded Davos’s death.

### wendel · Wendel Manderly
A companion of Catelyn and a soldier for Robb, murdered at his seat during the Red Wedding.

### marlon · Marlon Manderly
Commander of White Harbor’s garrison.

### donella · Donella Hornwood
Ramsay seized her lands through a forced marriage, then starved her in a locked tower. Servants described her gnawing her fingers before death.
