### arthur_d · Arthur Dayne
The Sword of the Morning carried Dawn in Aerys’s Kingsguard. Against the Kingswood Brotherhood, he won local support by protecting the people from the outlaws, then defeated the Smiling Knight. Jaime remembered him as the finest knight he had known. Arthur remained at the tower of joy after the royal cause collapsed. Ned Stark survived their last fight only with Howland Reed’s help; precisely what happened there has not been revealed.

### ashara · Ashara Dayne
Elia’s lady-in-waiting danced with Ned at Harrenhal. Stories of her lover and stillborn child remain incomplete. She reportedly jumped from Starfall, but her body was never found.

### allyria · Allyria Dayne
Betrothed to Beric Dondarrion. His repeated deaths left him unable to remember her hair colour.

### edric_d · Edric Dayne
Beric’s young squire befriended Arya while riding with the brotherhood. He believed his wet nurse Wylla was Jon Snow’s mother.

### gerold_d · Gerold Dayne
Darkstar escaped the failed plot to crown Myrcella. Doran blamed him for mutilating her; Hotah, Obara and Balon Swann were sent after him.
