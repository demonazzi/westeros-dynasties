## Фоссовеи красного яблока

### florence · Florence Fossoway ✎
The Apple Counter handled the finances nominally entrusted to Martyn Tyrell under Jaehaerys.

### owen_f · Owen Fossoway ✎
One of the Caltrops conspiring against Hugh Hammer and Ulf White. He died at Second Tumbleton.

### steffon_fs · Steffon Fossoway ✎
At Ashford he abandoned Dunk for Aerion’s promised lordship. Raymun answered the betrayal by founding the green-apple branch.

### tanton · Tanton Fossoway ✎
A knight of Cider Hall during the War of the Five Kings.

### bryan_fs · Bryan Fossoway ✎
Lothor Brune killed him at the Blackwater.

### edwyd · Edwyd Fossoway ✎
He died fighting Lothor Brune at the Blackwater.

### franklyn · Franklyn Flowers ✎
A Golden Company sellsword claiming birth at Cider Hall, where, he said, a knight’s son raped his mother.

### leonette · Leonette Fossoway ✎
Garlan’s wife treated Sansa kindly at court. Her exact Fossoway branch is not identified.

## Фоссовеи зелёного яблока

### raymun_f · Raymun Fossoway ✎
Steffon’s defection made him seek his own knighthood so he could defend Dunk. His green apple distinguished a new branch of House Fossoway.

### jon_f · Jon Fossoway ✎
An amiable knight of the green-apple Fossoways.

### jeyne_fs · Jeyne Fossoway ✎
Jeyne belongs to the green-apple Fossoways of New Barrel and is married to Ser Gunthor Hightower.
