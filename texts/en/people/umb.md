## Род Хорфроста

### hoarfrost · Hoarfrost Umber ✎
The Lord of Last Hearth known as Hoarfrost.

### hoarfrost_son · Сын Хорфроста, отец Большого Джона ✎
Hoarfrost Umber's unnamed son was the father of the Greatjon and a brother of Mors Crowfood and Hother Whoresbane.

### mors · Mors Umber ✎
Crowfood joined Stannis with boys and old men. His scouts helped Theon and Jeyne after their escape from Winterfell.

### hother · Hother Umber ✎
He brought Umber men to the Boltons while the Freys held the Greatjon hostage.

### greatjon · Jon Umber ✎
Grey Wind took two fingers during his first quarrel with Robb. He became a fierce supporter and first hailed him King in the North. At the Red Wedding, even drunkenness did not make him easy to overpower; the Freys captured him alive.

### greatjon_bros · Братья Большого Джона Амбера ✎
They answered Robb’s summons and accompanied the Greatjon to Winterfell.

### mors_sons · Сыновья Морса Амбера ✎
Killed at the Trident during Robert’s Rebellion.

### mors_dau · Дочь Морса Амбера ✎
Wildlings carried her away in 270 AC. Her later fate is unknown.

### smalljon · Jon Umber ✎
He overturned a table to shield Robb at the Red Wedding before Bolton men beheaded him.

### greatjon_sons · Младшие сыновья Большого Джона ✎
The Greatjon had younger sons besides his eldest, the Smalljon. Their names and number are unspecified, and their individual fates after the Red Wedding are not recorded.

### greatjon_daus · Дочери Большого Джона ✎
The Greatjon has at least two daughters. Neither their names nor their mother's identity are given, and the books do not describe their circumstances after their father's capture at the Red Wedding.
