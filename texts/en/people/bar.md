### orys · Orys Baratheon ✎
One of Aegon the Conqueror's closest companions and his first Hand. He was believed to be Aegon's bastard half-brother, though the relationship was never proved. He defeated Argilac Durrandon, married his daughter Argella, and adopted the former Storm Kings' arms and words. During the war in Dorne he was captured and lost a hand.

### davos_b · Davos Baratheon ✎
Davos fought alongside Orys Baratheon in the Vulture Hunt of 37 AC. Orys died of his wounds on the journey home; Davos said his father was content after taking revenge on the Wyls for his lost hand. King Aenys rewarded Davos with gold, offices, and honors. He may have succeeded Orys at Storm's End, though this is not confirmed.

### raymont · Raymont Baratheon ✎
The World of Ice & Fire named him as the Kingsguard knight who saved Aenys I from assassins. In Fire & Blood, the king's defender in this episode remained unnamed.

### orys_son · Отец Рогара Баратеона ✎
He cannot confidently be identified with any of Orys's named sons: this generation's exact genealogy is unknown.

### rogar · Rogar Baratheon ✎
An early supporter of young Jaehaerys against Maegor. He served as Hand and Lord Protector, and married the dowager queen, Alyssa Velaryon. His attempts to control the young king's affairs caused a breach, though they later reconciled.

### borys_b · Сир Борис Баратеон ✎
Once considered Rogar's heir. Boremund's birth ended that prospect; he quarrelled with Rogar and went overseas. On returning, he joined the Vulture King and raided the stormlands. In 61 AC Jaehaerys I killed him in single combat, unwilling to force Rogar to kill his own brother.

### garon_b · Сир Гарон Баратеон ✎
He lost his wife during the Shivers epidemic.

### garon_kids · Дети сира Гарона Баратеона ✎
Ser Garon Baratheon had children, as indicated by references to Rogar's young nephews and nieces who could be sent to court. Their names, number, and later lives are unrecorded.

### ronnal_b · Сир Роннал Баратеон ✎
The Shivers killed his wife and sons. Two daughters survived the epidemic.

### ronnal_kids · Ser Ronnal Baratheon's sons ✎
Ronnal Baratheon's sons died of the Shivers during the winter of 59–60 AC, together with their father and mother. Their two sisters survived and were later fostered at the royal court. The sons' names and number are unknown.

### orryn_b · Сир Оррин Баратеон ✎
He was considered as a possible husband for Alysanne, but she secretly married Jaehaerys. He later took part in an attempt to remove Rhaella Targaryen from Oldtown and use her claim to the throne. For this, he was exiled for ten years.

### orryn_sons · Ser Orryn Baratheon's daughter ✎
Orryn Baratheon's only recorded daughter was born to the Archon of Tyrosh's daughter. His paternity was questioned. Nothing is known of the child or her mother's fate after Orryn died in the Disputed Lands.

### boremund · Boremund Baratheon ✎
He inherited Storm's End and supported the claims of his niece Rhaenys and her son Laenor. At the Great Council of 101 AC, he backed the Velaryons. His son Borros succeeded him but chose the greens during the Dance.

### jocelyn · Jocelyn Baratheon ✎
She was born after a difficult operation that cost Alyssa her life. She married Aemon, Jaehaerys I's heir. After he died, the king placed Baelon ahead of her daughter Rhaenys in the succession.

### borros · Borros Baratheon ✎
Lord of Storm's End during the Dance of the Dragons. Unlike his father, he supported Aegon II, hoping to secure an advantageous marriage for one of his daughters. Aemond and Lucerys met in his castle, but the killing took place after their departure, over Shipbreaker Bay. Late in the war, Borros marched on the capital and was killed by Kermit Tully at the Battle of the Kingsroad.

### cassandra · Cassandra Baratheon ✎
After Helaena's death, she was betrothed to Aegon II, but he died before the wedding. She later married Walter Brownhill.

### maris · Maris Baratheon ✎
According to the chronicle, her taunt prompted Aemond to pursue Lucerys. She later became a silent sister.

### ellyn · Ellyn Baratheon ✎
One of the Four Storms. She and her sisters were considered as possible brides for Aemond when their father negotiated an alliance with the greens.

### floris · Floris Baratheon ✎
She was considered as a possible bride for Aemond when the alliance with the greens was negotiated. She later married Lord Thaddeus Rowan.

### royce · Royce Baratheon ✎
Born after Borros's death, he inherited Storm's End. Elenda Caron ruled his lands during his minority.

### boremund_daus · Ronnal Baratheon's two daughters ✎
Ronnal Baratheon's two daughters survived the Shivers, which killed their parents and brothers in the winter of 59–60 AC. Rogar brought them to King's Landing with his daughter Jocelyn. Jaehaerys and Alysanne agreed to foster the girls at court.

### lord_b208 · Лорд Баратеон (около 208 г.) ✎
Lord of Storm's End. His exact relationship to other generations of Baratheons is unknown.

### bar_lord_son1 · Старший сын лорда Баратеона ✎
An unnamed elder brother of Gowen Baratheon, who is described as the third son of a reigning Lord of Storm's End. One elder brother may have been Lyonel, the castle's heir in 209 AC, but that relationship is not confirmed.

### bar_lord_son2 · Второй сын лорда Баратеона ✎
Gowen Baratheon's other unnamed elder brother. His existence follows from Gowen being the third son of the reigning Lord of Storm's End; no personal history is recorded.

### gowen · Gowen Baratheon ✎
His only known son died in infancy.

### gowen_son · Сын Гоуэна Баратеона ✎
He died in infancy. His name is unknown.

### lyonel · Lyonel Baratheon, the Laughing Storm ✎
Lord of Storm's End, known as the Laughing Storm, who competed at the Ashford tourney. He supported Duncan the Tall in the trial of seven. When Prince Duncan rejected his daughter, he rebelled and proclaimed himself Storm King. Peace was restored after his duel with Duncan the Tall and an agreement for Ormund to marry Rhaelle Targaryen.

### lyonel_d · Дочь Лайонела Баратеона ✎
Duncan Targaryen broke their betrothal to marry Jenny of Oldstones. Lyonel regarded this as an insult and rebelled.

### ormund · Ormund Baratheon ✎
His marriage to Rhaelle Targaryen reconciled the Baratheons with the crown after his father's rebellion. Under Jaehaerys II he served as Hand and led the royal forces in the War of the Ninepenny Kings. Maelys Blackfyre killed him in the Stepstones; his son Steffon inherited Storm's End.

### steffon · Steffon Baratheon ✎
The king sent him to Essos to find a bride for Rhaegar, but his ship sank in a storm off his own coast on the return voyage. His wife, Cassana Estermont, drowned with him. Robert and Stannis watched the shipwreck from the castle.

### robert · Robert Baratheon ✎
The Lord of Storm's End led the rebellion against Aerys II alongside Eddard Stark and Jon Arryn. He defeated Rhaegar on the Trident and became king, strengthening his claim through his Targaryen grandmother Rhaelle. More successful as a warrior than a ruler, he left affairs of state to his councillors. He died after a boar attacked him while hunting, opening the struggle for his throne.

### stannis · Stannis Baratheon ✎
He held Storm's End through its siege during Robert's Rebellion. His brother granted him Dragonstone, and he served as master of ships. After Robert died, Stannis declared that Cersei's children were Jaime's and claimed the crown as the lawful heir. Defeat on the Blackwater did not end his campaign. In the books, he aided the Night's Watch and continues fighting the Boltons in the North.

### renly · Renly Baratheon ✎
Lord of Storm's End and Robert's master of laws. He was close to Loras Tyrell and married Loras's sister Margaery. After Robert's death, he proclaimed himself king with support from the stormlands and Reach, despite Stannis's senior claim. A supernatural shadow linked to Stannis and Melisandre killed him.

### joffrey_b · Joffrey Baratheon ✎
Cersei and Jaime's eldest son, officially regarded as Robert Baratheon's heir. After Robert died, he took the throne and ordered Eddard Stark's execution, wrecking attempts at a peaceful settlement. His cruelty made matters difficult even for his own supporters. He was poisoned at his wedding feast with Margaery Tyrell; Tyrion was accused of the murder.

### myrcella · Мирцелла Баратеон ✎
Under Tyrion's agreement, she was sent to Dorne and betrothed to Trystane Martell. Arianne tried to crown her queen under Dornish succession law, which placed an elder daughter before a younger son. The plot failed, and Myrcella suffered a serious wound and lost an ear. She remains alive in the books.

### tommen · Томмен I Баратеон ✎
He inherited the Iron Throne after Joffrey's death and married Margaery. Too young to rule independently, he became the focus of a struggle for influence between Cersei, the Tyrells and the royal council. In the published novels, he remains king and is still alive.

### mya_stone · Mya Stone ✎
She worked as a guide, leading mules along the mountain road to the Eyrie. She fell in love with Mychel Redfort, but he married a noblewoman. Robert visited her when she was a child, though Mya did not know that her father had become king.

### gendry · Gendry ✎
He grew up as an armourer's apprentice, unaware that Robert was his father. After the king died, Yoren took him out of the capital with recruits for the Night's Watch. He befriended Arya, survived captivity at Harrenhal and joined the Brotherhood Without Banners. Beric knighted him. He later worked as a smith at the inn at the crossroads, where orphaned children had found shelter.

### edric · Edric Storm ✎
Son of Robert and Delena Florent, and the only bastard he openly acknowledged because of his mother's noble birth. He was raised at Storm's End under Cortnay Penrose's protection. After Stannis took the castle, he was brought to Dragonstone, where Melisandre's plans threatened his life. Davos helped him escape across the narrow sea.

### bella · Bella ✎
A girl from Stoney Sept who claimed to be Robert's bastard daughter. Her mother placed her conception during Robert's stay in the town in the rebellion. She met Arya and her companions at the Peach. Bella's account of her parentage was not independently confirmed.

### barra · Barra ✎
Eddard saw her while investigating the parentage of Cersei's children. After Robert's death, she and her mother were killed on the queen's orders.

### robert_twins · Близнецы Роберта Баратеона ✎
They were born to a serving woman at Casterly Rock after the tourney of 295 AC. According to Baelish, Cersei ordered both killed; the events were not described directly.

### robert_others · Ещё девять бастардов Роберта Баратеона ✎
Maggy's prophecy spoke of sixteen children for Robert, but Varys knew of only eight bastards. This group represents his presumed unnamed children.

### shireen · Ширен Баратеон ✎
Childhood greyscale left marks on her face, but the disease was halted. She loved books and befriended Patchface, living under the protection of her father and his supporters. In the published novels she is at Castle Black and remains alive; her death at the stake belongs to the television series.
