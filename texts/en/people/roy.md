## Ройсы — жёны других домов

### lorra · Lorra Royce ✎
Lorra was Lady of Winterfell as Beron Stark's wife. They had seven children, five sons and two daughters. Their eldest sons, Donnor and Willam, subsequently became Lords of Winterfell in turn.

### perra · Perra Royce ✎
Perra was Walder Frey's first wife and the mother of Stevron, Emmon, Aenys and Perriane. Her sons founded the elder branches of his extensive family. Her precise position within House Royce is unknown.

### benedict · Benedict Royce ✎
Benedict was a knight from a junior Royce branch and Raymar's younger son. He married Jocelyn Stark. Their three daughters married a Waynwood, a Corbray and possibly a Templeton. His connection to the Royces of the Gates of the Moon has not been confirmed.

### benedict_daus · Дочери Бенедикта Ройса и Джослин Старк ✎
Their marriages carried Stark ancestry into several Vale houses, connections Catelyn raised when discussing Robb’s succession.

### jeyne_r · Jeyne Royce ✎
She died in childbirth; her daughter was stillborn.

### ryella · Ryella Royce ✎
Ryella is Ser Arwood Frey's wife and the mother of Ryella, Androw, Alyn and Hostella. Her relationship to Yohn Royce and other known members of the house has not been established.

## Рунный Камень и Лунные Врата в конце третьего века

### kyle_r · Kyle Royce ✎
Aerys executed him and his summoned father after Kyle accompanied Brandon Stark to court.

### yohn · Yohn Royce ✎
Bronze Yohn favoured helping the Starks. After Lysa’s death he led opposition to Littlefinger, who divided the lords against him.

### andar · Andar Royce ✎
Heir to Runestone.

### robar · Robar Royce ✎
He believed Catelyn’s account of Renly’s murder and allowed her escape. Loras killed him in the aftermath.

### waymar · Waymar Royce ✎
His first ranging met the Others. Killed fighting them, he rose as a wight and strangled Will.

### ysilla · Ysilla Royce ✎
Ysilla, Yohn Royce's daughter, married Mychel Redfort at his father Horton's insistence. Mychel had been in love with Mya Stone but submitted to his family's marriage plans.

### yohn_daus · Другие дочери Джона Ройса ✎
Yohn Royce has several daughters. Ysilla, who married Mychel Redfort, is the only one named. Her sisters' names and marriages are unrecorded.

### nestor · Nestor Royce ✎
Littlefinger rewarded his support with hereditary possession of the Gates of the Moon.

### albar · Albar Royce ✎
Heir to the Gates of the Moon.

### myranda · Myranda Royce ✎
The young widow managed the Gates’ household and befriended Alayne. Her questions nearly exposed Sansa’s identity.
