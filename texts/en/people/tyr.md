### olenna · Olenna Redwyne ✎
The Queen of Thorns combined sharp speech with practical concern for her family’s position. She distrusted Mace’s judgment and tried to secure Sansa for Willas. Littlefinger’s account implicates her in Joffrey’s murder: the poison was hidden in Sansa’s hair net, which Olenna adjusted during the wedding. Her outward manner gave little sign of the danger.

### mace · Mace Tyrell ✎
His shift from Renly to the Lannisters made the Tyrell army crucial to the Blackwater victory and secured Margaery’s royal marriages. As Tommen’s Hand, he defended that gain against the Faith’s arrest of his daughter. He claimed military credit freely, including Randyll Tarly’s victory at Ashford, while his own strength lay chiefly in the troops, wealth and alliances at his command.

### mina · Mina Tyrell ✎

## Дети Мейса и Алерии Хайтауэр (тексты — авторские, раньше стояли в полосе Хайтауэров)

### willas · Willas Tyrell ✎
A tourney injury ended his riding career, but not his friendship with Oberyn, whose involvement he did not resent. Highgarden’s heir became known for learning, judgment and breeding animals. The planned marriage to Sansa offered a useful alliance until the Lannisters intercepted it with Tyrion’s marriage.

### garlan · Garlan Tyrell ✎
The Gallant’s courtesy accompanied considerable fighting skill. Wearing Renly’s armour at the Blackwater helped create the appearance of the dead king’s return. The crown rewarded him with the Florent lands. Ironborn attacks later turned his attention towards defending the Reach.

### loras · Loras Tyrell ✎
The Knight of Flowers excelled in tourneys and loved Renly. Grief led him to kill guards he blamed for Renly’s murder before entering the royal Kingsguard. Reports later described terrible wounds during Dragonstone’s capture. His condition at the end of the published novels is not independently shown.

### margaery · Margaery Tyrell ✎
Three royal marriages placed her family at the centre of court politics. She cultivated popular goodwill and kept relatives and companions around her, provoking Cersei’s fear of displacement. The rivalry ended in accusations before the Faith. In the books she remains alive, awaiting the outcome of proceedings rather than the fate shown in the television adaptation.
