## Пайперы при Мейгоре и в Танец драконов

### pip_lord1 · Лорд Пайпер, отец Джона и Мелони
Lord of Pinkmaiden, otherwise unnamed in the record.

### jon_p · Jon Piper
Lord of Pinkmaiden during Maegor’s reign.

### jon_p_bros · Младшие братья Джона Пайпера
Jon Piper's younger brothers joined their sister Melony in supporting Aegon the Uncrowned. Melony and three of her brothers died at the Battle Beneath the Gods Eye in 43 AC. The identities of all the dead cannot be established confidently, since the accounts of Jon's own fate differ.

### melony · Melony Piper
Rhaena’s close companion backed Aegon the Uncrowned and died beneath the Gods Eye.

### petyr_p · Petyr Piper
Mushroom claimed that seeing his grandson’s severed head caused his death at Acorn Hall.

### petyr_child · Ребёнок Петира Пайпера
Lord Petyr Piper, who died at Acorn Hall during the Dance of the Dragons, had at least one child. The child's name and sex are unknown.

### petyr_gs · Внуки Петира Пайпера
Killed at Acorn Hall, where his head was displayed on a spear.

### stanton · Stanton Piper
Petyr’s successor died in Vermithor’s fire at Second Tumbleton.

## Розовая Дева в конце третьего века

### pip_lord18 · Лорд Пайпер, отец леди Пайпер
Lord of Pinkmaiden; his personal history is not recorded.

### lady_piper · Леди Пайпер, жена Квеллона Грейджоя
She died with her stillborn daughter after Balon insisted on a midwife rather than a maester.

### clement · Clement Piper
Lannister raids devastated his lands at the beginning of the war.

### marq · Marq Piper
He raided Lannister forces with Karyl Vance. The Red Wedding left him captive, later accused of resisting the Freys.

### lewys · Lewys Piper
A young squire of House Piper.

### pip_knights · Пятеро рыцарей Пайперов
Five knights who were nephews or cousins of Clement Piper accompanied Marq Piper to the Twins. Marq was captured during the Red Wedding. The individual fates of his companions are not specified, so the five cannot all be counted among the dead.
