## Первые годы после Завоевания

### manfred_h · Manfred Hightower
Lord of Oldtown during the Conquest. He chose to acknowledge Aegon, preserving his city and his house's influence. His daughter Ceryse became Maegor Targaryen's first wife.

### martyn_h · Martyn Hightower
He became Lord of Oldtown in 41 AC, during the Targaryens' struggle against the Faith Militant.

### ceryse · Ceryse Hightower
Maegor's first wife. They married in Oldtown's Starry Sept; Ceryse was about ten years older than her husband. They had no children. Maegor's later marriages violated the Faith's teachings and humiliated his first queen. She died in 45 AC; rumours of a violent death were never confirmed.

### morgan_h · Morgan Hightower
A knight of the Warrior's Sons, the Faith's military order that rebelled against the Targaryens.

## Отто, Алисента и их родня: Танец драконов

### hgt_lord8 · Лорд Хайтауэр, отец Отто
Lord of Oldtown.

### hgt_lord9 · Лорд Хайтауэр, брат Отто
Lord of Oldtown. He supported Otto's plans to place Aegon II on the throne.

### otto · Otto Hightower
A younger Hightower son who served as Hand to Jaehaerys I, Viserys I and Aegon II. Court influence and his daughter Alicent's marriage helped him strengthen his family's position. After Viserys died, he helped arrange Aegon's accession and sought allies for the greens. The new king dismissed him; Rhaenyra had him executed after taking the capital.

### alicent · Alicent Hightower
Viserys I's second queen. She defended her children's claims and became central to the greens' rivalry with Rhaenyra. After her husband's death, she supported Aegon II's coronation. She survived the war and all her children but spent her remaining years confined, dying of the winter fever in 133 AC.

### otto_sons · Старшие сыновья Отто Хайтауэра
Otto Hightower's elder sons, brothers of Alicent and Gwayne. Otto brought his wife and children to court when he became Jaehaerys's Hand. Only his youngest son, Gwayne, is named; the number and identities of the others are unknown.

### gwayne · Gwayne Hightower
He served in the City Watch of King's Landing, strengthening the greens' hold on the capital. When the gold cloaks switched to Rhaenyra, Luthor Largent killed him.

### ormund_h · Ormund Hightower
Lord of Oldtown during the Dance of the Dragons. He led the Hightower army in support of Aegon II. Daeron and Tessarion provided crucial help to his forces. Roderick Dustin killed him at the First Battle of Tumbleton; his son Lyonel then ruled Oldtown.

### lyonel_h · Lyonel Hightower
He inherited Oldtown after Ormund's death. He initially wanted to continue the war but agreed to peace. He became involved with his father's widow, Samantha Tarly. The High Septon long refused to let them marry, though their union was later recognized. Together they increased the Hightowers' wealth, and after the Rogare bank collapsed, their house founded the Bank of Oldtown.

### martyn_ho · Martyn Hightower
He served as a squire during Aegon III's reign.

### garmund · Garmund Hightower
During the Dance he served as a page and cupbearer at Highgarden. He later married Rhaena Targaryen; they had six daughters.

### bethany_h · Bethany Hightower
After Jaehaera died, she was proposed as a new wife for Aegon III. The king chose Daenaera Velaryon, and the marriage never took place.

### lyonel_kids · Дети Лионеля Хайтауэра и Саманты Тарли
They were born before Lyonel's formal marriage to Samantha. The new High Septon permitted the union, after which their six children were legitimized.

### garmund_daus · Шесть дочерей Гармунда Хайтауэра и Рейены
Garmund Hightower and Rhaena Targaryen had six daughters. Rhaena married Garmund after being widowed by Corwyn Corbray's death. The daughters' names and individual histories are unrecorded.

## Лейтон Хайтауэр и его дети: время книг

### leyton · Leyton Hightower
Lord of Oldtown and head of one of the Reach's wealthiest houses, bearing the title Voice of Oldtown. He had not left the Hightower for years, remaining there with his eldest daughter Malora. Stories about their studies of magic circulated as rumours; other family members organized the defence of his lands.

### baelor_h · Baelor Hightower
Brightsmile, the heir to Oldtown. In his youth, he was considered as a possible husband for Elia Martell, but the marriage did not take place.

### malora · Malora Hightower
Known as the Mad Maid. She lived with her father at the top of the Hightower, away from ordinary court life. In Oldtown, people said they were studying sorcery and seeking a way to protect the city. These stories did not provide a reliable account of her activities or intentions.

### alerie · Alerie Hightower
She came to King's Landing with the Tyrells and joined Margaery's household.

### garth_h · Garth Hightower
A knight known as Greysteel.

### denyse_h · Denyse Hightower
Denyse married Ser Desmond Redwyne and has a son, Denys. She is a daughter of Leyton Hightower.

### leyla_h · Leyla Hightower
Leyla is a daughter of Lord Leyton Hightower of Oldtown and the wife of Ser Jon Cupps.

### alysanne_h · Alysanne Hightower
Alysanne, a daughter of Leyton Hightower, married Lord Arthur Ambrose. Their son is Ser Alyn Ambrose.

### lynesse · Lynesse Hightower
Accustomed to Oldtown's wealth, she was unhappy on Bear Island. Jorah ruined himself trying to provide the life she wanted and began selling poachers into slavery. In exile in Lys, she left him for the wealthy merchant Tregar Ormollen.

### gunthor · Gunthor Hightower
A knight of Oldtown.

### humfrey_h · Humfrey Hightower
A knight of Oldtown.
