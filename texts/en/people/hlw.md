### har_lord · Лорд Харлоу, отец Родрика ✎

### gwynesse · Gwynesse Harlaw ✎
Widowed and back on Harlaw, she maintained that her seniority entitled her to Ten Towers.

### rodrik_h · Rodrik Harlaw ✎

### rodrik_sons · Сыновья Родрика Харлоу ✎

### harras · Harras Harlaw ✎

### hotho · Hotho Harlaw ✎
His gift of a rare book failed to secure Rodrik for his daughter. He next approached Victarion, then supported Euron.

### hotho_dau · Дочь Хото Харлоу ✎
Hotho's unnamed daughter is twelve and fair-haired. Her father first tried to marry her to Rodrik the Reader, who refused. Before the kingsmoot he offered her to Victarion as a queen. Victarion promised to consider her after winning the crown, but Euron won instead.

### boremund_h · Boremund Harlaw ✎
The master of Harridan Hill supported Asha at the kingsmoot.

### boremund_sons · Сыновья Боремунда Харлоу ✎
