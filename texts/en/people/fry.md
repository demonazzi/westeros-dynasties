## Танец драконов

### forrest_fr · Forrest Frey ✎
Mocked as Fool Frey after asking for Rhaenyra's hand, Forrest nevertheless fought for her during the Dance. His men helped Daemon take Stone Hedge. He died fighting the westermen at the Battle by the Lakeshore, leaving Sabitha to continue the family's war.

### sabitha_fr · Sabitha Frey ✎
Sabitha took command after Forrest's death and remained an active supporter of the blacks. She occupied Harrenhal, escaping Aemond's return by hiding in a privy, and fought alongside Alysanne Blackwood. After the war she held Widow Fairs at the Twins, finding wives for northmen who chose to settle in the riverlands.

## Лорды Переправы до Уолдера

### fr_lord14 · House Frey ✎
An unnamed ancestor shown as the father of Lord Frey and Ser Franklyn. His grandson Walder was still a small child at the Whitewalls tourney in 212 AC. No name or individual history is recorded for this earlier generation.

### fr_lord · Lord Frey ✎
Attended his daughter's wedding tourney at Whitewalls, then withdrew as Daemon II Blackfyre's conspiracy unravelled. Bloodraven let him leave, warning that another act of disloyalty would bring consequences.

### franklyn_fr · Franklyn Frey ✎
At Whitewalls he unhorsed Harbert Paege after five passes, but lost his next contest to John the Fiddler—the disguised Daemon II Blackfyre.

### addam_fr · Addam Frey ✎
A promising young jouster, he was unhorsed by Uthor Underleaf at Whitewalls. His cousin, the bride at the wedding tourney, fainted when he fell.

### lady_butterwell · Lady Butterwell ✎
Her wedding to Ambrose Butterwell at fifteen provided cover for Daemon II's conspiracy. Its failure cost the household Whitewalls and most of its fortune; Bloodraven allowed Ambrose to retain a tenth of his wealth.

### walder_fr · Walder Frey ✎
Walder made the Twins' bridge a bargaining tool, withholding his support until he could name his price. He arrived too late to fight at the Trident, then demanded a marriage pact before letting Robb cross. When Robb married elsewhere, Walder joined the Lannisters and Boltons in planning the Red Wedding. The massacre brought the family lands and titles, but also the brotherhood's revenge. Meanwhile, his many descendants competed for favour and the succession.

## Дети лорда Уолдера и Перры Ройс с потомками

### stevron_fr · Stevron Frey ✎
Courteous and cautious, Stevron spent decades waiting to inherit the Twins. He welcomed Robb's army and led Frey men in the western campaign. A wound at Oxcross seemed minor, yet he was found dead in his tent three days later. His death moved the succession to Ryman's branch.

### ryman_fr · Ryman Frey ✎
Ryman became heir after Stevron's death and killed Dacey Mormont at the Red Wedding. He later commanded the siege of Riverrun, where his drinking and threats accomplished little. Jaime dismissed him in favour of Edwyn. The brotherhood captured and hanged Ryman on his journey home.

### edwyn_fr · Edwyn Frey ✎
His hidden mail helped Catelyn realise that the wedding feast was a trap. Jaime later put him in command outside Riverrun after dismissing Ryman. Cold and deeply suspicious of Black Walder, Edwyn viewed his brother as a rival for the Twins and suspected him of involvement in their father's death.

### walda_fr_edwyn · Walda Frey ✎
Her place in the succession was complicated by the family's preference for male heirs. Rumours of her mother's affair with Black Walder also raised questions about her parentage, but the books do not confirm them.

### walder_fr_ryman · Walder Frey ✎
Black Walder fought for Robb before taking part in the Red Wedding. He forced Jason Mallister to surrender Seagard by threatening Patrek's life. Resolute and quick to anger, he frightened even his relatives and refused to marry. Reports of affairs within the family remained unconfirmed, as did claims that he fathered several younger Freys.

### petyr_fr · Petyr Frey ✎
Known as Petyr Pimple, he had been married since the age of ten. At the Red Wedding he passed out while trying to match the Greatjon's drinking. The brotherhood later captured him. By the time Merrett arrived with his ransom, Petyr was already hanging at Oldstones.

### perra_fr · Perra Frey ✎
Petyr's death left her fatherless. Rumours at the Twins suggested that Black Walder might be her biological father, but no confirmation followed.

### aegon_fr_stevron · Aegon Frey ✎
Jinglebell was the Twins' fool. During the Red Wedding, Catelyn held him hostage in a desperate attempt to save Robb, then cut his throat after her son was killed. The Freys later claimed that Robb had turned into a wolf and killed him.

### maegelle_fr · Maegelle Frey ✎
Died in childbirth.

### walton_fr · Walton Frey ✎
A knight whose life is recorded mainly through the family genealogy; no separate deeds are described.

### steffon_fr · Steffon Frey ✎
Known within the family as Steffon the Sweet.

### walda_fr_walton · Walda Frey ✎
Fair Walda was considered one of the family's prettiest young women. Relatives expected Roose Bolton to choose her, but he preferred Fat Walda's larger dowry. She sat beside Robb at the Red Wedding. Rumours linking her to Black Walder were not confirmed.

### bryan_fr · Bryan Frey ✎
Served as a squire.

### emmon_fr · Emmon Frey ✎
Long resident at Casterly Rock, Emmon received Riverrun as a reward for the Red Wedding. Jaime secured the castle's surrender without an assault and corrected Emmon's belief that the grant made him ruler of all the riverlands: that authority belonged to Baelish. Genna was the more confident partner in both family affairs and politics.

### cleos_fr · Cleos Frey ✎
Captured while fighting for the Lannisters in the Whispering Wood, Cleos carried Robb's peace terms to King's Landing. He later accompanied Jaime and Brienne after Jaime's release from Riverrun. Outlaws killed him with arrows near Maidenpool before the party could reach the capital.

### tywin_fr · Tywin Frey ✎
Called Ty, he stood to inherit Emmon's newly granted Riverrun. A proposed marriage to Edmure and Roslin's child, should she have a daughter, would link the new ruling family with the dispossessed Tullys.

### willem_fr · Willem Frey ✎
Served as a page at Ashemark.

### lyonel_fr · Lyonel Frey ✎
A knight of the Riverrun branch. The books provide little about his activities beyond the family records.

### tion_fr · Tion Frey ✎
Captured in the Whispering Wood, he was held at Riverrun. Rickard Karstark murdered him and Willem Lannister after Jaime's release, seeking revenge for his own sons despite the boys' lack of responsibility.

### walder_fr_emmon · Walder Frey ✎
Known as Red Walder, he was fostered at Casterly Rock. Sources describe him as either a page or a squire.

### aenys_fr · Aenys Frey ✎
An experienced commander in Roose Bolton's army, Aenys helped take Harrenhal and participated in the Red Wedding. He later brought Frey reinforcements north. In a published Winds of Winter sample chapter, his force rode into Mors Umber's pit traps outside Winterfell; Aenys fell from his horse and broke his neck.

### aegon_fr_aenys · Aegon Frey ✎
Called Bloodborn, he became an outlaw. His crimes and later fate are not described.

### rhaegar_fr · Rhaegar Frey ✎
He brought Wendel Manderly's bones to White Harbor while mocking Robb's memory and pressing the Freys' alliance. Wyman concealed his hostility until his surviving son was safe. Rhaegar then disappeared with Jared and Symond after leaving the city. Wyman's three wedding pies suggested what might have happened to them, but the narrative did not directly confirm it.

### robert_fr_rhaegar · Robert Frey ✎
Born in 286 AC, Robert is Rhaegar Frey's eldest son by Jeyne Beesbury. One of Lord Walder's great-grandchildren, he is the older brother of White Walda and Jonos.

### walda_fr_rhaegar · Walda Frey ✎
Known as White Walda. Her mother died of a wasting illness; her father disappeared after his visit to White Harbor.

### jonos_fr · Jonos Frey ✎
Jonos, born in 291 AC, is Rhaegar Frey's younger son by Jeyne Beesbury. Robert and White Walda are his older siblings.

### perriane_fr · Perriane Frey ✎
Perriane is Walder Frey's eldest daughter by Perra Royce. She married Ser Leslyn Haigh and has three sons: Harys, Donnel, and Alyn.

At the Red Wedding he was assigned to make the Greatjon too drunk to resist. Hoping to improve his standing in the family, he later took Petyr's ransom to Oldstones. ### amerei_fr · Amerei Frey ✎

### walda_fr_merrett · Walda Frey ✎

### walder_fr_merrett · Walder Frey ✎

He was found murdered in the snow. No killer was identified; the discovery triggered a clash between the Freys and Manderlys.

## Дети лорда Уолдера и Алиссы Блэквуд с потомками

### lothar_fr · Lothar Frey ✎
Lame from birth, Lothar managed the Twins and handled delicate negotiations. He brought the demand that Edmure marry Roslin, then helped Roose organise the Red Wedding. He chose The Rains of Castamere as the signal, hid crossbowmen among the musicians and assigned the other Freys their roles in the massacre.

### tysane_fr · Tysane Frey ✎
Tysane, born in 292 AC, is the eldest daughter of Lame Lothar Frey and Leonella Lefford. Her younger sisters are Walda, Emberlei, and Leana.

### walda_fr_lothar · Walda Frey ✎
Born in 294 or 295 AC, Walda is Lothar Frey's second daughter by Leonella Lefford. She is distinct from both Roose Bolton's wife, Fat Walda, and Edwyn Frey's daughter of the same name.

### emberlei_fr · Emberlei Frey ✎
Emberlei is Lothar Frey's third daughter by Leonella Lefford, born in 296 or 297 AC. She is younger than Tysane and Walda and older than Leana.

### leana_fr · Leana Frey ✎
An infant during the events of A Dance with Dragons.

### jammos_fr · Jammos Frey ✎
A knight whose individual activities are scarcely described.

### walder_fr_jammos · Walder Frey ✎
Big Walder was fifty-two days older than Little Walder, despite being the smaller boy. Both were wards at Winterfell before becoming Ramsay's squires. Big Walder was more cautious and seldom joined his cousin's cruelties. After Little Walder's murder, he said the boy had gone to collect a debt from a White Harbor man. His own role, if any, remained unknown.

### dickon_fr · Dickon Frey ✎
Dickon and his twin Mathis were born in 294 AC to Jammos Frey and Sallei Paege. Big Walder, fostered at Winterfell, is their elder brother.

### mathis_fr · Mathis Frey ✎
Mathis is Dickon Frey's twin, born in 294 AC to Jammos Frey and Sallei Paege. Their older brother is Big Walder.

### whalen_fr · Whalen Frey ✎

### morya_fr · Morya Frey ✎

### perwyn_fr · Perwyn Frey ✎
Escorted Catelyn on her mission to Renly and Storm's End. He left Riverrun with the other Freys after Robb married, but was absent from the Red Wedding. Daven Lannister later spoke of him as a decent man.

### benfrey_fr · Benfrey Frey ✎
Seized Dacey Mormont during the Red Wedding, but she smashed a flagon into his face and broke free. He died soon afterwards from the wound.

### della_fr · Della Frey ✎
Known as Deaf Della.

### osmund_fr · Osmund Frey ✎
Osmund, born in 297 AC, is the younger child of Benfrey Frey and his wife and cousin Jyanna. His elder sister is Della.

### willamen_fr · Willamen Frey ✎
Completed his studies at the Citadel, took his vows and served the Hunters as maester at Longbow Hall.

### olyvar_fr · Olyvar Frey ✎
A loyal squire who fought in Robb's battle guard in the Whispering Wood. He wanted to remain with the king after the broken marriage pact, but his family made him return. He was away from the Twins when the Red Wedding took place.

### roslin_fr · Roslin Frey ✎
Her marriage to Edmure was presented as reconciliation, while the feast concealed a massacre. Edmure later said she knew the plan and had been forced to comply, explaining her tears. Pregnant afterwards, she prayed for a daughter: a son could give the Freys a Tully heir and remove their reason to keep Edmure alive.
