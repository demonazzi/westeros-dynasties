## Русе Болтон и его сыновья

### roose · Roose Bolton
Soft-spoken and fond of leeching, Roose commanded Robb Stark's infantry while carefully preserving his own men. He conspired with the Freys and Lannisters, then personally killed Robb at the Red Wedding. Rewarded with the wardenship of the North, he relied on marriages, hostages and divided enemies to hold it. He suspected Ramsay of poisoning Domeric and distrusted his cruelty, yet made him his heir. At Winterfell, his coalition waited for Stannis amid murders and growing quarrels.

### roose_sons · Сыновья Русе Болтона, умершие в колыбели
Roose's unnamed sons died in the cradle and were buried beneath the Dreadfort. Their names were not recorded.

### domeric · Domeric Bolton
Domeric trained first as Barbrey Dustin's page, then as Horton Redfort's squire in the Vale. He played the harp, read histories and showed considerable promise as a rider. Wanting a brother of his own, he sought out Ramsay against Roose's wishes. He died soon afterwards in 297 AC. The maester diagnosed a bowel sickness; Roose believed Ramsay had poisoned him.

### ramsay · Ramsay Bolton
Ramsay forced Donella Hornwood into marriage and starved her to death to seize her lands. He destroyed the northern force outside Winterfell, burned the castle and tortured Theon into submission as Reek. Royal legitimization gave him the Bolton name. He married Jeyne Poole, presented as Arya Stark, to support his claim to Winterfell. Her escape with Theon endangered that claim. A letter purportedly from Ramsay later demanded their return and threatened Jon Snow; its account of Stannis's defeat remained unconfirmed.
