### dragonseeds · Dragonseeds
People believed to have Valyrian ancestry answered Jacaerys’s call for new dragonriders during the Dance. Many died attempting the task. Hugh, Ulf, Nettles and Addam successfully claimed dragons; Alyn survived his own attempt but did not become a rider. Their success did not establish the parentage of the group as a whole.

### hugh_hammer · Hugh Hammer
Claiming Vermithor made a smith’s bastard a military prize. At Tumbleton he betrayed Rhaenyra, then sought a crown for himself. Jon Roxton killed him before the second battle.

### ulf_white · Ulf the White
Silverwing’s rider betrayed Rhaenyra with Hugh and demanded rewards beyond Bitterbridge. Hobert Hightower ended his ambitions with poisoned wine, drinking it himself to make Ulf trust the offer.

### nettles · Nettles
She won Sheepstealer by bringing him sheep rather than asserting noble ancestry. After fighting in the Gullet, she hunted Aemond with Daemon. Rumours about their intimacy helped turn Rhaenyra against her, but Daemon allowed her to leave instead of obeying the execution order. She and Sheepstealer were later reported in the Mountains of the Moon; their ultimate fate is unknown.
