## Криган Старк и его дети

### cregan · Cregan Stark ✎
The Pact of Ice and Fire committed him to Rhaenyra’s cause. He reached King’s Landing after both rival monarchs were dead, then investigated Aegon II’s poisoning despite having opposed him. During the Hour of the Wolf he judged the conspirators, briefly served as Hand to Aegon III and returned north once the settlement was made.

### rickon_c · Rickon Stark ✎
The promised marriage to Jacaerys’s future daughter never materialised. He later died in Daeron’s Dornish wars, before inheriting Winterfell.

### jonnel · Jonnel Stark ✎
One-Eye inherited Winterfell and married Sansa to unite family branches. Neither marriage produced children.

### edric_c · Edric Stark ✎
Cregan Stark's third son, by Lynara Stark, married his niece Serena, Rickon's daughter. They had the twins Torrhen and Cregard and two daughters, Arrana and Argelle. After Jonnel, Winterfell passed to Edric's younger brother Barthogan; why Edric's branch did not inherit is unknown.

### lyanna_c · Lyanna Stark ✎
Lyanna was Cregan Stark's only daughter by his third wife, Lynara. Her brothers were Jonnel, Edric, Barthogan and Brandon.

### barthogan · Barthogan Stark ✎
Black Sword died suppressing rebellion on Skagos, leaving no children.

### cregan_daughters · Сарра, Алис, Рейя и Мирия Старк ✎
Cregan Stark and Alysanne Blackwood had four daughters after the Dance of the Dragons: Sarra, Alys, Raya and Mariah. None became Lady of Winterfell in her own right. After Cregan's eldest son died, the succession continued through his sons by Lynara Stark.

### sansa_c · Санса Старк, дочь Рикона ✎
Her marriage to Jonnel united two succession lines but remained childless.

### serena_c · Serena Stark ✎
Widowed after marriage to an Umber lord, she married Edric Stark and had four children.

### torrhen_e · Torrhen Stark ✎
Torrhen was Edric and Serena Stark's son and the elder twin of Cregard. He and his siblings, including Arrana and Argelle, belonged to the branch passed over in Winterfell's succession after Jonnel Stark.

### cregard · Cregard Stark ✎
Cregard was the younger twin son of Edric and Serena Stark, after Torrhen. He also had two sisters, Arrana and Argelle. No marriage or children are recorded for him.

### arrana · Arrana Stark ✎
Edric and Serena Stark's elder daughter married Lord Osric Umber of Last Hearth. They had children, whose names are unknown.

### argelle_s · Argelle Stark ✎
Argelle, Edric and Serena Stark's younger daughter, married Lord Robard Cerwyn and had children whose names are unrecorded. Her marriage connected this Stark branch to Winterfell's neighbours at Castle Cerwyn.

## Младшая ветвь: от Брандона, сына Кригана, до Эддарда

### brandon_c · Brandon Stark ✎
He succeeded after Barthogan’s death on Skagos.

### rodwell · Rodwell Stark ✎
His childless death passed Winterfell to Beron.

### beron · Beron Stark ✎
War with Dagon’s ironborn left him mortally wounded.

### arsa · Arsa Stark ✎
Arsa was Brandon Stark and Alys Karstark's only daughter. Her elder brothers, Rodwell and Beron, ruled Winterfell in succession. She also had a half-brother, Brandon's natural son Lonnel Snow.

### lonnel_snow · Lonnel Snow ✎
Lonnel, also called Lonny, was Brandon Stark's natural son by Wylla Fenn. He was older than Brandon's legitimate children with Alys Karstark: Rodwell, Beron and Arsa.

### donnor · Donnor Stark ✎
His childless death made Willam Lord of Winterfell.

### willam_s · Willam Stark ✎
Killed at the Long Lake fighting Raymun Redbeard’s wildlings.

### artos · Artos the Implacable ✎
He continued the Long Lake battle and killed Raymun Redbeard.

### brandon_a · Brandon Stark ✎
Brandon was the elder twin son of Artos Stark and Lysara Karstark, before Benjen. He had children, but neither their names nor their mother's identity is recorded.

### benjen_a · Benjen Stark ✎
Benjen was the younger twin son of Artos Stark and Lysara Karstark. Like his brother Brandon, he had children, but the descendants of this branch are unnamed.

### berena · Berena Stark ✎
Berena was Beron Stark and Lorra Royce's elder daughter and fourth child. Her brothers Donnor and Willam became Lords of Winterfell, while her youngest brother Rodrik was known as the Wandering Wolf.

### alysanne_s · Alysanne Stark ✎
Alysanne was the younger daughter of Beron Stark and Lorra Royce, with a sister, Berena, and five brothers. Donnor and Willam both became Lords of Winterfell. No marriage or children are recorded for Alysanne.

### errold · Errold Stark ✎
Errold was Beron Stark and Lorra Royce's fourth son. His elder brothers Donnor and Willam ruled Winterfell, and his younger brother Rodrik served with the Second Sons.

### rodrik_b · Rodrik Stark ✎
Beron Stark and Lorra Royce's youngest son was known as the Wandering Wolf. He served with the Second Sons and married Arya Flint of the northern mountain clans. They had two daughters, Branda and Lyarra; through Lyarra, he was Eddard Stark's grandfather.

### edwyle · Edwyle Stark ✎
Lord of Winterfell.

### jocelyn_s · Jocelyn Stark ✎
Willam Stark and Melantha Blackwood's only daughter married Benedict Royce of a junior branch. Their three daughters married into the Waynwoods, Corbrays and possibly the Templetons. Catelyn raised these relatives as potential successors when Robb considered his heir, but he preferred Jon Snow.

### rickard · Rickard Stark ✎
His southern marriage plans joined the Starks to the Tullys and Baratheons. Summoned after Brandon’s arrest, he demanded trial by combat. Aerys named fire his champion and burned him alive, making the mock trial one of the immediate provocations of Robert’s Rebellion.

### brandon_r · Brandon Stark ✎
Lyanna’s disappearance sent him to court demanding Rhaegar answer him. Aerys imprisoned him and summoned Rickard. During their joint execution, Brandon strangled himself trying to reach the sword that could free him and save his burning father. His death passed both Winterfell and the Tully marriage to Ned.

### eddard · Eddard Stark ✎
He grew up in the Vale with Robert Baratheon, under Jon Arryn's care. After his elder brother's death, he became Lord of Winterfell, married Catelyn and joined the rebellion. Later, as Robert's Hand, he discovered that Cersei's children were Jaime's. Following the king's death, he was imprisoned and executed on Joffrey's orders despite an agreement to spare him.

### lyanna · Lyanna Stark ✎
Remembered as wilful and brave, she was betrothed to Robert Baratheon. She disappeared with Rhaegar; the books reveal only part of what happened. At the rebellion's end, Eddard found her dying at the Tower of Joy and made a promise whose exact content is not directly disclosed. The television series confirms her marriage to Rhaegar and Jon's birth.

### benjen · Benjen Stark ✎
He joined the Night's Watch after Robert's Rebellion. He became First Ranger and helped Jon adjust to Castle Black. He went ranging beyond the Wall and never returned. His fate remains unknown in the books; he cannot be identified as Coldhands.

### robb · Robb Stark ✎
He led the northern army after his father's arrest. Major victories brought him recognition as King in the North and of the Trident. His marriage to Jeyne Westerling broke his promise to the Freys, while the loss of allies weakened his position. He was murdered at the Red Wedding in a Frey and Bolton conspiracy backed by Tywin.

### jon · Jon Snow ✎
Raised at Winterfell as Eddard Stark's acknowledged bastard, Jon joined the Night's Watch and became Lord Commander. His efforts to prepare for the Others and allow wildlings through the Wall provoked resistance among his brothers. Conspirators attack him at the end of A Dance with Dragons; the published novels have not revealed the outcome. Readers strongly infer that Rhaegar and Lyanna were his parents, but this is explicitly confirmed only in the television series.

### sansa · Sansa Stark ✎
She initially dreamed of an elegant courtly life. After her father's fall, she became a Lannister hostage, endured Joffrey's cruelty and was forced to marry Tyrion. Petyr Baelish helped her escape after Joffrey's poisoning. In the books, she hides in the Vale as Alayne Stone and gradually learns to understand the politics around her.

### arya · Arya Stark ✎
She preferred weapons training to courtly conventions. After her father's execution, she fled the capital and travelled through the war in the riverlands, changing names and falling captive. She reached Braavos and began training with the Faceless Men. Although she tries to abandon her former identity, family ties and memories continue to shape her actions.

### bran · Bran Stark ✎
He lost the use of his legs when Jaime pushed him from a tower. After Winterfell fell, he travelled beyond the Wall with Hodor, Jojen and Meera Reed. He discovered his abilities as a warg and became a pupil of the ancient Brynden Rivers, whose powers are bound to the weirwoods. In the books he is only beginning to understand those abilities; his election as king occurs in the television series.

### rickon · Rickon Stark ✎
He survived Winterfell's fall alongside Bran. When their companions separated, he left with Osha and his direwolf Shaggydog. According to Wex, they were heading for Skagos.
