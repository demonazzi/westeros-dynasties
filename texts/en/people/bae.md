## Бейлиши

### bae_ss · Наёмник Бейлиш ✎
A Braavosi sellsword who came to the Vale in Lord Corbray's service. The family's later knightly and landed branches grew from his descendants.

### bae_hk · Рыцарь Бейлиш, дед Петира ✎
A landless hedge knight who adopted the Titan's stone head as his arms, recalling the family's Braavosi origins.

### bae_lord · Лорд Бейлиш, отец Петира ✎
Held a small tower and a few stony acres on the Fingers. His friendship with Hoster Tully, formed during the War of the Ninepenny Kings, secured Petyr a place at Riverrun as a ward. He died several years before the novels begin.

### petyr · Petyr Baelish ✎
Littlefinger rose from a modest Vale estate through customs appointments to become master of coin. He betrayed Eddard Stark, brokered the Lannister–Tyrell alliance and helped arrange Joffrey's murder and Sansa's escape. The crown rewarded him with Harrenhal and authority over the Trident. After marrying and killing Lysa Arryn, he ruled the Vale as Robert's protector. He concealed Sansa as Alayne Stone and planned her marriage to Harrold Hardyng, hoping to combine the Vale's succession with her claim to Winterfell.
