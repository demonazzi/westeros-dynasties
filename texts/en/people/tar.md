### randyll · Randyll Tarly ✎
A capable commander whose discipline could be brutal. He defeated Robert at Ashford, then later forced Sam into the Watch under threat of death. In Tyrell service he restored order at Maidenpool and helped besiege Dragonstone. As Tommen’s master of laws, he supported Margaery’s release.

### samwell · Samwell Tarly ✎
Threatened with death by his father, he found refuge in the Watch. Fear did not prevent him killing an Other with dragonglass or helping Gilly escape Craster. He also helped secure Jon’s election by persuading rival candidates to withdraw. Jon sent him to the Citadel with Aemon and Gilly; after Aemon died on the voyage, Sam reached Oldtown and encountered Marwyn.

### talla · Talla Tarly ✎
Talla was one of three daughters born to Randyll Tarly and Melessa Florent during the three years between Samwell's birth and Dickon's.

### randyll_daus · Другие дочери Рендилла Тарли ✎
Randyll Tarly and Melessa Florent had two unnamed daughters as well as Talla. All three girls were born during the three years separating Samwell's birth from Dickon's.

### dickon · Dickon Tarly ✎
Sam’s departure made him Horn Hill’s heir. Marriage to Eleanor Mooton connected him with Maidenpool’s succession.
