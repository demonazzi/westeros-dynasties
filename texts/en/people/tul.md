### edmyn · Edmyn Tully ✎
Lord of Riverrun and the first of the major river lords to openly support Aegon against Harren the Black. After Harrenhal fell, he received authority over the Trident. He served as Aegon I's Hand for two years, then returned to govern his own lands.

### edmyn_daus · Три дочери Эдмина Талли ✎
Harren the Black promised them in marriage to whoever killed Aegon's dragons. One later married the Lord of Harrenhal.

## Танец драконов

### grover · Grover Tully ✎
The elderly Lord of Riverrun, who supported Viserys at the Great Council of 101 AC. At the start of the Dance, he wanted to side with Aegon II but was too ill to lead an army. His grandson Elmo delayed committing the house and then supported Rhaenyra.

### grover_son · Сын Гровера Талли, отец Элмо ✎
An unnamed son of Grover Tully was Elmo's father. His descendants included Kermit and Oscar Tully, who fought in the Dance of the Dragons.

### elmo · Elmo Tully ✎
He managed the family's affairs during Grover's illness. After Grover died, he inherited Riverrun and sided with Rhaenyra. He brought an army into the closing stages of the war but fell ill after drinking bad water and died just forty-nine days after succeeding. His son Kermit inherited his authority and command of the river army.

### kermit · Kermit Tully ✎
Lord of Riverrun at the end of the Dance of the Dragons. With his brother Oscar and Benjicot Blackwood, he led the young commanders of the blacks known as the Lads. He killed Borros Baratheon at the Battle of the Kingsroad. Their victory deprived Aegon II of his last substantial army on the approaches to the capital.

### oscar · Oscar Tully ✎
With Kermit and Benjicot Blackwood, he commanded the blacks' river army near the end of the Dance of the Dragons. They were known as the Lads.

## Время книг

### tul_lord17 · Лорд Талли, отец Хостера и Бриндена ✎
Lord of Riverrun.

### hoster · Hoster Tully ✎
Lord of Riverrun. During the rebellion, he secured his house's alliances by marrying his daughters to Eddard and Jon Arryn. He forced Lysa to end her pregnancy by Petyr Baelish, leaving lasting wounds within the family. He was gravely ill by the start of the War of the Five Kings. After his death, Edmure inherited Riverrun.

### brynden_t · Brynden Tully, the Blackfish ✎
The Blackfish. He refused an arranged marriage and served in the Vale as Knight of the Bloody Gate. When war broke out, he returned to his family and became one of Robb's principal commanders. He held Riverrun after the Red Wedding but escaped when Edmure was forced to surrender. His subsequent whereabouts are unknown in the books.

### hoster_sons · Старшие сыновья Хостера Талли ✎
They died in infancy.

### catelyn · Catelyn Tully ✎
After her husband's arrest, she supported Robb, negotiated alliances and tried to protect her family through the war. She released Jaime in the hope of exchanging him for her daughters, angering Robb's allies. Murdered at the Red Wedding, she was restored to life by Beric Dondarrion in the books. As Lady Stoneheart, she leads a ruthless campaign against the Freys and their supporters.

### lysa · Lysa Tully ✎
At Petyr Baelish's urging, she poisoned her husband and sent her sister a false accusation against the Lannisters. After his death, she kept the Vale out of the war and became fiercely overprotective of her son. She married Petyr, but her jealousy of Sansa led to a final confrontation: he pushed Lysa through the Moon Door.

### edmure · Edmure Tully ✎
He cared for the people of his lands, but did not always understand his commanders' wider plans. During the war, he defended the river crossings against Tywin, displeasing Robb. His marriage to Roslin Frey was meant to restore their alliance, but the wedding became a massacre. He remained a prisoner and was later forced to order Riverrun's surrender to protect his family.

### hoster_son · Последний сын Хостера Талли ✎
