## Карстарки — жёны Старков

### alys_kk · Alys Karstark ✎
Alys became Lady of Winterfell through her marriage to Brandon Stark, Cregan's youngest son. Their children were Rodwell, Beron, and Arsa. She is the historical Alys Karstark, distinct from Rickard's daughter who later fled to the Wall.

### lysara · Lysara Karstark ✎
Lysara married Artos Stark, known as the Implacable, and bore twin sons, Brandon and Benjen.

## Кархолд в конце третьего века

### arnolf · Arnolf Karstark ✎
His apparent loyalty to Stannis concealed a Bolton plot to seize Karhold. In a published Winds of Winter chapter, Stannis exposed and arrested him.

### cregan_k · Cregan Karstark ✎
He pursued Alys to the Wall to enforce a marriage. Jon confined him in an ice cell.

### arnolf_gs · Шестеро внуков Арнольфа Карстарка ✎
Three were arrested in Stannis’s camp after Arnolf’s treachery was exposed, in a published Winds of Winter chapter.

### arthor · Arthor Karstark ✎
Stannis arrested him with Arnolf when their betrayal was discovered, in a published Winds of Winter chapter.

### arnolf_daus · Дочери Арнольфа Карстарка ✎
Arnolf Karstark's unnamed daughters, sisters of Cregan and Arthor. Some of Arnolf's grandsons could be their children, but the precise relationships are uncertain. Neither the daughters nor their husbands are named.

### rickard_k · Rickard Karstark ✎
Grief for his sons became defiance of Robb’s authority. He murdered two young prisoners after Jaime’s release; Robb executed him, and many Karstark soldiers deserted.

### harrion · Harrion Karstark ✎
Captivity kept him from ruling Karhold after Rickard’s execution. Arnolf hoped his own declared support for Stannis would cause the crown to kill Harrion.

### eddard_k · Eddard Karstark ✎
Jaime killed him defending Robb in the Whispering Wood.

### torrhen_k · Torrhen Karstark ✎
He fell to Jaime’s sword while protecting Robb in the Whispering Wood.

### alys_k · Alys Karstark ✎
Flight to the Wall saved her from Cregan’s forced marriage. Jon arranged her marriage to Sigorn, giving her the Thenns’ protection against her scheming kin.
