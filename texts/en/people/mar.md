## Завоевание и Первая Дорнийская война

### meria · Meria Martell
Princess of Dorne during the Conquest, called the Yellow Toad. She refused to acknowledge Aegon and Rhaenys, choosing to wear the invaders down rather than face their dragons in open battle. Under her rule, Dorne endured the destruction of the First Dornish War and remained independent. Meraxes's death above Hellholt was the conquerors' heaviest defeat.

### nymor · Nymor Martell
He inherited Dorne after Meria's death. Seeking to end the war, he sent his daughter Deria to Aegon with Meraxes's skull and a sealed letter. After reading it, the king agreed to peace. Its contents are unknown, so explanations for Aegon's sudden change of heart remain speculation.

### deria · Deria Martell
She represented Dorne in negotiations with Aegon the Conqueror. She brought Meraxes's skull and her father's letter to the capital, after which the First Dornish War ended. Despite a hostile reception, she completed her diplomatic mission. She later ruled Dorne herself.

## Эпоха Танца драконов

### qoren · Qoren Martell
Prince of Dorne during Viserys I's reign and at the start of the Dance of the Dragons. He supported the Triarchy in the struggle for the Stepstones, fearing Targaryen and Velaryon power near the Dornish coast. During the crown's civil war, he maintained Dorne's independence. His daughter Aliandra succeeded him.

### aliandra · Aliandra Martell
She ruled Dorne after Qoren's death. A young and ambitious princess, she encouraged Dornish raids into the borderlands. She received Alyn Velaryon during his voyages. Accounts of their relationship differed, so a love affair cannot be regarded as established.

### qyle · Qyle Martell
During Aegon III's regency, he was considered as a possible husband for Baela Targaryen.

### coryanne · Coryanne Martell
Coryanne was the younger sister of Aliandra and Qyle Martell. During Aegon III's regency, Aliandra's flirtation with Alyn Velaryon angered both her siblings, who resented the attention she gave the visiting lord.

## Мирия и Марон: союз с Таргариенами

### dorne_p14 · Принц Дорна, отец Мирии и Марона
Ruler of Dorne. He arranged Myriah's marriage to the future Daeron II; Maron later succeeded him in Dorne.

### myriah · Myriah Martell
Her marriage to Daeron II helped replace years of war between Dorne and the crown with a dynastic alliance. She bore Baelor, Aerys, Rhaegel and Maekar. Through her children and relatives, she linked the royal court to the Martells, provoking resentment among opponents of Dornish influence.

### maron · Maron Martell
In 187 AC he married Daenerys, Daeron II's sister, securing Dorne's peaceful entry into the realm. His lands retained their own laws and titles, including succession without preference for sons over elder daughters.

### maron_son · Старший сын Марона Мартелла
Heir to Sunspear. His name is unknown.

### maron_sons · Младшие сыновья Марона Мартелла
Maron Martell and Daenerys Targaryen had younger sons whose names are unknown. Daenerys let her children play with other Dornish children at the Water Gardens and later welcomed common-born children there as well.

### maron_daus · Дочери Марона Мартелла
Maron Martell and Daenerys Targaryen had daughters whose names are unrecorded. Their parents' marriage helped seal Dorne's entry into the Seven Kingdoms.

## Время книг

### dorne_p19 · Принцесса Дорна, мать Дорана, Элии и Оберина
Ruler of Dorne. She was a friend of Joanna Lannister and discussed marriages between their children. After Joanna died, she visited Casterly Rock, but Tywin refused the proposed matches and offered Tyrion as a husband for Elia.

### lewyn · Lewyn Martell
He served in Aerys II's Kingsguard and commanded the Dornish contingent in the royal army. He died at the Battle of the Trident.

### doran · Doran Martell
The ruling Prince of Dorne. Afflicted by gout, he acted cautiously, which many close to him mistook for inaction. He secretly prepared for a Targaryen restoration and revenge for his sister's death, but his plans depended on complicated marriage agreements and distant allies. He remains alive in the books.

### mors_m · Mors Martell
He died in infancy.

### olyvar · Olyvar Martell
He died in infancy.

### elia · Elia Martell
She was sickly but remembered by those close to her as kind and considerate. After Rhaegar left, she remained in the capital with her children. Gregor Clegane killed her during the sack of King's Landing; her family's deaths began the long feud between the Martells and Lannisters.

### oberyn · Oberyn Martell
The Red Viper. A warrior, traveller and student of poisons, he fathered eight daughters known as the Sand Snakes. He came to the capital seeking justice for Elia and volunteered to defend Tyrion in a trial by combat. He nearly defeated Gregor Clegane, but his attempt to force a confession gave his opponent the chance to kill him.

### arianne · Arianne Martell
Dorne's heir under its laws. Believing her father intended to give Quentyn the succession, she tried to crown Myrcella and pursue her own political plans. The plot failed, leaving Arys Oakheart dead and Myrcella wounded. After confronting Doran, she learned of the family's secret plans. She remains alive in the published novels.

### quentyn · Quentyn Martell
Sent to Essos to seek a marriage with Daenerys under an old agreement. He reached Meereen after a difficult journey, but Daenerys had already chosen another marriage. Hoping to salvage his mission, he tried to take one of her dragons. Rhaegal burned him, and he died a few days later without returning to Dorne.

### trystane · Trystane Martell
He was betrothed to Myrcella under Doran's agreement with Tyrion. He lived in Dorne and took no part in Arianne's attempt to proclaim Myrcella queen.

### sand_snakes · Sand Snakes
Oberyn's eight bastard daughters: Obara, Nymeria, Tyene, Sarella, Elia, Obella, Dorea and Loreza. Ellaria Sand bore the four youngest; the older daughters had different mothers. After their father's death, the elder sisters demanded revenge, but their methods and views differed. Doran tried to put their abilities to work in his secret plans.
