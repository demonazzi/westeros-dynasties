He welcomed Rhaena to Fair Isle. A fish bone lodged in his throat killed him in 50 AC.

### franklyn_f · Franklyn Farman ✎
His attempt to remove Rhaena met resistance on Fair Isle. He also withdrew Elissa’s allowance.

### elissa · Elissa Farman ✎
The sea offered a freedom her family would not. She took three dragon eggs from Dragonstone and sold them in Braavos to fund Sun Chaser. Sailing as Alys Westhill, she found three islands beyond the Sunset Sea, then continued west when her companions turned back. Corlys Velaryon later believed he saw her ship in Asshai. Neither that sighting nor any surviving account settles what became of Elissa herself.

### androw · Androw Farman ✎
Excluded and mocked at Rhaena’s court, he poisoned several of her companions. When exposed, he confessed and jumped to his death.
