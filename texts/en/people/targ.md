## Таргариены: владыки Драконьего Камня

### aenar · Aenar the Exile ✎
Heeding Daenys's dream of Valyria's destruction, Aenar sold his holdings and moved his household to Dragonstone in 114 BC. He brought his wealth and five dragons, including Balerion. Twelve years later the Doom destroyed the Freehold; the family's withdrawal had preserved its dragons and its future.

### daenys · Daenys the Dreamer ✎
Her dream of Valyria burning persuaded Aenar to leave the Freehold twelve years before the Doom. She became known as the Dreamer, and her prophetic writings were collected in Signs and Portents. Her marriage to Gaemon continued the family's line on Dragonstone.

### gaemon · Gaemon the Glorious ✎
Succeeded Aenar as Lord of Dragonstone during the Century of Blood. Remembered as Gaemon the Glorious, he ruled with Daenys before the island passed to Aegon and Elaena.

### aegon_l · Aegon ✎
Ruled Dragonstone jointly with Elaena after Gaemon's death. Their son Maegon succeeded them during the Century of Blood.

### elaena_l · Elaena ✎
Shared the rule of Dragonstone with Aegon during the Century of Blood. Their successor was Maegon.

### gaemon_dau · Младшая дочь Гейемона Славного ✎
Married a petty lord. A descendant of that marriage later put forward a claim at the Great Council of 101 AC.

### maegon · Maegon ✎
Held Dragonstone after the joint rule of Aegon and Elaena. Aerys succeeded him.

### aerys_l · Aerys ✎
Succeeded Maegon as Lord of Dragonstone. Aelyx, Baelon and Daemion later held the island in turn.

### aelyx · Aelyx ✎
Inherited Dragonstone after Aerys. His rule was followed by Baelon's.

### baelon_l · Baelon ✎
Held Dragonstone between the lordships of Aelyx and Daemion, before the Conquest.

### daemion · Daemion ✎
Ruled Dragonstone after Baelon and retained a maester there, a practice continued by his successors. Aerion inherited the island from him.

### aerion_l · Aerion ✎
Held Dragonstone in the generation before the Conquest. His marriage to Valaena Velaryon produced the three future conquerors. Orys Baratheon was widely believed to be his bastard, although the surviving accounts do not establish that parentage as fact.

## Завоеватели

### visenya · Visenya ✎
Rode Vhagar and wielded Dark Sister during the Conquest, taking an active part in both war and government. She helped create Aegon's Kingsguard after demonstrating the inadequacy of his existing protection. When Aenys died, she recalled Maegor from exile and supported his seizure of the throne. Her authority rested on experience, force and a willingness to use both.

### aegon1 · Aegon the Conqueror ✎
From Dragonstone, Aegon brought six of Westeros's seven kingdoms under his authority. With Visenya, Rhaenys and their dragons, he defeated the principal rival kings, founded King's Landing and forged the Iron Throne. Dorne resisted the Conquest and later invasions. Royal progresses and new appointments helped turn the military victory into a lasting monarchy.

### rhaenys · Rhaenys ✎
Rhaenys helped conquer and govern Westeros while riding Meraxes. She enjoyed songs, dancing and flight, and arranged marriages to strengthen the new realm. A scorpion bolt brought Meraxes down at Hellholt in 10 AC. Whether Rhaenys died in the fall or survived it remains disputed; her body was never returned.

### aenys1 · Aenys I ✎
Aenys inherited a realm held together by his father's victories but struggled to answer its rebellions. He preferred conciliation and delayed difficult decisions. The marriage of Aegon and Rhaena brought the Faith's opposition to Targaryen incest into open revolt. He died on Dragonstone in 42 AC, leaving his heir an unsettled kingdom and Maegor an opportunity to seize it.

### maegor1 · Maegor the Cruel ✎
Maegor took the crown ahead of his nephew and used Balerion to break resistance, particularly the Faith Militant. He completed the Red Keep, then murdered its builders to protect its secrets. Executions and punishments increasingly stripped him of support. Six marriages produced no surviving heir. Found dead on the Iron Throne in 48 AC, he left a disputed account of his own death and a realm ready to accept Jaehaerys.

## Дети Эйниса I и Алиссы Веларион

### rhaena_a · Rhaena ✎
Rhaena rode Dreamfyre and survived the killing of Aegon the Uncrowned, only to be forced into marriage with Maegor. She escaped with her daughter Aerea and the sword Blackfyre, supporting Jaehaerys's claim. Later quarrels and bereavements, especially Aerea's death, separated her from the court. She spent her final years at Harrenhal.

### aegon_u · Aegon the Uncrowned ✎
Denied the crown when Maegor seized it, Aegon gathered supporters and attempted to recover his inheritance. He claimed Quicksilver, but faced Maegor and the much larger Balerion at the Gods Eye. Both prince and dragon were killed. His title, the Uncrowned, records the coronation he never received.

### aerea · Aerea ✎
For a time Aerea stood next in the succession after Jaehaerys. In 54 AC she left Dragonstone on Balerion and returned two years later, dying from a horrifying illness. Septon Barth believed the dragon had carried her to ruined Valyria. No witness confirmed their route or the source of the creatures found within her body.

### rhaella_a · Rhaella ✎
Entered religious life at Oldtown and became a septa. The chronicle notes a striking reversal in the twins' temperaments and preserves the suspicion that she and Aerea exchanged identities. It offers no conclusive proof of the supposed exchange.

### viserys_a · Viserys ✎
Kept as Maegor's squire and hostage, Viserys was left behind when Alyssa escaped with her younger children. Maegor had him tortured for nine days until he died. His body was then displayed in the Red Keep in an attempt to draw Alyssa out of hiding.

### jaehaerys1 · Jaehaerys the Conciliator ✎
Over fifty-five years, Jaehaerys restored the crown's relationship with the Faith, revised the realm's laws and expanded its roads. Alysanne and advisers such as Septon Barth shared much of that work. His family endured repeated losses, and disagreements over who should inherit outlasted the apparent stability of his reign. The Great Council of 101 AC settled the immediate succession in favour of Viserys.

### alysanne · Good Queen Alysanne ✎
Alysanne used royal progresses and women's courts to hear grievances directly. She pressed for the abolition of the first night and helped secure the New Gift for the Night's Watch. Silverwing carried her across the kingdom. Her influence reached well beyond ceremony, though she repeatedly disagreed with Jaehaerys over marriage arrangements and the claims of female heirs.

### vaella_a · Vaella ✎
Died in early childhood.

## Дети Джейехериса I и Алисанны

### aegon_j · Эйгон, сын Джейехериса I ✎
Lived for three days. His death left Jaehaerys and Alysanne without the heir they had hoped for.

### daenerys_j · Дейенерис, дочь Джейехериса I ✎
Alysanne regarded Daenerys as her rightful heir, while Jaehaerys looked ahead to a son's succession and a marriage between the children. The dispute ended with Daenerys's death from the Shivers in 60 AC, a loss that also challenged the family's confidence that Targaryens resisted ordinary diseases.

### aemon_j · Aemon ✎
As Prince of Dragonstone, Aemon served as Jaehaerys's heir, master of laws and lord justiciar. He rode Caraxes against Dornish and Myrish forces. A crossbow bolt killed him on Tarth in 92 AC while he stood beside Lord Cameron Tarth. Jaehaerys then chose Baelon over Aemon's daughter Rhaenys, reopening the question of female succession.

### baelon_j · Baelon the Brave ✎
Baelon rode Vhagar, became the king's heir after Aemon's death and later served as Hand. He avenged Aemon against the Myrish invaders of Tarth. In 101 AC a sudden illness described as a burst belly killed him. The loss prompted Jaehaerys to summon the Great Council to determine the next heir.

### alyssa_j · Alyssa ✎
Alyssa relished riding Meleys and took her infant sons flying with her. She preferred vigorous activity to the expected reserve of a princess. Her third pregnancy ended in a difficult delivery; she never fully recovered and died in 84 AC. The child, Aegon, also died in infancy.

### maegelle · Септа Мейгелле ✎
A learned and compassionate septa, Maegelle helped reconcile Jaehaerys and Alysanne after their quarrels. She cared for children suffering from greyscale and eventually contracted it herself. She died at Oldtown in 96 AC.

### vaegon · Vaegon the Dragonless ✎
Uninterested in courtship or martial training, Vaegon found his place among the books of the Citadel. He became an archmaester and never claimed a dragon. After Baelon's death he advised Jaehaerys to convene a Great Council rather than settle the succession alone. His vows excluded him from its choice of heir.

### daella_j · Дейелла, дочь Джейехериса I ✎
Daella struggled with reading, unfamiliar people and the demands of court. Allowed to choose from several proposed husbands, she chose Rodrik Arryn and moved to the Vale. She died of childbed fever in 82 AC shortly after delivering Aemma, whose later marriage brought this branch back into the royal succession.

### saera · Saera ✎
Saera's relationships at court led to a bitter confrontation with Jaehaerys and confinement under the Faith's supervision. She escaped Oldtown for Lys and later owned a profitable pleasure house in Volantis. She did not reconcile with her father. Three of her sons sought the crown at the Great Council of 101 AC, but she stayed in Essos.

### viserra · Viserra ✎
Betrothed to the elderly Theomore Manderly, Viserra resisted the match and sought a different future at court. Before the wedding she rode through King's Landing with companions after drinking. A collision threw her against a wall and broke her neck. She was fifteen.

### gaemon_j · Гейемон, сын Джейехериса I ✎
Born prematurely and lived for about three months. He was named for Gaemon the Glorious.

### valerion · Валерион ✎
Died before his first nameday.

### gael · Gael ✎
Called the Winter Child, Gael remained especially close to Alysanne. The court announced her death as a fever, but later accounts said she had borne a singer's stillborn child and drowned herself in Blackwater Bay. Her death deepened the queen's grief in her final years.

## Внуки Джейехериса

### rhaenys_q · Rhaenys, the Queen Who Never Was ✎
Passed over after Prince Aemon's death, Rhaenys saw Baelon chosen as the next heir. At the Great Council of 101 AC, Laenor represented her branch's principal claim, but Viserys prevailed. The decision gave her the name the Queen Who Never Was. During the Dance she supported Rhaenyra and died with Meleys fighting Sunfyre and Vhagar at Rook's Rest.

### viserys1 · Viserys I ✎
Chosen by the Great Council of 101 AC, Viserys inherited the peace built under Jaehaerys. After Aemma's death he named Rhaenyra his heir and retained that decision despite having sons by Alicent. Attempts to keep both branches of the family together failed to settle their competing claims. His death allowed the dispute to become the Dance of the Dragons.

### daemon · Daemon ✎
Daemon commanded the gold cloaks, rode Caraxes and carried Dark Sister. His campaigns in the Stepstones brought a short-lived royal title. Later he became a leading commander for Rhaenyra during the Dance. Above the Gods Eye, he leapt onto Vhagar and killed Aemond before the dragons fell into the lake. His body was never found; tales of his survival remain unconfirmed.

### aegon_b · Эйгон, сын Бейлона ✎
Survived his mother's difficult labour but died before reaching his first nameday. Alyssa had never recovered from the birth.

### saera_sons · Внебрачные сыновья Саэры ✎
Three sons put forward claims at the Great Council of 101 AC. One was the son of a Volantene triarch; another was said to resemble Jaehaerys in his youth. Saera herself did not attend.

### gael_son · Мертворождённый сын Гаэль ✎
Born dead. Gael subsequently drowned herself in Blackwater Bay.

## Дети Визериса I

### viserys1_son · Сын Визериса I и Эйммы Аррен ✎
Died in infancy, before Rhaenyra's birth.

### rhaenyra · Rhaenyra ✎
Named heir in 105 AC, Rhaenyra was crowned on Dragonstone after Viserys's death, while the greens crowned Aegon II. Her supporters initially gained the capital, but taxation, rumours and the uprising in King's Landing destroyed her hold on it. She fled to Dragonstone, where Aegon ordered Sunfyre to devour her before her son Aegon's eyes.

### baelon_v · Бейлон, Наследник на день ✎
Aemma died giving birth to Baelon, who survived her only briefly. His death ended Viserys's immediate hope for a male heir. An alleged remark by Daemon about an "heir for a day" contributed to Viserys naming Rhaenyra his successor instead.

### aegon2 · Aegon II ✎
Crowned with the greens' support despite Viserys's chosen succession, Aegon fought Rhaenyra for the throne. He suffered severe burns at Rook's Rest and further injuries taking Dragonstone. He had Sunfyre kill Rhaenyra and recovered King's Landing, but advancing enemy armies left his rule precarious. Poisoned in 131 AC, he was succeeded by Aegon III.

### helaena · Helaena ✎
A rider of Dreamfyre, Helaena was well liked by the people of King's Landing. Blood and Cheese destroyed her life at court: after forcing her to choose between her sons, they killed Jaehaerys instead of the child she named. She withdrew from her children and her duties. Her fatal fall from Maegor's Holdfast was generally regarded as suicide, though rumours of murder also circulated.

### aemond · Aemond One-Eye ✎
Claimed Vhagar as a boy and lost an eye in the fight that followed. During the Dance he killed Lucerys, governed while Aegon recovered from his wounds and devastated the riverlands. His search for Daemon ended above the Gods Eye. Daemon leapt from Caraxes onto Vhagar and drove Dark Sister through Aemond's remaining eye; both riders and dragons fell.

### daeron_d · Daeron the Daring ✎
Raised in Oldtown, Daeron joined the Hightower campaign on Tessarion. His intervention at the Honeywine rescued the army and earned him the name the Daring. Later victories gave way to uncontrolled violence and the dragonseeds' betrayals. He died during the Second Battle of Tumbleton, but the chroniclers disagree about how he was killed.

### trystane_tf · Trystane Truefyre ✎
Perkin the Flea crowned his young squire during the chaos after Rhaenyra fled King's Landing, claiming he was Viserys's bastard. Their followers occupied the Red Keep and received mass knighthoods. When Aegon II recovered the city, Trystane was executed. His supposed royal parentage was never established.

## Дети Деймона и Лейны Веларион

### baela · Baela ✎
Baela flew Moondancer against Aegon II and Sunfyre during the fall of Dragonstone. Her dragon died, but she survived the fight and captivity. After the war she rejected the husband chosen by the regents and married Alyn Velaryon. Her spirited conduct repeatedly frustrated the royal council's attempts to control the surviving princesses.

### rhaena_d · Rhaena ✎
Sheltered in the Vale during the Dance, Rhaena finally obtained a living dragon when Morning hatched there. After the war she returned to court and married Corwyn Corbray. His killing at Runestone left her widowed. She later married Garmund Hightower and had six daughters.

### son_laena · Сын Деймона и Лейны ✎
Died shortly after birth. Laena also failed to recover from the labour.

## Дети Рейениры

### jacaerys · Jacaerys Velaryon ✎
Rhaenyra's heir flew Vermax north to secure support, concluding the Pact of Ice and Fire with Cregan Stark. He also encouraged the search for new riders among the dragonseeds. Laenor was his acknowledged father, although rumours named Harwin Strong. During the Battle of the Gullet, Jacaerys was killed while defending the Velaryon ships.

### lucerys · Lucerys Velaryon ✎
The heir to Driftmark rode Arrax and bore an old grievance with Aemond, whose eye he had cut out as a child. Sent to seek Borros Baratheon's support, he found Aemond already at Storm's End. Borros forbade bloodshed in his hall, but Vhagar pursued Arrax over Shipbreaker Bay and killed dragon and rider.

### joffrey · Joffrey Velaryon ✎
During the uprising in King's Landing, Joffrey tried to reach the Dragonpit and save Tyraxes. He mounted his mother's dragon Syrax without permission. She would not accept another rider and threw him off, killing him in a fall onto the city streets.

### aegon3 · Aegon the Dragonbane ✎
Witnessing his mother's death left Aegon deeply afraid of dragons. Crowned after the Dance, he endured a turbulent minority under regents while much of his family and the realm recovered from the war. The last Targaryen dragon died during his reign, earning him the name Dragonbane without proving his responsibility. His second marriage to Daenaera produced five children after the childless marriage to Jaehaera.

### viserys2 · Viserys II ✎
Captured during the Battle of the Gullet, Viserys was presumed dead until Alyn brought him back from Lys with Larra Rogare. He later served as Hand to Aegon III and both of Aegon's sons, providing continuity through very different reigns. He became king after Baelor's death but ruled for only about a year. The claim that Aegon IV poisoned him remains unproven.

### visenya_r · Висенья, дочь Рейениры ✎
Born dead after Rhaenyra learned of Viserys's death and Aegon II's coronation. The news brought on a prolonged, premature labour.

## Дети Эйгона II

### jaehaerys_a · Джейехерис, сын Эйгона II ✎
The young heir had a dragon, Shrykos, but never lived to ride her. Blood and Cheese murdered him in the Red Keep in revenge for Lucerys's death. They forced Helaena to name a son to spare the other, then killed Jaehaerys despite her choice of Maelor.

### jaehaera · Jaehaera ✎
Married Aegon III after the Dance as part of the settlement between the surviving royal branches. In 133 AC she fell onto the spikes beneath Maegor's Holdfast. Her death was recorded as suicide, though the chronicle preserves suspicions of murder and Unwin Peake's involvement.

### maelor · Maelor ✎
Rickard Thorne attempted to carry Maelor away after the blacks took King's Landing. They were recognized at Bitterbridge, where townspeople fought over the reward offered for the prince. Both were killed. The accounts disagree about precisely how the child died amid the struggle.

### aegon2_son · Внебрачный сын Эйгона II ✎
Mushroom claimed that Aegon fathered the boy on a woman of the Street of Silk whose maidenhead he had bought. No independent account establishes the parentage.

### aegon2_dau · Внебрачная дочь Эйгона II ✎
Mushroom attributed the girl's birth to Aegon's relationship with one of Alicent's servants. The allegation of royal parentage is unconfirmed.

### gaemon_ph · Gaemon Palehair ✎
Crowned in a brothel during the Moon of the Three Kings, Gaemon became the figurehead of a rival court on the Street of Silk. Essie first named Aegon II as his father, then named a Lysene oarsman under torture. Spared after the court fell, he became Aegon III's companion. Poison intended for the royal household killed him.

## Сын Эймонда

### aemond_son · Сын Алис Риверс, мнимый сын Эймонда ✎
Alys Rivers presented the boy as Aemond's legitimate son and a claimant to the throne. The surviving accounts do not establish that claim or reveal what ultimately became of him.

## Дети Эйгона III и Дейнейры Веларион

### daeron1 · Daeron the Young Dragon ✎
Daeron conquered Dorne without dragons while still a teenager, winning the name the Young Dragon. Holding the country proved harder than defeating its armies. Rebellion drew him back into war, and he was killed under a banner of truce. He left no children, so Baelor inherited the crown.

### baelor1 · Baelor the Blessed ✎
Baelor ended the Dornish war and walked south to negotiate peace and free Aemon the Dragonknight. His piety also led him to annul his marriage and confine his sisters in the Maidenvault. He commissioned the Great Sept but did not live to see it finished. A prolonged fast preceded his death; rumours that Viserys poisoned him remain unproved.

### daena · Daena the Defiant ✎
Daena resisted the confinement imposed on her and her sisters in the Maidenvault. She escaped its restrictions on several occasions and bore Aegon's son Daemon while refusing to identify the father publicly. After Baelor's death, the crown passed over the sisters to Viserys. Her son later founded the Blackfyre line.

### rhaena_b · Рейена, дочь Эйгона III ✎
Confined in the Maidenvault with Daena and Elaena, Rhaena found comfort in the Faith rather than the adventures her sisters desired. After Baelor's death released them, she became a septa.

### elaena_b · Elaena ✎
Elaena spent her youth in the Maidenvault but later proved an able manager of money. During her marriage to Ronnel Penrose she effectively performed much of his work as master of coin. Her earlier relationship with Alyn Velaryon produced the Waters twins. Three marriages followed, the last to Michael Manwoody.

## Дети Визериса II и Ларры Рогаре

### aegon4 · Aegon the Unworthy ✎
Aegon rewarded favourites, sold influence and repeatedly humiliated Naerys. He encouraged doubts about Daeron's parentage and gave Blackfyre to his bastard Daemon. On his deathbed he legitimized all his bastards, including those born to women of low rank. The decree left rival lines with competing claims and helped prepare the ground for the Blackfyre rebellions.

### aemon_dk · Aemon the Dragonknight ✎
A celebrated swordsman with Dark Sister, Aemon served the Kingsguard and became its Lord Commander. He defended Naerys's honour, though rumours about their relationship and Daeron's parentage never became established facts. Despite Aegon IV's hostility, he died protecting the king from assassins.

### naerys · Naerys ✎
Naerys would have preferred a religious life, but Viserys arranged her marriage to Aegon. Repeated pregnancies endangered her health, while her husband mistreated her and questioned Daeron's legitimacy. Aemon defended her against accusations of adultery. She died after a later childbirth, with the accusations against her still unproved.

## Дети Эйгона IV

### daeron2 · Daeron the Good ✎
Daeron secured Dorne's peaceful entry into the realm through marriage and negotiation, rather than another invasion. Court hostility towards Dornish influence and rumours about his parentage helped gather support for Daemon Blackfyre. He survived the First Blackfyre Rebellion and retained the throne. The Great Spring Sickness killed him in 209 AC.

### twins_n · Близнецы Нейерис ✎
Both children died soon after their birth in 161 AC. Naerys barely survived the labour. Baelor mourned them with a fast and sent Aegon to Braavos, giving the queen time to recover.

### daenerys_n · Дейенерис, дочь Эйгона IV ✎
Her marriage to Maron Martell helped seal Dorne's peaceful entry into the Seven Kingdoms. At the Water Gardens, built as a wedding gift, she invited servants' children to play alongside the highborn. The gardens became a summer residence for the ruling family.

### naerys_twin · Мертворождённый сын Эйгона IV и Нейерис ✎
Born dead, while his twin Daenerys survived.

### naerys_last · Последний ребёнок Эйгона IV и Нейерис ✎
Naerys died following the birth. The child's survival is not recorded, and the accounts suggest that both were lost.

### daemon_bf · Daemon I Blackfyre ✎
Aegon's gift of Blackfyre gave Daemon both a name and a powerful symbol for his claim. He eventually rebelled against Daeron II. At the Redgrass Field, Bloodraven's archers killed him and his eldest twins. Bittersteel took the surviving family to Essos, where the descendants continued to seek the crown.

### alysanne_mg · Alysanne ✎
After Aegon's relationship with Megette ended, she and her sisters were entrusted to the Faith to be raised as septas.

### lily_mg · Lily ✎
Placed in the Faith's care with her sisters to be raised as a septa after Aegon's separation from Megette.

### willow_mg · Willow ✎
Raised under the Faith's supervision as a future septa alongside her sisters.

### rosey_mg · Rosey ✎
Entrusted to the Faith with her sisters for an upbringing intended to lead to life as a septa.

### bellenora · Bellenora Otherys ✎
Bellenora became a celebrated courtesan in Braavos and inherited her mother's name, the Black Pearl. Her descent from Aegon was traditionally claimed, although Bellegere had other lovers and the paternity was not certain.

### narha · Narha Otherys ✎
Born during Bellegere's long relationship with Aegon. The prince was credited as her father, but Bellegere's other relationships left that claim uncertain.

### balerion_o · Balerion Otherys ✎
Traditionally counted among Aegon's children by Bellegere Otherys. His mother's other lovers meant the prince's paternity could not be established with certainty.

### aegor · Aegor Rivers, Bittersteel ✎
Bittersteel backed Daemon Blackfyre and took the surviving family into exile after the Redgrass Field. He founded the Golden Company to keep displaced Westerosi fighters together and preserve the Blackfyre cause. His rivalry with Bloodraven drew on the Bracken–Blackwood feud, political loyalties and their competition for Shiera Seastar.

### mya · Mya Rivers ✎
Included in Aegon IV's deathbed decree legitimizing his bastards.

### gwenys · Gwenys Rivers ✎
Legitimized with Aegon IV's other bastards by his final decree.

### brynden · Brynden Rivers, Bloodraven ✎
Bloodraven used archers, spies and royal authority against the Blackfyres, later serving as Hand under Aerys I and Maekar. Aegon V sent him to the Wall for executing Aenys Blackfyre despite a promise of safe conduct. He became Lord Commander and vanished on a ranging. Bran eventually found him beyond the Wall, sustained within a weirwood cave.

### shiera · Shiera Seastar ✎
Shiera was renowned for her beauty, learning and mismatched eyes; tales also credited her with sorcery. She took Bloodraven as a lover but repeatedly declined marriage. Bittersteel also sought her favour, adding a personal grievance to the brothers' political rivalry.

### butterwell_kids · Бастарды Эйгона IV от дочерей лорда Баттервелла ✎
Stories credited Aegon IV with fathering children on all three of Lord Butterwell's daughters in a single night. The king gave their father a dragon egg, later offered as the prize at the Whitewalls tourney.

### jeyne_lt · Jeyne Lothston ✎
Became Aegon IV's mistress at fourteen. Rumours also named him as her father. After catching a pox from the king, she and her family were quickly sent away from court.

## Блэкфайры

### aegon_bf · Эйгон Блэкфайр ✎
Daemon's intended heir was killed by Bloodraven's archers at the Redgrass Field. Daemon tried to shield him and fell under the same attack, with the death of the prince's twin Aemon following.

### aemon_bf · Эймон Блэкфайр ✎
After Daemon and Aegon fell at the Redgrass Field, Aemon picked up Blackfyre and tried to rally their men. Bloodraven's archers killed him too.

### daemon2_bf · Деймон II Блэкфайр ✎
Travelled as John the Fiddler to the wedding tourney at Whitewalls in 212 AC. His supporters hoped a dragon egg would hatch and rally the realm to his claim. Bloodraven exposed the conspiracy before it became a sustained war. Daemon was defeated in the lists and taken captive.

### haegon · Хейгон I Блэкфайр ✎
Led the Third Blackfyre Rebellion with Bittersteel in 219 AC. After the invasion failed, he surrendered his sword but was killed treacherously. The surviving histories do not identify the precise circumstances of that killing.

### daemon3_bf · Деймон III Блэкфайр ✎
Invaded Westeros with Bittersteel in 236 AC in the Fourth Blackfyre Rebellion. The campaign quickly failed, and Duncan the Tall killed him. The remaining Blackfyre claimants continued their cause from exile.

### haegon_sons · Младшие сыновья Хейгона I Блэкфайра ✎
Haegon I Blackfyre had younger sons besides his eldest, Daemon III. They belonged to the exiled Blackfyre family, but their names and later lives are not recorded.

### aenys_bf · Эйнис Блэкфайр ✎
Sought the crown through the Great Council of 233 AC rather than another invasion. Bloodraven promised him safe passage, then had him beheaded on arrival. Aegon V punished that breach of the king's peace by sending Bloodraven to the Night's Watch.

### daemon_bf_sons · Двое младших сыновей Деймона I Блэкфайра ✎
The two youngest of Daemon Blackfyre and Rohanne of Tyrosh's seven sons remain unnamed.

### calla · Калла Блэкфайр ✎
Promised in marriage to Bittersteel as part of Daemon's alliance with his principal supporter. The surviving accounts do not confirm whether the wedding took place.

### daemon_bf_daus · Младшие дочери Деймона I Блэкфайра ✎
Daemon Blackfyre and Rohanne of Tyrosh had daughters besides Calla. Their names and exact number are unknown; Calla is the only daughter identified in the published histories.

### maelys · Maelys the Monstrous ✎
Known for immense strength and the second head growing from his neck, Maelys joined the Band of Nine and prepared a Blackfyre invasion. He killed Ormund Baratheon during the fighting in the Stepstones, then fell to Barristan Selmy. His death ended the known male line of House Blackfyre.

## Дети Дейерона II и Мирии Мартелл

### baelor_b · Baelor Breakspear ✎
As heir and Hand, Baelor combined martial skill with a reputation for sound judgment. At Ashford he joined Duncan's side in the trial of seven, opposing members of his own family. A head wound, probably inflicted by Maekar, killed him after the contest. His death abruptly changed the succession.

### valarr · Валарр, Молодой принц ✎
Succeeded Baelor as Daeron II's heir after the trial at Ashford. His marriage to Kiera produced stillborn twins. The Great Spring Sickness killed him, Matarys and Daeron in 209 AC, clearing the way for Aerys I's succession.

### valarr_sons · Мертворождённые сыновья Валарра ✎
Both twins were stillborn.

### matarys · Матарис ✎
Died in the Great Spring Sickness of 209 AC, along with Valarr and Daeron II. Their deaths removed Baelor's surviving branch from the immediate royal succession.

### aerys1 · Aerys I ✎
Aerys preferred books and prophecy to the routine of government, leaving much of it to Bloodraven. Ironborn attacks and further Blackfyre attempts disturbed his reign. His childless marriage meant the succession passed through several relatives before settling on Maekar.

### rhaegel · Рейегель ✎
A gentle prince troubled by periods of erratic behaviour, Rhaegel became heir during Aerys's childless reign. He died after choking on food in 215 AC. Aelor and then Aelora inherited his place in the succession, before their own deaths moved it onward.

### aelor · Эйлор ✎
Became Prince of Dragonstone after Rhaegel's death. In 217 AC he died in an accident involving his twin and wife, Aelora. The brief surviving account does not explain the accident itself.

### aelora · Эйлора ✎
Blamed herself for the accident that killed Aelor and was overwhelmed by grief. For a time she stood as Aerys's heir. An attack at a masked ball was followed by her suicide, ending another branch's immediate claim to the throne.

### daenora · Дейенора ✎
Her marriage to Aerion produced Maegor in 232 AC. After Maekar's death, the infant's claim went before the Great Council. The lords preferred Aegon, fearing a long minority and the possibility that Maegor would inherit Aerion's cruelty.

### maekar1 · Maekar I ✎
A stern soldier, Maekar fought against Baelor in the trial of seven and probably delivered the blow that killed him. He later succeeded Aerys I and ruled for twelve years. His death during the assault on Starpike in the Peake Uprising of 233 AC left a disputed succession requiring a Great Council.

## Дети Мейкара I и Дианны Дейн

### daeron_dr · Daeron the Drunken ✎
Troubled by prophetic dreams, Daeron drank heavily to escape them. His false account of Duncan's conduct helped bring about the trial of seven at Ashford. He later married Kiera and had a daughter, Vaella, but died before Maekar and never inherited the crown.

### vaella_s · Вейелла Простушка ✎
Her position in Daeron's branch gave Vaella a possible claim after Maekar's death. The Great Council of 233 AC dismissed it, describing her as unable to understand the duties of rule. Aegon was chosen instead.

### aerion_m · Aerion Brightflame ✎
Aerion's assault on a puppeteer at Ashford brought him into conflict with Duncan and led to the trial of seven. Exile in Lys did not temper his cruelty. In 232 AC he drank wildfire, convinced it would transform him into a dragon. His death left the infant Maegor as a potential claimant.

### maegor_a · Мейгор, сын Эйриона ✎
Considered for the crown at the Great Council of 233 AC. The prospect of a prolonged regency and fear that he might resemble Aerion counted against him. The council selected Aegon V.

### aemon_m · Maester Aemon ✎
Trained at the Citadel, Aemon declined the crown offered after Maekar's death and joined the Watch to keep his name from being used against Aegon. He served for decades at Castle Black, advising Jon and Sam. During the voyage to Oldtown he died aboard the Cinnamon Wind, leaving Sam to carry his final thoughts on prophecy south.

### daella_m · Дейелла, дочь Мейкара I ✎
Married and had children. The surviving accounts do not identify her husband or trace that family further.

### daella_kids · Дети Дейеллы, дочери Мейкара I ✎
Daella, Maekar I's daughter, had children whose names and father are unknown. On his final voyage, Maester Aemon remembered Daella and Rhae singing to their children.

### aegon5 · Aegon the Unlikely ✎
Travelling as Egg with Duncan taught Aegon how ordinary people lived. Chosen by the Great Council of 233 AC, he tried to improve their position, facing sustained resistance from the nobility. His children's marriages also frustrated plans to secure powerful allies. He died at Summerhall during an attempt to hatch dragons; the surviving account leaves much of the disaster unexplained.

### rhae · Рейе ✎
As a child she put a love potion in Egg's drink, hoping he would marry her rather than Daella. She later married and had children, but the identity of her husband is not recorded.

### rhae_kids · Дети Рейе, дочери Мейкара I ✎
Rhae Targaryen's children are known through Maester Aemon's memories of his sisters singing to their families. The published books give neither their names nor their number, and do not identify their father.

## Дети Эйгона V и Беты Блэквуд

### duncan · Duncan the Small ✎
Chose Jenny of Oldstones over his arranged marriage to Lyonel Baratheon's daughter. When required to choose between Jenny and the crown, he gave up the succession. Known as the Prince of Dragonflies, he later died in the fire at Summerhall with Aegon V.

### jaehaerys2 · Jaehaerys II ✎
Married Shaera in secret, defying Aegon's planned alliances. He later insisted that Aerys and Rhaella marry after hearing a prophecy about the prince that was promised. His short reign included the War of the Ninepenny Kings, which defeated the last known male Blackfyre claimant.

### shaera · Shaera ✎
Defied her betrothal to Luthor Tyrell by secretly marrying Jaehaerys. She became queen when he inherited the throne.

### daeron_e · Дейерон, сын Эйгона V ✎
Broke his betrothal to Olenna Redwyne. He remained close to Jeremy Norridge, with whom he fought in royal campaigns. Both were killed putting down a rebellion in 251 AC.

### rhaelle · Rhaelle ✎
Sent to Storm's End after Duncan's broken betrothal, Rhaelle later married Ormund Baratheon. The match repaired the crown's relationship with the Baratheons. Her descendants included Robert, whose descent through her supplied a dynastic argument for his claim after the rebellion.

## Дети Джейехериса II и Шейры

### aerys2 · Aerys the Mad King ✎
Early promises of reform gave way to paranoia and brutality, especially after his imprisonment at Duskendale. The killing of Rickard and Brandon Stark, followed by demands for Robert and Eddard's heads, helped start the rebellion. As King's Landing fell, he ordered the wildfire caches lit. Jaime killed him before the city could be burned.

### rhaella · Rhaella ✎
Her arranged marriage brought repeated pregnancy losses and increasingly severe abuse from Aerys. Sent to Dragonstone near the end of Robert's Rebellion, she died giving birth to Daenerys in 284 AC. Viserys and the newborn escaped the island soon afterwards.

## Дети Эйериса II и Рейеллы

### rhaegar · Rhaegar ✎
A reader, harpist and student of prophecy, Rhaegar became a capable warrior despite his scholarly beginnings. His disappearance with Lyanna helped trigger the crisis that became Robert's Rebellion. Robert killed him at the Trident. The published books have not fully disclosed his relationship with Lyanna or the events behind it.

### shaena · Шейна, дочь Эйериса II ✎
Stillborn.

### daeron_a2 · Дейерон, сын Эйериса II ✎
Born in 269 AC and lived for roughly six months. Aerys responded to this and other infant deaths by looking for conspiracies and questioning Rhaella's fidelity. His accusations provide no reliable explanation of the child's death.

### stillborn_a2 · Мертворождённый ребёнок Эйериса II и Рейеллы ✎
Stillborn.

### aegon_a2 · Эйгон, сын Эйериса II ✎
Born two months prematurely in 272 AC and died the following year.

### jaehaerys_a2 · Джейехерис, сын Эйериса II ✎
Died before his first nameday.

### viserys3 · Viserys, the Beggar King ✎
Carried into exile after the dynasty fell, Viserys spent years seeking help to recover his crown and became known as the Beggar King. He arranged Daenerys's marriage to Drogo in hopes of gaining an army. When he threatened her and demanded his promised crown, Drogo killed him with molten gold.

### daenerys · Daenerys Stormborn ✎
Raised in exile, Daenerys emerged from Drogo's funeral pyre with three living dragons. In Essos she challenged the slave trade, then tried to govern Meereen amid famine, siege and resistance from its former masters. Drogon's return to the fighting pit carried her away from the city. A Dance with Dragons leaves her beside him in the Dothraki sea, facing a new khalasar.

### rhaego · Rhaego ✎
The dosh khaleen predicted that Rhaego would be the stallion who mounts the world. He was stillborn during Mirri Maz Duur's bloodmagic ritual. Daenerys believed his death paid for her attempt to save Drogo. The disturbing description of his body comes from Mirri's account, rather than Daenerys witnessing it herself.

### rhaenys_r · Рейенис, дочь Рейегара ✎
Hid beneath Rhaegar's bed during the sack of King's Landing. Amory Lorch found and killed her when she was about three. Her death, alongside those of Elia and the child believed to be Aegon, sustained the Martells' hostility towards the Lannisters.

### aegon_r · Эйгон, сын Рейегара ✎
Believed killed by Gregor Clegane during the sack, Aegon later became the claimed identity of Young Griff. Varys's account says the infant had been exchanged before the city's fall. Jon Connington and the Golden Company support the young man's return. His identity remains unproved in the published novels.
