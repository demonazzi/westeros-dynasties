## Штормовые короли и Таргариены

### bastard_bh · Bastard of Blackhaven ✎
An illegitimate son of House Dondarrion who served Argilac Durrandon, the last Storm King. He died in 2 BC at the Last Storm, when Meraxes's fire engulfed Argilac's vanguard; his name is unknown.

### harmon_d · Harmon Dondarrion ✎
Lord of Blackhaven under Aenys I. He dismissed the Vulture King's host as a rabble, but in 37 AC it took Blackhaven and the Vulture King cut off Harmon's nose. Later Harmon led the marcher lords in the Vulture Hunt that captured him.

### dnd_lord54 · Лорд Дондаррион, отец Саймона ✎
Lord of Blackhaven early in the reign of Jaehaerys I and father of Simon; his name is unknown.

### simon_d · Simon Dondarrion ✎
Lord of Blackhaven under Jaehaerys I, a celebrated knight and singer. In 54 AC his songs so charmed the king and queen that they lingered at Blackhaven longer than planned; in 55 AC he won the tourney marking the completion of the Dragonpit, and in 61 AC, during the Third Dornish War, he led marcher knights into the Red Mountains against the Vulture King.

## При Дейероне II

### jena_d · Jena Dondarrion ✎
Wife of Prince Baelor Breakspear and mother of Valarr and Matarys. How she was related to the other Dondarrions is unknown.

### dnd_lord206 · Лорд Дондаррион, отец Манфреда ✎
Lord of Blackhaven under Daeron II and father of Manfred. In 206 AC, together with Lord Caron, he drove another Vulture King out of the Red Mountains; his name is unknown.

### manfred_d · Manfred Dondarrion ✎
Son and heir of Lord Dondarrion under Daeron II; in 206 AC he fought beside his father against the Vulture King. At the Ashford tourney in 209 AC he refused to vouch for Duncan the Tall's knighthood and ignored his call for a seventh knight in the trial of seven.

## Конец третьего века

### beric · Beric Dondarrion ✎
Lord of Blackhaven, betrothed to Allyria Dayne, whose nephew Edric became his squire. After the Hand's tourney Eddard Stark sent him to bring Gregor Clegane to justice; his party was ambushed and Beric was mortally wounded, but Thoros of Myr brought him back. He led the Brotherhood Without Banners in the riverlands, died and returned several times, and in 299 AC gave his last life to bring back Catelyn Stark.
