## Пламмы при Таргариенах

### ossifer · Ossifer Plumm
An elderly, wealthy lord reportedly dead on his wedding night. Rumour attributed his posthumous heir to Aegon IV.

### viserys_pl · Viserys Plumm
Recognised as Ossifer’s heir despite rumours that Aegon IV fathered him. The circumstances invite suspicion, but no proof establishes the claim.

### plumm_sons · Сыновья Визериса Пламма
Viserys Plumm had sons whose names are unknown. Tyrion suspects that Brown Ben Plumm descends from a younger son of Viserys, which would connect him to the Targaryens. The intervening generations have not been established.

### petyr_pl · Petyr Plumm
A competitor in the Ashford tourney in 209 AC.

### maynard · Maynard Plumm
At Whitewalls he helped Dunk and showed unusual knowledge of Bloodraven’s intentions. His disappearance before the royal forces arrived, together with hints about his appearance, suggests Bloodraven in disguise. The identification is never stated outright.

## Пламмы в конце третьего века

### philip_pl · Philip Plumm
Head of House Plumm during the War of the Five Kings.

### dennis_pl · Dennis Plumm
Tywin sent him and his brothers to search the riverlands for Jaime.

### peter_pl · Peter Plumm
He joined his brothers in Tywin’s search for Jaime.

### harwyn · Harwyn Plumm
Hardstone cleared outlaws from Darry and commanded its garrison. He favoured a warhammer and spoke little.

### ben_pl · Ben Plumm
Brown Ben led the Second Sons from Daenerys’s service to Yunkai’s, fearing her defeat. Daenerys’s dragons accepted his presence unusually readily. His claim to a little dragon blood may explain it, but the proposed connection through Viserys Plumm remains uncertain.
