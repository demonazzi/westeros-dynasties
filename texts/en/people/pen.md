### robin_pn · Robin Penrose ✎
Robin was Ronnel Penrose and Elaena Targaryen's eldest child and only son. He was presumably the Penrose heir, although this has not been confirmed. His younger sisters were Laena, Jocelyn and Joy.

### laena_pn · Laena Penrose ✎
Laena was the eldest daughter of Ronnel Penrose and Elaena Targaryen, with an older brother, Robin, and two younger sisters. Her mother handled the crown's finances while Ronnel formally held the office of master of coin.

### jocelyn_pn · Jocelyn Penrose ✎
Jocelyn was Ronnel Penrose and Elaena Targaryen's second daughter, younger than Robin and Laena and older than Joy.

### joy_pn · Joy Penrose ✎
Joy was the youngest daughter of Ronnel Penrose and Elaena Targaryen, after Robin, Laena and Jocelyn.
