### gry_lord10 · Лорд Грейджой, отец Дальтона ✎

### vickon · Vickon Greyjoy ✎
The first Lord of the Iron Islands after the Hoares' fall. Aegon allowed the islanders to choose him themselves. Vickon swore allegiance, banned raids on the Seven Kingdoms and allowed the Faith of the Seven to return. He reminded discontented priests that rebellion against the dragons would destroy the islands.

### goren · Goren Greyjoy ✎
He inherited the islands in 33 AC and defeated a conspiracy by supporters of the Volmarks. He then assembled a hundred ships and crushed Lodos's rebellion. As his reward, he asked Aenys for permission to expel the septons and close the septs. The king agreed.

### dalton · Dalton Greyjoy ✎
He went raiding from childhood and came to power at fifteen. During the Dance, he supported Rhaenyra, using the war to plunder the west. He took Fair Isle and sacked Lannisport. He continued raiding after the war ended. In 133 AC his salt wife Tess slit his throat, plunging the islands into a succession struggle.

### veron · Veron Greyjoy ✎
During the capture of Fair Isle, he took one of the captive Farman women as a salt wife. He was not named among the claimants after Dalton's death. He may have died earlier, but this cannot establish a date.

### toron · Toron Greyjoy ✎
He came to power as a child after Dalton's murder. Relatives seized him and executed his mother. Opponents put forward Rodrik and the pretender Sam Salt. Pyke withstood an attack by western forces in 134 AC, and Toron retained his position.

### rodrik_d · Rodrik Greyjoy (son of Dalton) ✎
After Dalton died, he was put forward as a rival to the young Toron. He was captured during the invasion by western forces. On Johanna's orders, he was gelded and given to her son Loreon as a fool.

### alton · Alton Greyjoy ✎
Lord of the Iron Islands, called the Holy Fool. He dreamed of conquests far to the west of Lonely Light. Lists of rulers place him after Toron and before Torwyn; his exact dates are unknown.

### torwyn · Torwyn Greyjoy ✎
He made a blood pact with Aegor Rivers, then betrayed him during one of the Blackfyre rebellions. The chronicle does not specify which rebellion. The dates of his rule are unknown.

### loron · Loron Greyjoy (lord) ✎
Lord of the Iron Islands, called the Bard. His close friendship with Desmond Mallister ended tragically, but the details have not been revealed. Lists of rulers place him between Torwyn and Dagon.

### dagon · Dagon Greyjoy ✎
He resumed widespread raids on the North, the west and the Reach while the crown was occupied with the Blackfyre threat. His men plundered Fair Isle and reached the Arbor. Beron Stark and Tybolt Lannister gathered forces to resist him. The Targaryens later suppressed his attacks; the exact sequence of the final campaign is unknown.

### quellon · Quellon Greyjoy ✎
He tried to bring the islands closer to the mainland: he banned slavery, restricted raids, taxed salt wives and invited maesters. He fought in the War of the Ninepenny Kings. During Robert's Rebellion, he intervened only after Rhaegar's death and was killed in a sea battle at the Mander. Balon abandoned many of his reforms.

### harlon · Harlon Greyjoy ✎
He contracted greyscale as a child. Aeron remembered that the disease had frozen his mouth, leaving him unable to speak or call for help. He died on Pyke.

### quenton · Quenton Greyjoy ✎
He died in infancy.

### donel_g · Donel Greyjoy ✎
He died in infancy.

### balon · Balon Greyjoy ✎
He rejected Quellon's reforms and tried to restore the Old Way. His first rebellion ended in defeat and the loss of his sons; Theon became a Stark hostage. During the War of the Five Kings, he refused an alliance with Robb and instead attacked the North, again proclaiming himself king. He died in a fall from a bridge on Pyke. His death's timing, just before Euron's return, raised suspicions.

### euron · Euron Greyjoy ✎
The Crow's Eye was exiled from the islands, but returned after Balon's death and won the kingsmoot by promising conquests and dragons. He captured the Shield Islands and sent Victarion to Daenerys with a horn. In a published chapter of The Winds of Winter, he holds Aeron captive and prepares to confront the Reach's fleet. His claims of having visited Valyria are unconfirmed.

### victarion · Victarion Greyjoy ✎
Commander of the Iron Fleet. He supported Balon, then lost to Euron at the kingsmoot. Ordered to bring Daenerys back, he intended to marry her himself. During the voyage, the priest Moqorro saved his wounded hand and gained influence over him. As the fleet approaches Meereen, Victarion plans to use the horn to control dragons.

### urrigon · Urrigon Greyjoy ✎
Aeron accidentally wounded him with an axe during a game. The maester did not cauterize the wound, and it became infected; amputation failed to save him. Urrigon died at fourteen while his father and elder brothers were fighting on the mainland.

### aeron · Aeron Greyjoy ✎
After a dissolute youth, he became a stern priest of the Drowned God. Urrigon's death and Euron's abuse during childhood left lasting scars. He called the kingsmoot, hoping to prevent Euron from taking power, but failed. In a published chapter of The Winds of Winter, he is Euron's prisoner and witnesses preparations for a sacrifice.

### euron_bast · Внебрачные сыновья Эурона Грейджоя ✎
Euron acknowledged having several bastards but showed no concern for them. Their names are unknown.

### robin_g · Robin Greyjoy ✎

### quellon_dau · Мертворождённая дочь Квеллона Грейджоя ✎
She was stillborn after Quellon's death.

### alannys · Alannys Harlaw ✎
She lost her elder sons during Balon's rebellion, while Theon was taken away as a hostage. She later moved to Ten Towers. Illness and grief clouded her memory: she sometimes forgot that Rodrik and Maron were dead and waited for her children to return.

### rodrik_g · Rodrik Greyjoy ✎
He commanded a ship during Balon's rebellion. Jason Mallister killed him during the attack on Seagard.

### maron_g · Maron Greyjoy ✎
He defended Pyke against Robert's forces and died beneath a collapsing tower during the assault.

### asha · Asha Greyjoy ✎
Captain of the Black Wind, trusted by Balon. She captured Deepwood Motte, then claimed power at the kingsmoot, offering peace on terms favourable to the islands. After Euron's victory, she fled an imposed marriage. Stannis defeated her force and took her captive. During his northern campaign, she met Theon again.

### theon · Theon Greyjoy ✎
He grew up as a hostage at Winterfell. Robb sent him to propose an alliance with Balon, but Theon chose his father and seized the Starks' castle. To conceal Bran and Rickon's escape, he killed two other boys. Captured by Ramsay, he endured torture and lost his own name. He later helped Jeyne Poole escape. He came into Stannis's custody, facing judgement for his betrayal.

### quenton_c · Quenton Greyjoy (cousin) ✎
Captain of the Salty Wench. After the kingsmoot, he went with Asha to Deepwood Motte and advised joining Dagmer. During Stannis's attack, a northern mountain clansman killed him with an axe in the wolfswood.

### dagon_d · Dagon Greyjoy (Drunkard) ✎
Called the Drunkard. He left with Asha after the kingsmoot and advised joining Dagmer at Torrhen's Square. His fate after the defeat at Deepwood Motte is unknown.
