### unwin · Unwin Peake ✎
A green commander who secured control of Aegon III’s government after the Dance. He filled offices with his own allies and tried to make Myrielle queen. Aegon’s choice of Daenaera defeated that ambition; Unwin eventually resigned. Suspicion connects him with Jaehaera’s death and other crimes, but the histories do not establish his responsibility.
