### sharra · Sharra Arryn ✎
Queen regent of Mountain and Vale. She relied on the mountains and castles to defend her realm, but Visenya flew Vhagar straight to the Eyrie. Seeing Ronnel beside her, Sharra accepted Aegon's rule without a battle.

### ronnel_k · Ronnel Arryn, the King Who Flew ✎
The last King of Mountain and Vale, who accepted Aegon the Conqueror's rule while still a child. After the Eyrie's surrender, he received his promised flight on Vhagar with Visenya and later ruled as Lord Arryn. He married a daughter of Torrhen Stark. In 37 AC his brother Jonos seized the castle and killed Ronnel, his wife and their children.

### ronnel_kids · Дети Роннела Аррена ✎
They died with their parents after Jonos seized the Eyrie.

### jonos · Jonos Arryn, the Kinslayer ✎
Ronnel Arryn's younger brother, who took advantage of the unrest after Aegon I's death. He seized the Eyrie, killed his brother and his family, and proclaimed himself king. Forces loyal to the crown marched against him, while Maegor arrived on Balerion. Jonos's supporters handed him over to the victors, and he was hanged.

### hubert · Hubert Arryn ✎
He received the Eyrie after Ronnel's death and Jonos's execution. He was their cousin, though the precise degree of kinship is unknown.

### darnold · Darnold Arryn ✎
Lord of the Eyrie. He died fighting the mountain clans alongside Rymond. Ten-year-old Rodrik inherited his lands.

### rymond · Rymond Arryn ✎
He died alongside Darnold fighting the mountain clans. After their deaths, Rodrik inherited the Eyrie.

### hubert_sons · Другие сыновья Хьюберта Аррена ✎
Four of Hubert's six sons are unnamed. Darnold and Rymond probably belonged to this family, but their exact relationship is unknown.

### rodrik · Rodrik Arryn ✎
Lord of the Eyrie, which he inherited at ten after his father and uncle died. He served for many years as Jaehaerys I's master of laws. After being widowed, he married Princess Daella, who gave birth to Aemma and died soon afterwards.

### elys_a · Elys Arryn ✎
Elys was three years older than her stepmother, Daella Targaryen, and the two clashed immediately. When the pregnant princess kept bursting into tears, Elys complained about her to Queen Alysanne. Daella died giving birth to Aemma, Elys's younger half-sister.

### amanda_a · Amanda Arryn ✎
Amanda helped her stepmother Daella Targaryen correspond with Queen Alysanne, writing most of the princess's letters. Daella later died in childbirth, leaving Amanda a younger half-sister, Aemma Arryn.

### jeyne_f · Лорд Аррен, отец Джейны ✎
His place among Rodrik's descendants is not fully established. In 97 AC the Stone Crows, one of the mountain clans, killed him and his sons. The young Jeyne then inherited his lands.

### rodrik_s2 · Второй сын Родрика Аррена ✎
This branch's exact place among Rodrik Arryn's descendants is unknown.

### aemma · Aemma Arryn ✎
Rhaenyra was the only child of her marriage to Viserys to survive infancy. Several pregnancies ended in loss. In 105 AC she died giving birth to Baelon, who also died soon afterwards; Viserys subsequently named Rhaenyra his heir.

### jeyne · Jeyne Arryn, the Maiden of the Vale ✎
Lady of the Eyrie, who inherited the Vale after her father and brothers were killed. She had to defend her right to rule against relatives who considered a man the more suitable heir. During the Dance she supported Rhaenyra and gave refuge to Daemon's daughter Rhaena. After the war she served as a regent for Aegon III. Her death brought another dispute over the Vale's succession.

### jeyne_br · Братья Джейны Аррен ✎
They died with their father in an attack by the Stone Crows. Jeyne then inherited the Eyrie.

### arnold · Arnold Arryn, the Mad Heir ✎
He twice tried to remove Jeyne from power and was imprisoned. After her death, his branch of the family again contested the succession to the Eyrie.

### eldric · Eldric Arryn ✎
After Jeyne's death, he claimed the Eyrie against her chosen successor, Joffrey. Corwyn Corbray captured and executed him during the war for the Vale.

### joffrey_a · Joffrey Arryn ✎
A distant relative of Jeyne Arryn and Knight of the Bloody Gate. She named him her heir in her will, but other branches of the family refused to accept her choice. After her death, he had to fight for the Eyrie. The crown supported his claim, but securing recognition as lord required military force.

### isembard · Isembard Arryn ✎
A wealthy Arryn of Gulltown. After Jeyne's death, he claimed the Eyrie against Joffrey and Arnold's supporters.

### isembard_sons · Сыновья Изембарда Аррена ✎
They were imprisoned with Isembard during the struggle for power in the Vale. Their names are unknown.

### donnel · Donnel Arryn ✎
Lord of the Eyrie under Daeron II. During the First Blackfyre Rebellion, he commanded the royal army's vanguard at the Redgrass Field; Gwayne Corbray's reinforcements helped save him from defeat. In 209 AC he closed the passes and sea routes into the Vale to protect it from the Great Spring Sickness. His measures kept the epidemic out of the region.

### alys_r · Alys Arryn ✎
Her marriage to Rhaegel placed her family in the royal succession. She bore the twins Aelor and Aelora and a younger daughter, Daenora. After Rhaegel's death, her children stood in the line of succession to Aerys I. Through Daenora, Alys was the grandmother of Maegor, the infant whose claim the Great Council of 233 AC rejected.

### jasper · Jasper Arryn ✎
Lord of the Eyrie.

### jon_a · Jon Arryn ✎
Lord of the Eyrie, who fostered Eddard Stark and Robert Baratheon. He refused to surrender them to Aerys II and called the Vale to arms, becoming one of the rebellion's principal leaders. After victory he served as Robert's Hand and investigated the parentage of Cersei's children with Stannis. His wife Lysa poisoned him at Petyr Baelish's urging.

### ronnel_j · Ronnel Arryn ✎
Keeper of the Gates of the Moon. He died of a sickness of the belly before Robert's Rebellion. His son Elbert became Jon Arryn's heir but was executed by Aerys II.

### alys_j · Alys Arryn ✎
Sister of Jon and Ronnel Arryn, and wife of Ser Elys Waynwood. She bore a son and eight daughters, but most of their lines died out or could not provide an heir to the Eyrie. Her youngest daughter became Harrold Hardyng's mother. This connection made Harrold next in line after Robert Arryn.

### jon_a_dau · Мертворождённая дочь Джона Аррена ✎
She was stillborn; Jeyne Royce died giving birth to her.

### jon_a_kids · Мертворождённые дети Джона Аррена и Лизы Талли ✎
Both children were stillborn. Lysa also suffered five miscarriages before giving birth to Robert.

### robert_a · Robert Arryn, Sweetrobin ✎
He inherited the Eyrie after his father's death but remained a sickly child dependent on the adults around him. After his mother was killed, he came under Petyr Baelish's control. In the books he is called Robert; the television series changes his name to Robin.

### elbert · Elbert Arryn ✎
Ronnel Arryn's son and his uncle Jon's heir before Robert was born. He travelled to King's Landing with Brandon Stark, who demanded answers about Lyanna's disappearance. Aerys II ordered Brandon's companions executed. His death removed Jon's heir on the eve of the rebellion.

### jasper_w · Jasper Waynwood ✎
He died at three. After the other branches failed, the claim to the Eyrie passed to his youngest sister's descendant, Harrold Hardyng.

### waynwood_e · Старшая дочь Алис Аррен ✎
After Denys died at the Battle of the Bells, she also lost her young son. This branch left no heir to the Eyrie.

### waynwood_m · Шесть средних дочерей Алис Аррен ✎
Two died, one became a septa, another a silent sister, one remained childless, and another was carried off by clansmen. The claim to the Eyrie therefore passed to their youngest sister's descendant, Harrold Hardyng.

### waynwood_y · Младшая дочь Алис Аррен ✎
Her marriage to a Hardyng gave Harrold a claim to the Eyrie after the elder Arryn lines failed.

### denys · Denys Arryn ✎
A distant relative of Jon Arryn and a celebrated knight, married to one of Alys Arryn's daughters. He was considered a possible heir after Elbert's death. During Robert's Rebellion, Jon Connington killed him at the Battle of the Bells. His young son died soon afterwards, ending this line of succession.

### denys_son · Сын Дениса Аррена ✎
He died soon after Denys was killed at the Battle of the Bells. His death ended this branch of claimants to the Eyrie.

### harrold · Harrold Hardyng, Harry the Heir ✎
His descent from Alys Arryn made him next in line to the Eyrie after Robert. He was raised by Anya Waynwood and knighted. Petyr Baelish intended to use a possible marriage between him and Sansa to advance his plans in the Vale and the North.
