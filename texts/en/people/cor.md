## Корбреи при Таргариенах

### gawen · Gawen Corbray
Chosen by Visenya to train Maegor on Dragonstone, Gawen had a reputation as one of the realm's most dangerous knights. The young prince took readily to swordplay under his instruction.

### qarl · Qarl Corbray
Qarl backed Aegon the Uncrowned against Maegor and survived the defeat at the Gods Eye. Jaehaerys later put him in command of the capital's City Watch. When Rogar Baratheon resisted Alyssa Velaryon's dismissal, Qarl drew Lady Forlorn in her support. He died during the Shivers in 59 AC.

### cor_lord9 · Лорд Корбрей, отец Леовина и Корвина
The unnamed Lord of Heart's Home and father of Leowyn and Corwyn Corbray. Impressed by his younger son's prowess, he gave Corwyn the family's Valyrian steel sword, Lady Forlorn.

### leowyn · Leowyn Corbray
Leowyn helped lead the Vale's army south at the end of the Dance. Under Aegon III, he became Protector of the Realm, though Tyland Lannister soon held greater influence at court. The Winter Fever killed him in the Red Keep in 133 AC.

### quenton_cb · Quenton Corbray
After succeeding Leowyn at Heart's Home, Quenton backed Joffrey Arryn in the dispute over the Eyrie. The killing of his uncle Corwyn at Runestone committed the Corbrays to that cause. He attended the gathering to choose new royal regents in 136 AC.

### corwyn_c · Corwyn Corbray
A renowned swordsman who carried Lady Forlorn, Corwyn helped command the Vale's forces for the blacks. After the war he married Rhaena Targaryen and joined Aegon III's council of regents. Sent to settle the Arryn succession, he upheld Jeyne's chosen heir, Joffrey. In 135 AC a crossbowman killed him outside Runestone, where Arnold Arryn had taken refuge.

### corwyn_daus · Дочери Корвина Корбрея
Corwyn Corbray's two daughters from his first marriage. Their mother died before the Dance of the Dragons, and their father later married Rhaena Targaryen. Neither daughter's name or later life is recorded.

### gwayne_c · Gwayne Corbray
At the Redgrass Field, Gwayne fought Daemon Blackfyre with Lady Forlorn for almost an hour. Daemon broke through his helm, leaving him blinded and badly wounded, then stopped to arrange his treatment. That delay gave Bloodraven's archers time to take position. Gwayne's service in Daeron II's Kingsguard was recorded in the White Book.

## Дом Сердец в конце третьего века

### cor_lord17 · Лорд Корбрей, отец Лионеля
Wounded at the Trident, Lord Corbray was carried to the maesters by Lyonel while Lyn took up Lady Forlorn and led a charge against the Dornishmen. On his death, the lord left the sword to Lyn and the lands and title to Lyonel, creating a lasting grievance between them.

### lyonel_c · Lyonel Corbray
Lyonel inherited Heart's Home but resented losing Lady Forlorn to Lyn. His first child died in infancy, leaving his brother as heir. Littlefinger arranged his second marriage to a wealthy Gulltown merchant's daughter; her dowry eased the family's poverty. Lyonel stayed out of the Lords Declarant's opposition to Baelish. In the published Winds of Winter sample, he remained at home with his pregnant wife.

### lyonel_baby · Ребёнок Лионеля Корбрея
The sickly child died in infancy. Lyn consequently remained heir to Heart's Home until Lyonel's second marriage offered the prospect of another child.

### lyn · Lyn Corbray
Lyn carried Lady Forlorn at the Trident, killed the wounded Lewyn Martell and received the sword when his father died. A reckless and dangerous swordsman, he later joined the Lords Declarant and threatened Littlefinger during their negotiations. Baelish told Sansa that Lyn was already in his pay, making his apparent hostility useful for undermining the other lords.

### lucas_c · Lucas Corbray
A knight of House Corbray named in the appendices of A Feast for Crows and A Dance with Dragons.
