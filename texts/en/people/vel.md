### valaena · Valaena Velaryon
Lady of Dragonstone before the Conquest, Valaena married Aerion Targaryen and bore Visenya, Aegon, and Rhaenys. Her own mother was a Targaryen. Her Velaryon ancestry later made Aenys's marriage to Alyssa a union between cousins.

### daemon_v0 · Daemon Velaryon
Lord of Driftmark and Aegon the Conqueror's admiral. He supported the Targaryens during their invasion of Westeros and commanded their fleet. He died in the naval battle off Gulltown, where the Vale's forces defeated the invaders' ships. His son Aethan inherited his lands and the office of master of ships.

### aethan · Aethan Velaryon
He inherited Driftmark and command of the fleet after Daemon's death. He served as master of ships under Aegon I and his successors, and fought the pirate Sargoso Saan. His daughter Alyssa married Aenys I and became queen.

### corlys_kg · Ser Corlys Velaryon
The first Lord Commander of the Kingsguard, founded under Aegon the Conqueror.

### alyssa_v · Alyssa Velaryon
Aenys I's queen survived him and Maegor's persecution. After her husband's death, she protected her family from Maegor, then became regent for the young Jaehaerys after Maegor died. She later married Rogar Baratheon and bore Boremund and Jocelyn. She died after Jocelyn's difficult birth in 54 AC.

### daemon_v · Daemon Velaryon
Lord of Driftmark and commander of the royal fleet. He initially served Maegor but later abandoned him for Jaehaerys. Under the new king, he was master of ships and briefly Hand of the King. His eldest son Corwyn died before him, so Driftmark passed to his grandson Corlys, the Sea Snake.

### aethan_sons · Другие сыновья Этана Велариона
This branch's exact place in the Velaryon genealogy is unknown.

### lianna · Lianna Velaryon
Rhaena Targaryen's companion on Dragonstone. Androw Farman poisoned her along with other women in his wife's household.

### larissa · Larissa Velaryon
Rhaena Targaryen's first close friend was recalled to Driftmark to marry the Evenstar's second son. Widowed by 55 AC, Larissa lived with her daughter at Greenstone. Rhaena spent about a year there after the murder of her companions and Aerea's disappearance.

### corwyn · Corwyn Velaryon
He died before Lord Daemon, so Driftmark passed directly to Corlys.

### jorgen · Jorgen Velaryon
Jorgen accompanied Jaehaerys and Alysanne around Driftmark's shipyards in 50 AC alongside his brothers Corwyn and Victor. He may have been Daemon Velaryon's second son, who died of the Shivers in 59 AC, but the dead son's name is not given.

### victor · Victor Velaryon
Victor and his brothers Jorgen and Corwyn escorted Jaehaerys and Alysanne on their visit to Driftmark's shipyards in 50 AC. He was one of Lord Daemon Velaryon's sons.

### daemon_v_daus · Дочери Деймона Велариона
Three died of the Shivers in 59 AC.

### corlys_br1 · Старший брат Корлиса Велариона
Corlys Velaryon's unnamed brother was Vaemond's father. He was the elder of Corlys's two brothers; the younger fathered the silent five. His age relative to Corlys himself is not established.

### corlys · Corlys Velaryon, the Sea Snake
The Lord of Driftmark, renowned navigator and husband of Rhaenys Targaryen. Nine great voyages brought wealth and the name Sea Snake; he built High Tide and made his house one of the realm's most influential. He supported Rhaenyra in the Dance, but their relationship deteriorated when Addam was accused of treason. Corlys survived the war and joined Aegon III's regency council.

### corlys_br2 · Другой брат Корлиса Велариона
This branch's exact relationship to the Velaryon "silent five" is unknown.

### vaemond · Vaemond Velaryon
He contested Lucerys's inheritance of Driftmark, declaring Rhaenyra's sons bastards. The queen ordered his execution; Daemon cut off his head, and his body was fed to Syrax.

### daemion_v · Daemion Velaryon
After Corlys's death in 132 AC, Daemion and his brother Daeron challenged Alyn's succession to Driftmark. The regents rejected their claims. The brothers accepted the ruling and received land from Alyn in return for contributing ships to his fleet.

### daeron_v · Daeron Velaryon
He fought in the Stepstones with Alyn Oakenfist's forces and died in a naval battle.

### daenaera · Daenaera Velaryon
She lost her parents young and was raised by Baela and Alyn. At the Maiden's Day Ball, Aegon III chose her as his second wife.

### malentine · Malentine Velaryon
Viserys had his tongue cut out for accusing Rhaenyra's sons of bastardy. After the Dance, he joined a plot against Alyn Velaryon, disputing his right to Driftmark. He died after the conspiracy failed.

### rhogar_v · Rhogar Velaryon
Viserys ordered his tongue cut out for accusing Rhaenyra's sons of bastardy. He later joined a conspiracy against Alyn Velaryon and was captured.

### silent3 · Трое безымянных из «молчаливой пятёрки»
Three other relatives among the Velaryon "silent five". Alongside Malentine and Rhogar, they repeated the accusation that Rhaenyra's children were not Laenor's sons. Viserys I had their tongues removed.

### laena · Laena Velaryon
Vhagar's rider. After a proposed marriage to Viserys I came to nothing, she became Daemon's second wife and travelled with him through the Free Cities. She bore the twins Baela and Rhaena. In the books, she died of childbed fever after giving birth to a son, trying to reach her dragon before she died.

### laenor · Laenor Velaryon
Seasmoke's rider. At the Great Council of 101 AC, he represented the principal claim of his mother's branch but lost to Viserys. He married Rhaenyra and was officially the father of Jacaerys, Lucerys and Joffrey, although their parentage was questioned. In the books, Qarl Correy killed him; Daemon's possible involvement remained an allegation.

### alyn · Alyn Velaryon, Oakenfist
Marilda of Hull's son, whom she named as Laenor's child, while others believed Corlys was his father. He was legitimized as a Velaryon and inherited Driftmark after his brother Addam died. His naval campaigns earned him the name Oakenfist. He married Baela Targaryen and later fathered Elaena's twins. He disappeared on a voyage; the exact circumstances of his death are unknown.

### addam · Addam Velaryon
Alyn's elder brother from Hull, who claimed Seasmoke during the search for new dragonriders. Rhaenyra legitimized him, and Corlys made him heir to Driftmark. When the queen suspected the dragonseeds of treason, Addam fled but continued fighting for her. He died at the Second Battle of Tumbleton. The word "Loyal" was later inscribed on his tomb.

### laena_a · Laena Velaryon
She was given a dragon egg as a child. It hatched into a deformed creature that attacked her and was killed.

### alyn_kids · Другие дети Элина Велариона и Бейлы
Alyn Velaryon and Baela Targaryen had children besides Laena, but their names and number are unknown. The ancestry of the later Velaryons has not been traced to any particular child of this marriage.

### jon_w · Jon Waters
He became a renowned knight. His legitimate son added "Long" to the surname Waters, founding House Longwaters.

### jeyne_w · Jeyne Waters
Jeyne was one of the twins born from Elaena Targaryen's affair with Alyn Oakenfist. Her brother Jon became a knight and his descendants took the name Longwaters. Jeyne's own later life is unrecorded.

### longwaters · Сын Джона Уотерса, основатель дома Лонгвотерс
He added "Long" to his father's surname to emphasize that he had been born in wedlock, founding House Longwaters. A later descendant, Rennifer Longwaters, served as chief undergaoler of the Red Keep.

### lucerys_v · Lucerys Velaryon
Lord of Driftmark and master of ships under Aerys II. At court he supported the king in his rivalry with his heir, Rhaegar.

### monford · Monford Velaryon
Lord of Driftmark during the War of the Five Kings. He supported Stannis Baratheon and brought his ships to King's Landing. He died on the Blackwater when wildfire destroyed his ship, the Pride of Driftmark. His young son Monterys inherited his lands.

### aurane · Aurane Waters
He fought for Stannis on the Blackwater and was captured. After swearing allegiance to Joffrey, he was appointed master of ships by Cersei. When she was arrested, he sailed away with the new royal fleet. Reports from the Stepstones later described a pirate with similar ships who called himself the Lord of the Waters.

### monterys · Monterys Velaryon
He inherited Driftmark at about six after Monford died on the Blackwater.
