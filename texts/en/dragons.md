## Откуда драконы (ветви)

### valyria · Валирия ✎
Twelve years before the Doom, Aenar the Exile brought five dragons to Dragonstone. Only Balerion survived to the Conquest.

### eggs · Яйца драконов Эйнара ✎
The other four Valyrian dragons died on Dragonstone, leaving clutches of eggs. Vhagar and Meraxes hatched from these eggs.

### mont · Драконья Гора ✎
Dragons hatched on Dragonstone. The books do not identify which dragons laid their eggs.

### cradle · Яйца в колыбелях ✎
The Targaryens placed a dragon egg in a newborn child's cradle. If it hatched, the dragon grew up alongside the child.

### wild · Дикие драконы ✎
The riderless dragons of the Dragonmont: two were never claimed, while Nettles tamed the third.

### essos · Окаменевшие яйца ✎
Three eggs given to Daenerys as a wedding gift by Magister Illyrio Mopatis. They hatched in Khal Drogo's funeral pyre.

## Валирия

### balerion · Balerion the Black Dread ✎
The Black Dread, the last known dragon born in Valyria before the Doom. Aegon rode him when he burned Harrenhal and conquered most of Westeros. Maegor, Aerea and Viserys later claimed him. He returned badly wounded from a mysterious journey with Aerea; Barth suspected they had visited ruined Valyria. He died of old age in 94 AC.

## Яйца драконов Эйнара

### vhagar · Vhagar ✎
Visenya's dragon, who survived the Conquest and several generations of Targaryens. Her later riders were Baelon the Brave, Laena Velaryon and Aemond. By the Dance, she was the largest living dragon and the greens' chief advantage. She killed Arrax and helped bring down Meleys. Above the Gods Eye, she grappled with Caraxes and fell into the lake with him and both riders.

### meraxes · Meraxes ✎
Rhaenys's dragon, who fought in the Conquest, including the defeat of the Storm King and the Field of Fire. During her lifetime she was larger than Vhagar, though smaller than Balerion. In 10 AC a Dornish scorpion bolt struck her eye above Hellholt. Her fall destroyed part of the castle. Her skull was later brought to Aegon during peace negotiations; Rhaenys's fate after the fall remained uncertain.

## Драконья Гора

### quicksilver · Quicksilver ✎
Aenys I's dragon, bonded to him from childhood. After the king died, she accepted Aegon the Uncrowned. He rode her against Maegor in an attempt to reclaim the throne. At the Battle Beneath the Gods Eye, the much larger Balerion killed Quicksilver and her rider.

### dreamfyre · Dreamfyre ✎
A pale blue dragon with silver markings. Her first rider was Rhaena, Aenys I's daughter; her second was Helaena, Aegon II's wife. During the storming of the Dragonpit, she was wounded in the eye, broke her chains and brought down the dome, dying beneath it. A connection between the eggs Elissa Farman stole and Daenerys's dragons remains a theory. This ancestry has never been directly confirmed.

### meleys · Meleys, the Red Queen ✎
The Red Queen, a scarlet dragon ridden first by Alyssa and then by Rhaenys Targaryen. In her youth she was considered one of the fastest dragons. At Rook's Rest, Rhaenys was ambushed and fought Sunfyre and Vhagar. Meleys badly wounded Sunfyre, but all three dragons crashed to the ground, and she died. Her head was paraded through the capital as proof of the greens' victory.

### caraxes · Caraxes, the Blood Wyrm ✎
The Blood Wyrm, a red dragon ridden by Aemon and later Daemon. He fought in the Stepstones and for Rhaenyra during the Dance of the Dragons. Above the Gods Eye, he seized Vhagar by the throat. Both fell into the lake. With a wing torn off and his belly ripped open, Caraxes crawled ashore near Harrenhal and died.

### syrax · Syrax ✎
Rhaenyra's yellow dragon, named after a Valyrian goddess. Well fed and less accustomed to battle than Caraxes, she was nevertheless a large and dangerous dragon. Morning hatched from one of her eggs; her presumed mating with Caraxes does not establish the other dragons' full ancestry. During the riots in the capital, she threw off Joffrey, who had tried to ride her, and died fighting the crowd near the Dragonpit. Accounts of who killed her conflict.

### morning · Morning ✎
A pink dragon with black horns, hatched in the Vale from one of Syrax's eggs near the end of the Dance. She accepted Rhaena, Daemon and Laena's daughter, and was among the few dragons to survive the war. At first she was too small to carry a rider, but Rhaena was later able to ride her. The published chronicle gives no exact date or circumstances for Morning's death.

### seasmoke · Seasmoke ✎
Laenor Velaryon's silver-grey dragon. He remained riderless for years after Laenor's death, until Addam of Hull claimed him during the Dance. Addam continued fighting for Rhaenyra even after she accused him of treason. At the Second Battle of Tumbleton, Seasmoke fought Tessarion and Vermithor and was killed when Vermithor tore off his head.

### sunfyre · Sunfyre the Golden ✎
Aegon II's golden dragon, with pink wing membranes, considered exceptionally beautiful. Badly wounded at Rook's Rest, he struggled to fly for a long time but reached Dragonstone. He killed Grey Ghost and then Moondancer, suffering further severe injuries. He killed Rhaenyra on Aegon's orders. He died from his wounds, leaving the king without a dragon.

### tessarion · Tessarion, the Blue Queen ✎
The Blue Queen, Daeron the Daring's dragon, with blue scales and copper markings. She helped the Hightower army win at the Honeywine. At the Second Battle of Tumbleton, she fought Seasmoke and Vermithor after her rider had died. Mortally wounded, she repeatedly tried to take flight. On Benjicot Blackwood's orders, the archer Billy Burley ended her suffering with arrows to the eye.

### vermax · Vermax ✎
Jacaerys Velaryon's dragon, hatched from an egg placed in the prince's cradle. He carried his rider on diplomatic journeys that helped Rhaenyra gather allies. At the Battle of the Gullet, he attacked the Triarchy's ships and died above the sea. The chronicles offered different explanations for his fall, including flying too low and being struck by a ship's weapon. Jacaerys briefly survived the fall but was killed in the water.

### arrax · Arrax ✎
Lucerys's young dragon. He carried him to Storm's End with Rhaenyra's message. On the return journey, in a storm above Shipbreaker Bay, the far larger Vhagar overtook him. He died with his rider.

### tyraxes · Tyraxes ✎
Joffrey Velaryon's dragon, the youngest of the dragons belonging to Rhaenyra's three eldest sons. He was still growing at the start of the Dance and remained smaller than Vermax and Arrax. During the riots in the capital, he was chained inside the Dragonpit. He killed attackers until he became entangled and could no longer defend himself. Joffrey's attempt to reach him on Syrax ended in the prince's death.

### stormcloud · Stormcloud ✎
The young dragon of Aegon, the future Aegon III. When the Triarchy attacked the ship carrying the princes, he took flight with his rider for the first time. Despite arrows and a scorpion bolt in his neck, he brought Aegon to Dragonstone. He died of his wounds soon afterwards. It was Aegon's only flight on his own dragon.

### moondancer · Moondancer ✎
Baela Targaryen's small, pale green dragon. Much smaller than Sunfyre, she was fast and agile. During the struggle for Dragonstone, Baela rode her into battle against Aegon II. Moondancer died after they fell to the ground, while Baela survived. The wounds she inflicted on Sunfyre eventually proved fatal.

### shrykos · Shrykos ✎
A young dragon bonded to Prince Jaehaerys, the son of Aegon II and Helaena. Blood and Cheese killed the prince before he could become a dragonrider. Shrykos remained chained in the Dragonpit and burned many attackers during its storming. Hobb the Hewer managed to reach her with an axe and killed her.

### morghul · Morghul ✎
The young dragon of Princess Jaehaera, Aegon II and Helaena's daughter. During the Dance he was kept in the Dragonpit; the princess herself was too young to fly into battle. When the crowd broke in, his chains restricted his movement. He was killed by the attackers; the chronicle named his killer as a man known as the Burning Knight.

## Яйца в колыбелях: Джейехерис и Алисанна

### vermithor · Vermithor, the Bronze Fury ✎
The Bronze Fury, Jaehaerys I's dragon, second only to Vhagar in size by the Dance. After the king died, he remained riderless for about a quarter of a century. Hugh Hammer claimed him during the search for dragonseeds and later betrayed Rhaenyra. At the Second Battle of Tumbleton, Vermithor lost his rider, killed Seasmoke and died from wounds suffered while fighting several dragons.

### silverwing · Silverwing ✎
Good Queen Alysanne's dragon and Vermithor's companion. During Alysanne's northern journey, she refused to cross the Wall; the reason is unknown. After the queen died, Ulf White claimed her and betrayed Rhaenyra at Tumbleton. Silverwing survived the Dance. After Ulf's death, she became wild and settled near Red Lake. She was one of four dragons still alive at the end of the war.

## Дикие драконы

### cannibal · Cannibal ✎
A large, black wild dragon of Dragonstone who ate dragon eggs, hatchlings and the remains of other dragons. Attempts to tame him failed. Locals claimed he had lived on the island before the Targaryens arrived, but the tale does not establish his age. He survived the Dance and later disappeared from reliable accounts; the time and place of his death are unknown.

### grey_ghost · Grey Ghost ✎
A shy wild dragon who avoided people and fed mainly on fish. He lived in an inaccessible crevice of the Dragonmont. Would-be riders failed to find and claim him. Sunfyre killed him during the Dance, though the Cannibal was initially suspected.

### sheepstealer · Sheepstealer ✎
A brown wild dragon of Dragonstone, named for his habit of hunting sheep. Nettles gradually won his trust by regularly leaving food for him. They fought for Rhaenyra until the queen declared Nettles a traitor. She flew away on him; later sightings linked them to the Mountains of the Moon. No reliable date for Sheepstealer's death is known.

## Драконы Дейенерис

### drogon · Drogon ✎
A black dragon with red markings, named by Daenerys after Drogo. He was the largest and most wilful of the three dragons hatched in her pyre. He helped destroy the Undying in Qarth and took part in freeing slaves. In Meereen, he returned to the fighting pit, from which Daenerys flew away on him. At the end of A Dance with Dragons, they are in the Dothraki sea.

### rhaegal · Rhaegal ✎
A green dragon with bronze markings, named after Rhaegar. Daenerys confined him and Viserion beneath a pyramid in Meereen after her dragons became a danger to the city's children. Quentyn's attempt to claim them opened a way out. Rhaegal escaped and burned the prince, who died from his wounds. He remains without an established rider in the published novels.

### viserion · Viserion ✎
A cream-coloured dragon with gold markings, named after Viserys. Daenerys confined him and Rhaegal beneath a pyramid while struggling to keep the city safe. Both escaped during Quentyn's attempt to tame them. Viserion later made his lair in one of Meereen's pyramids.
