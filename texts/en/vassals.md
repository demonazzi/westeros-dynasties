## Север

### stark · House Stark ✎
An ancient First Men house, the Starks ruled the North from Winterfell long before the Targaryens arrived. Tradition names Brandon the Builder as their founder. Their Kings of Winter subdued neighbouring kingdoms, including the Neck, and overcame the Boltons. They kept the old gods, supported the Night's Watch and sheltered the Manderlys after their exile from the Reach. During the Conquest, Torrhen marched south with thirty thousand men but knelt to Aegon after hearing of Harrenhal and the Field of Fire.

The North kept its rulers within the new realm. Cregan supported Rhaenyra in the Dance, then briefly served as Hand and judged those involved in Aegon II's murder. Aerys II's execution of Rickard and Brandon drew the house into Robert's Rebellion. Eddard's arrest brought the northern army south; after his execution, Robb became King in the North and of the Trident. The Red Wedding destroyed his army and enabled the Boltons to take power.

Bran, Rickon, Sansa and Arya survived, scattered from Skagos to Braavos. Some northern lords seek a Stark restoration. Wyman Manderly sends Davos to find Rickon; Robb's will also leaves the succession unresolved.

### ironsmith · House Ironsmith ✎
A northern house known chiefly from heraldic material. No member has appeared in the published novels, and little is recorded about its lands or history.

### umber · House Umber ✎
The Umbers hold the Last Hearth, close enough to the Wall for wildling raids to remain a regular danger. Once kings in their own right, they became Stark bannermen and helped the Kings of Winter defeat Gendel and Gorne. Harmond Umber later fought Raymun Redbeard at Long Lake.

In the War of the Five Kings, the Greatjon became one of Robb Stark's strongest supporters and proposed restoring the northern crown. The Freys tried to incapacitate him with drink at the Red Wedding, but eight men were needed to subdue him. He survived as a prisoner; the Smalljon died protecting Robb.

With their lord captive, the remaining Umbers divided their strength. Hother took older men to the Boltons, while Mors brought younger men to Stannis. Mors wanted revenge for the Starks but opposed settling wildlings south of the Wall. His men laid traps outside Winterfell and received the fleeing Theon Greyjoy and Jeyne Poole.

### burley · House Burley ✎
One of the northern mountain clans. Its chief is normally called simply "the Burley"; the title of lord is chiefly used when dealing with the Starks.

### blackmyre · House Blackmyre ✎
A crannogman house of the Neck, sworn to the Reeds. The published novels name the family but introduce no individual Blackmyres.

### boggs_north · House Boggs ✎
The Boggs of the Neck are crannogmen sworn to House Reed. Their seat and their part in the region's wars have not been described.

### bolton · House Bolton ✎
The Boltons once ruled the eastern North from the Dreadfort as the Red Kings. They rivalled the Starks for centuries; two Bolton kings burned Winterfell. Flaying captured enemies gave the house its feared reputation and its sigil. Submission to Winterfell did not end the rivalry: the Boltons later rebelled with the Greystarks, surviving the defeat that destroyed their allies.

Roose fought for Eddard in Robert's Rebellion and later commanded Robb Stark's infantry. He gradually sacrificed other northern contingents while preserving his own, then joined the Freys and Lannisters in planning the Red Wedding. His murder of Robb earned him the wardenship of the North. Ramsay had already seized Hornwood and burned Winterfell.

Ramsay's marriage to a girl presented as Arya Stark was intended to strengthen the family's new position. In fact she was Jeyne Poole. Frey troops, hostages and uneasy northern alliances supported Bolton rule, but the gathered army at Winterfell was divided. Jeyne's escape, Stannis's advance and mounting violence inside the castle threatened to undo it.

### bolton_of_winterfell · House Bolton of Winterfell ✎
Ramsay Bolton's branch claimed Hornwood and Winterfell. His marriage to a girl presented as Arya Stark was meant to give his rule a Stark connection. The bride was actually Jeyne Poole; her escape deprived him of the person on whom that claim depended.

### bole · House Bole ✎
A woodland house sworn to the Glovers. After Stannis recovered Deepwood Motte, Sybelle Glover provided him with Bole scouts familiar with the surrounding forests.

### branch · House Branch ✎
Glover bannermen whose scouts joined Stannis's army after he restored Deepwood Motte to its owners.

### woods · House Woods ✎
Sworn to the Glovers, the Woods provide guides and scouts in the northern forests. Ned Woods accompanies Stannis's army on its march towards Winterfell.

### wull · House Wull ✎
The largest of the northern mountain clans, the Wulls have often suffered from ironborn raids along the Bay of Ice. Theo Wull died with Eddard Stark's companions at the tower of joy. Hugo Wull later led his clansmen into Stannis's campaign against Winterfell, seeking vengeance for the Starks.

### woolfield · House Woolfield ✎
A northern house best known through Leona Woolfield, wife of Wylis Manderly. The books have not named its seat.

### glover · House Glover ✎
The Glovers are masters of Deepwood Motte, a wooden stronghold in the wolfswood. Their ruler is styled master rather than lord; the Boles, Branches and Woods are among their bannermen. Bartogan Glover accompanied Cregan Stark to King's Landing after the Dance of the Dragons.

Galbart and Robett supported Robb in the War of the Five Kings. Galbart went into the Neck with Maege Mormont to seek Howland Reed before the Red Wedding. Robett was captured after the northern defeat at Duskendale and later exchanged. Meanwhile, Asha Greyjoy seized Deepwood Motte, and Sybelle Glover's children were carried away as hostages.

Stannis retook the castle and restored it to the family. Sybelle supplied food, scouts and local support in return. At White Harbor, Robett helped Wyman Manderly plan a Stark restoration and confirmed that Rickon had survived. Galbart's whereabouts remained unknown, and recovering Deepwood did not bring the captive children home.

### greengood · House Greengood ✎
A crannogman house of the Neck, sworn to the Reeds. No separate family history has been developed in the published novels.

### dustin · House Dustin ✎
The Dustins hold Barrowton and trace their ancestry to the Barrow Kings. Their lands occupy the fertile southwestern North, where ancient barrows preserve the memory of that kingdom. During the Dance, Roderick Dustin led the Winter Wolves for Rhaenyra. He died at Tumbleton after killing Ormund and Bryndon Hightower.

Willam Dustin accompanied Eddard Stark to the tower of joy and never returned. His widow Barbrey now rules the house. She resents Eddard for leaving her husband's bones in Dorne and Catelyn for receiving the marriage she once hoped for herself.

Barbrey supports Roose Bolton and brings men to Winterfell, but suspects Ramsay of killing Domeric and refuses to admit him to Barrow Hall. Her grievances have drawn her into the Bolton alliance without making her blind to its dangers, particularly the hostility between the northern lords and the Freys.

### karstark · House Karstark ✎
The Karstarks descend from Karlon Stark, rewarded with eastern lands for putting down a rebellion. His castle's name became Karhold, and his descendants formed a separate house while retaining the old gods and their Stark kinship.

Rickard Karstark brought a substantial force to Robb's war. Jaime Lannister killed two of his sons, and Catelyn's release of Jaime drove Rickard to murder the captive Willem Lannister and Tion Frey. Robb executed him; the Karstark soldiers deserted. With the heir Harrion imprisoned, Arnolf took control at Karhold. He professed loyalty to Stannis while conspiring with the Boltons to dispose of Harrion and marry Alys to his own son.

Alys escaped to Jon Snow. Her marriage to Sigorn, Magnar of Thenn, founded a new house with a claim to Karhold and frustrated Arnolf's marriage scheme. In a published chapter of The Winds of Winter, Stannis exposes the treachery and seizes Arnolf and his closest supporters. Harrion's fate and the northern war leave the inheritance unsettled.

### cassel · House Cassel ✎
A small house in Winterfell's service. Jory commanded Eddard Stark's guards and died fighting Jaime Lannister's men. Rodrik trained the Stark children and later governed Winterfell; Ramsay killed him while he was attempting to recover the castle from Theon Greyjoy.

### quagg · House Quagg ✎
A crannogman house sworn to the Reeds. Its history beyond the Neck has not been described in the books.

### condon · House Condon ✎
Cerwyn bannermen. Ser Kyle Condon served as one of Medger Cerwyn's trusted companions and fought in the northern army during the War of the Five Kings.

### crowl · House Crowl ✎
One of the three principal houses of Skagos. The Crowls rule from Deepdown, far from the main campaigns of the northern war.

### cray · House Cray ✎
Crannogmen of the Neck, sworn to the Reeds. The family is mentioned in the novels without a detailed account of individual members.

### lightfoot · House Lightfoot ✎
A little-known northern house. Its seat and family history have not been described in the published books.

### lake_north · House Lake ✎
A minor northern house whose lands and involvement in the region's wars remain largely unrecorded.

### liddle · House Liddle ✎
A mountain clan loyal to the Starks. Its men join Stannis's northern campaign. A Liddle encountered by Bran's party in the mountains shares his food with them and keeps their presence secret.

### locke · House Locke ✎
The Lockes hold Oldcastle and once possessed the Wolf's Den, later incorporated into White Harbor. The family lost men in the fighting on the Trident during the War of the Five Kings.

### long · House Long ✎
A northern family about which little is recorded. Gareth Long served as Unwin Peake's master-at-arms during Aegon III's reign.

### manderly · House Manderly ✎
White Harbor makes the Manderlys the North's wealthiest house: it is the region's only city and its chief trading port. Driven from the Reach, they received lands at the mouth of the White Knife from the Starks. They retained the Faith of the Seven and southern knightly customs while serving Winterfell for centuries. Manderly knights fought for Rhaenyra in the Dance; Torrhen later became Aegon III's Hand.

Wyman supported Robb, proposed building a fleet and sent both his adult sons to war. Wendel died at the Red Wedding, while Wylis remained a Lannister prisoner. Wyman therefore submitted publicly to the new regime and announced Davos Seaworth's execution, but secretly spared him. Once Wylis returned, he revealed that Rickon Stark was alive on Skagos and offered support for Stannis in exchange for the boy's recovery.

Ships were being built in secret at White Harbor. At Ramsay's wedding, Wyman supplied pies and conspicuously honoured Robb's memory, while the disappearance of his Frey companions deepened suspicions about his intentions.

### marsh · House Marsh ✎
A northern house represented most prominently by Bowen Marsh, Lord Steward of the Night's Watch. His opposition to Jon Snow's dealings with the wildlings ends in his participation in the attack on Jon.

### mollen · House Mollen ✎
A minor northern house. Hallis Mollen commanded Winterfell's guards before escorting Eddard Stark's bones north. The party's eventual fate has not been revealed.

### mormont · House Mormont ✎
The Mormonts rule poor, wooded Bear Island in the Bay of Ice. Northern tradition says Rodrik Stark won it from an ironborn king in a wrestling match and granted it to them. Their hall is built of logs, and women as well as men bear arms against seaborne raids. The family kept the Valyrian steel sword Longclaw for five centuries.

Jeor left the lordship for the Night's Watch. Jorah succeeded him but fled to Essos after selling poachers into slavery, leaving Maege to rule. Longclaw returned to Jeor, who eventually gave it to Jon Snow. Maege and Dacey supported Robb; Maege went into the Neck on a secret mission before the Red Wedding, where Dacey was killed.

Young Lyanna refused to recognise Stannis as king, insisting on her loyalty to the King in the North. Alysane nevertheless aided his attack on Deepwood Motte, carrying soldiers in fishing boats and destroying the ironborn ships, then joined his march on Winterfell. Maege's whereabouts remain unknown; Jorah meanwhile seeks to return to Daenerys's service.

### moss · House Moss ✎
A little-known northern house. No named member has appeared in the published novels.

### norrey · House Norrey ✎
One of the northern mountain clans. Norreys helped Cregan Stark hunt down Sylas the Grim's wildlings, and later joined Stannis's northern army.

### knott · House Knott ✎
A northern mountain clan traditionally loyal to the Starks. Like neighbouring clan chiefs, its leader uses the title of lord when attending Winterfell.

### overton · House Overton ✎
A minor northern house. The published novels have not identified its members or described its lands.

### flint_of_the_mountains · House Flint of the mountains ✎
The oldest Flint branch lives in the northern mountains. Its clansmen helped pursue Sylas the Grim's wildlings under Cregan Stark and later supported Stannis against the Boltons.

### peat · House Peat ✎
A crannogman house of the Neck, sworn to the Reeds. Almost nothing has been recorded about individual members.

### poole · House Poole ✎
A family in Winterfell's service. Vayon Poole was Eddard Stark's steward and died following his lord's downfall in King's Landing. His daughter Jeyne was later passed off as Arya Stark and married to Ramsay Bolton. Theon helped her escape Winterfell.

### ryder · House Ryder ✎
The Ryders once ruled as First Men kings before their defeat by the Kings of Winter. They are not mentioned in the period of the main novels, and their survival is uncertain.

### reed · House Reed ✎
The Reeds rule the crannogmen of the Neck, guarding the land route into the North. They became Stark bannermen after the Kings of Winter subdued the ancient Marsh Kings. Their elusive seat, Greywater Watch, is said to move through the swamps, making it almost impossible for strangers to find.

Crannogmen use small boats and frog spears, and prefer ambushes and poisoned arrows to open battle. Howland Reed befriended Eddard Stark at Harrenhal and later saved his life at the tower of joy. After the deaths of their companions, the two were the only surviving witnesses to that fight.

During the War of the Five Kings, Reed men harassed the ironborn occupying Moat Cailin. Robb planned to use their help to recover the fortress, but died before he could attempt it. Jojen and Meera came to Winterfell and subsequently helped Bran escape its destruction and travel beyond the Wall. Howland himself has yet to appear directly in the published novels.

### ryswell · House Ryswell ✎
The Ryswells hold the Rills in the southwestern North, where their lands are known for horse breeding. Several branches distinguish themselves by the colours of the same horse's-head arms. Marriages tie them to the Dustins and Boltons: Barbrey rules Barrowton, while Bethany was Roose's wife and Domeric's mother. Mark Ryswell died at the tower of joy.

After the Starks' defeat, Lord Rodrik and his sons backed the Boltons. With Barbrey's men, they destroyed ironborn longships and helped isolate Moat Cailin before its surrender. They then attended Ramsay's wedding at Winterfell. Their alliance with Roose rests on longstanding family ties, but Domeric's death and Barbrey's hostility to Ramsay complicate the relationship.

### redbeard · House Redbeard ✎
The house of Gerrick Kingsblood emerged as wildlings crossed the Wall. Gerrick claims descent from Raymun Redbeard, though Tormund disputes it. He accepts Stannis's offer of settlement south of the Wall, leaving his son as a hostage of the Night's Watch.

### cerwyn · House Cerwyn ✎
The Cerwyns hold a small castle near Winterfell and have close ties to the Starks. Medger marched south with Robb, was wounded and captured at the Green Fork, and died of his injuries. Cley returned north and helped Rodrik Cassel gather men against the ironborn. Ramsay destroyed their army outside Winterfell and killed him, leaving Jonelle to head the house.

The proximity of their lands to Winterfell exposed them to Bolton pressure. Cerwyn banners appeared in Roose's army, while some surviving soldiers joined Stannis. The family endured, but the loss of successive rulers and the occupation of the northern capital limited its freedom to choose sides.

### slate · House Slate ✎
A northern house that once held the Wolf's Den. Little is known about its present members.

### stout · House Stout ✎
An old house of Goldgrass, sworn to the Dustins. Harwood Stout hosts the Boltons on their way to Winterfell; earlier, Stout men served with the northern army on the Trident.

### stane · House Stane ✎
One of Skagos's principal houses, ruling from Driftwood Hall. The novels reveal little about its current members.

### tallhart · House Tallhart ✎
The Tallharts are masters of Torrhen's Square, near the forests of the western North. Like the Glovers, their head is styled master rather than lord. Helman fought for Robb in the riverlands but died at Duskendale after marching there on Roose Bolton's orders. His heir Benfred had already been killed by the invading ironborn. Leobald tried to defend the North with Rodrik Cassel and died when Ramsay turned on their army outside Winterfell.

Dagmer took Torrhen's Square and held Eddara, Beren and Brandon captive. Through kinship with the Hornwoods, the younger Tallhart line also had a possible claim to that neighbouring inheritance. Some surviving Tallhart soldiers joined Stannis, but the family seat remained in ironborn hands.

### whitehill · House Whitehill ✎
A minor northern house whose seat and history receive little attention in the books. The more extensive Whitehill story in Telltale's game belongs to that adaptation's separate continuity.

### waterman · House Waterman ✎
A little-known northern house. The books do not name its seat or words.

### wells_north · House Wells ✎
The northern Wells branch is represented by Ser Theodan Wells, who joins the Warrior's Sons after the restoration of the Faith Militant.

### fenn · House Fenn ✎
A crannogman house of the Neck, sworn to the Reeds. It has no developed independent history in the novels.

### fisher_of_the_stony_shore · House Fisher of the Stony Shore ✎
The Fishers formerly ruled the Stony Shore as kings. The Kings of Winter defeated them and made them Stark vassals. Whether the house survived into the period of the main novels is unknown.

### flint_of_widows_watch · House Flint of Widow's Watch ✎
The Flint branch at Widow's Watch is headed by Lady Lyessa. The family once held the Wolf's Den and is connected to other northern Flint branches.

### flint_of_breakstone_hill · House Flint of Breakstone Hill ✎
An ancient Flint branch subdued by the Kings of Winter. It does not appear in the main novels, and its later fate is unknown.

### flint_of_flints_finger · House Flint of Flint's Finger ✎
A Flint branch established at Flint's Finger, descended from the family at Widow's Watch.

### forrester · House Forrester ✎
Woodland bannermen of the Glovers whose scouts help Stannis after the recovery of Deepwood Motte. Ironrath and the family history developed in Telltale's game are not described in the book canon.

### harclay · House Harclay ✎
A northern mountain clan. Maester Aemon appealed to the Harclays for help when wildlings threatened the Wall.

### holt_north · House Holt ✎
A northern Holt branch that once possessed the Wolf's Den. Ser Maynard Holt serves in the Night's Watch and captains the Talon.

### hornwood · House Hornwood ✎
The Hornwoods hold wooded lands between the Boltons and Manderlys. Their men followed Cregan Stark south after the Dance. During the War of the Five Kings, Halys died at the Green Fork and his heir Daryn in the Whispering Wood, leaving Donella without a legitimate successor. Neighbours and relatives pressed claims; legitimising Larence Snow or giving the lands to Beren Tallhart under the Hornwood name were among the proposals.

Ramsay forestalled them by abducting Donella and forcing her to marry him. He confined her without food, then claimed the inheritance after her death. The Manderlys occupied the castle and resisted the seizure, but the crown later ordered them to yield to the Boltons. Hornwood men subsequently served on both sides, some under Ramsay and others with Stannis. Surviving relatives leave the possibility of restoring the family, although no Hornwood ruler is presently recognised.

### ashwood · House Ashwood ✎
A northern house that once held the Wolf's Den. Nothing is known about its current members, but its extinction has not been established.

## Речные земли

### tully · House Tully ✎
Aegon the Conqueror made the Lords of Riverrun paramount lords of the Trident. Edmyn was the first major river lord to rebel against Harren the Black and gained regional authority after the Hoares' destruction. The Tullys had never been kings; some vassals possess larger estates and armies. Rivers and a moat defend Riverrun, allowing it to become an island. During the Dance, old Grover favoured Aegon II, but the younger Tullys took their army to Rhaenyra's side.

Kermit and Oscar helped defeat Borros Baratheon. Hoster supported Robert's Rebellion, sealing the alliance through marriages to the Starks and Arryns. Those ties again determined their allegiance in the War of the Five Kings: after the Lannister invasion, the river lords recognised Robb as king. Edmure stopped Tywin at the fords but inadvertently spoiled Robb's plan to draw him west. Edmure's marriage to Roslin Frey was meant to restore the alliance; the Red Wedding instead made him a prisoner.

Brynden held Riverrun until Jaime secured its surrender through Edmure. The castle went to Emmon Frey, the paramount title to Baelish. Brynden escaped; Edmure was sent under guard towards Casterly Rock.

### butterwell · House Butterwell ✎
The Butterwells grew wealthy through their cattle herds. Ambrose tried to keep connections on both sides of the first Blackfyre rebellion. Later, the tournament at his new castle, Whitewalls, provided cover for another rising. Brynden Rivers uncovered the plot, stripped Ambrose of most of his lands and ordered Whitewalls demolished.

### bigglestone · House Bigglestone ✎
A little-known riverlands house. Following the Tullys' removal, Petyr Baelish became its nominal overlord, but its own lands and family history remain largely undescribed.

### blanetree · House Blanetree ✎
A riverlands family whose castle Visenya burned with Vhagar during the Faith Militant uprising. The house survived and still existed during the War of the Five Kings.

### blackwood · House Blackwood ✎
An ancient First Men house of Raventree Hall. Family tradition says the Blackwoods ruled part of the wolfswood until the Starks drove them south. In the riverlands they kept the old gods; ravens gather around their castle's huge dead weirwood, whose death they blame on the Brackens. Both houses once claimed the river crown and have disputed lands for centuries, each calling the other former vassals and usurpers.

Marriages and truces only briefly interrupted the feud. The Blackwoods supported Rhaenyra in the Dance: young Benjicot and Alysanne helped defeat the Greens. Later connections brought them to court through Melissa, Brynden Rivers's mother, and Queen Betha. Tytos backed Robb and resisted after the Red Wedding. The Brackens besieged Raventree Hall, hoping to gain disputed lands.

Jaime secured a surrender that left the Blackwoods their castle but granted some lands to Jonos Bracken. Jaime took Hoster Blackwood as a hostage. Hoster accompanied him to Pennytree, where Jaime disappeared after leaving with Brienne.

### bracken · House Bracken ✎
The Brackens of Stone Hedge are an ancient First Men house that adopted the Faith of the Seven after the Andal invasion. Their feud with the Blackwoods concerns disputed lands and rival accounts of an earlier kingship: each family claims the other usurped it. Even their joint resistance to the Andals failed to end the quarrel.

They supported Aegon II in the Dance before defeat forced a change of allegiance. Amos Bracken killed Samwell Blackwood at the Burning Mill and died in the same battle. Barba Bracken's influence under Aegon IV gave the feud a place at court; her son Aegor Rivers, Bittersteel, continued it against Melissa Blackwood's descendants. The Brackens supported Daemon in the first Blackfyre rebellion.

Jonos pledged himself to Robb during the War of the Five Kings, while Lannister armies ravaged his lands. After the Red Wedding he submitted to the crown, besieged Raventree Hall and demanded his neighbours' territory. Jaime granted some disputed lands but required a Bracken hostage as well as a Blackwood one.

### vypren · House Vypren ✎
A riverlands house of Andal origin. Lucias and Damon Vypren attended the Red Wedding; Vypren men later joined the Frey siege of Riverrun.

### vance_of_atranta · House Vance of Atranta ✎
One of the two Vance branches descended from the Andal conqueror Armistead. Ronnel Vance supported Aegon the Uncrowned and died at the battle beneath the Gods Eye. Norbert heads the Atranta branch during the War of the Five Kings.

### vance_of_wayfarers_rest · House Vance of Wayfarer's Rest ✎
The Vances of Wayfarer's Rest supported Robb Stark. Karyl inherited after his father's death at the Golden Tooth, attended the council that proclaimed Robb king, and later returned to defend his own lands.

### grey · House Grey ✎
A minor riverlands house known from scattered references. Its holdings and family history have received little attention in the books.

### grell · House Grell ✎
A family in Riverrun's service. Desmond Grell trained the Tullys for many years. When the castle surrendered, he chose the Night's Watch rather than service to its new masters.

### goodbrook · House Goodbrook ✎
The Goodbrooks remained loyal to the Targaryens in Robert's Rebellion and were punished by Hoster Tully. Ser Garse later sided with the Freys and was killed by Sandor Clegane during the events surrounding the Red Wedding.

### darry · House Darry ✎
The Darrys are an old house of the Trident whose loyalty to the Targaryens repeatedly cost them dearly. They supported Rhaenyra in the Dance; Aemond burned their castle and fields with Vhagar, but the family survived. They later stood with Daeron II against the Blackfyres. Robert's Rebellion killed several heirs and deprived the defeated house of lands and influence. Willem Darry rescued Viserys and Daenerys from Dragonstone and took them to Braavos.

During the War of the Five Kings, Castle Darry changed hands repeatedly. Gregor Clegane killed young Lyman, ending the legitimate male line. The crown awarded the castle to Lancel Lannister and arranged his marriage to a Frey woman descended from the Darrys through her mother. Lancel soon abandoned the marriage and his lands for the Faith Militant, leaving his wife and her relatives to maintain their hold on the inheritance.

### deddings · House Deddings ✎
The family suffered dragonfire twice: Visenya burned its castle during the Faith Militant uprising, and Lyonel Deddings died beneath Vermithor at Second Tumbleton. Lannister men later killed Lord and Lady Deddings during the War of the Five Kings.

### qoherys · House Qoherys ✎
Aegon granted Harrenhal to his master-at-arms Quenton Qoherys after Harren the Black's death. The house's last lord, Gargon the Guest, made himself hated by abusing women under his power. Harren the Red killed him, and Harrenhal passed to the Harroways.

### keath · House Keath ✎
A little-known riverlands house. Its seat and individual members have scarcely been described.

### cox · House Cox ✎
A knightly riverlands house whose holdings and independent role in the region's wars remain largely unrecorded.

### lannister_of_darry · House Lannister of Darry ✎
This branch began when the crown awarded Castle Darry to Lancel Lannister. He married Amerei Frey, a descendant of its previous rulers, but relinquished the marriage and his lands to join the Warrior's Sons. Amerei and her relatives remained in control of the castle.

### lychester · House Lychester ✎
Visenya burned the family's castle during the Faith Militant uprising. In Robert's Rebellion, Lymond Lychester's sons fought on opposing sides and all died. The old lord survived them but lost his reason.

### lolliston · House Lolliston ✎
A small riverlands house known only through scattered references, without a developed family history.

### lothston · House Lothston ✎
Former rulers of Harrenhal. Lucas received the castle from Aegon III, and later generations acquired a reputation for cruelty and disturbing practices. Danelle supported the crown against the second Blackfyre rebellion. The house fell during Maekar's reign, and the Whents succeeded it.

### mallister · House Mallister ✎
The Mallisters of Seagard defend the western riverlands against the ironborn. Their seaside castle has a warning bell to alert the population to approaching raiders, and the family maintains ships of its own.

Jason fought at the Trident in Robert's Rebellion. During Balon's rebellion he repelled an attack on Seagard and killed Rodrik Greyjoy. He subsequently supported Robb Stark and took part in his campaigns. Before attempting to return north, Robb sent Galbart Glover and Maege Mormont into the Neck on a Mallister ship with secret instructions.

Seagard escaped the destruction at the Red Wedding, but Black Walder Frey later forced Jason to surrender by threatening to hang his captured son Patrek. The castle survived under Frey control, with both Mallisters held prisoner. Another family member, Denys, had long commanded the Shadow Tower for the Night's Watch.

### mooton · House Mooton ✎
The Mootons rule Maidenpool, a wealthy port on the Bay of Crabs. Once local kings, they eventually submitted to larger riverland kingdoms. They first resisted Aegon's Conquest before accepting his rule.

Manfryd supported Rhaenyra in the Dance but refused her order to kill Nettles, which would have broken guest right. He changed allegiance to Aegon II and later served on Aegon III's regency council. Myles Mooton was Rhaegar's squire and died at Robert's hands during the Battle of the Bells.

Maidenpool endured repeated attacks and plundering during the War of the Five Kings before Randyll Tarly occupied it. William received a pardon after the Stark defeat. His heir Eleanor's marriage to Dickon Tarly secured a new alliance, tying the port's future to the family of its recent military governor.

### nutt · House Nutt ✎
A riverlands house associated with Jeyne Nutt's brief kingship. She rose against the Durrandons and proclaimed herself Queen of the Trident, but her rule did not last.

### nayland · House Nayland ✎
The family holds Hag's Mire. It is probably a knightly house among the Freys' bannermen, although that allegiance is not explicitly confirmed in the narrative.

### piper · House Piper ✎
The Pipers hold Pinkmaiden. Marq Piper and Karyl Vance resisted the Lannister invasion in the War of the Five Kings. Gregor Clegane ravaged the castle, and Marq was later taken prisoner at the Red Wedding.

### pemford · House Pemford ✎

Janos Slynt's betrayal of Eddard Stark brought him a lordship and a grant of Harrenhal from Joffrey. He never established himself there: Tyrion sent him to the Night's Watch. Jon Snow later executed him for refusing an order.

### smallwood · House Smallwood ✎
Vance bannermen seated at Acorn Hall. Ravella Smallwood shelters the Brotherhood Without Banners and helps Arya. Earlier Smallwoods supported Aegon the Uncrowned and served Targaryen kings as Hand.

### strong · House Strong ✎
An ancient First Men house, the Strongs received Harrenhal under Jaehaerys I. Its extensive lands helped make them influential at court. Lyonel became Viserys's master of laws and later replaced Otto Hightower as Hand. Harwin's closeness to Rhaenyra fuelled rumours that he fathered her first three sons. Both men died in a fire at Harrenhal whose cause remains disputed.

Larys inherited the castle and served Aegon II as a spymaster. During the Dance, Aemond accused the Strongs remaining at Harrenhal of treason and killed them. Alys Rivers, considered a bastard of the house, survived. After the war Cregan Stark executed Larys for his part in Aegon II's murder.

### towers_of_harrenhal · House Towers of Harrenhal ✎
Walton Towers won Harrenhal through Maegor's tournament. His descendants held it only briefly: the house died out in 61 AC, and the castle was later granted to the Strongs.

A knightly house in Harrenhal's service. Ser Willis accompanied Catelyn after Tyrion's capture and died in the Vale. Oswald Wode had earlier fought in the Dance against Criston Cole's forces.

### wayn · House Wayn ✎
A riverlands house whose castle suffered Vhagar's attack during the Faith Militant uprising. Utherydes Wayn serves as Riverrun's steward.

### whent · House Whent ✎
Once Lothston bannermen, the Whents received Harrenhal after that house's fall. Possession of the enormous castle brought prestige but also costs the family struggled to meet. Walter Whent's lavish tournament in 281 AC drew the court and great lords. Rhaegar's victory and his choice of Lyanna Stark as queen of love and beauty, rather than his own wife, became central memories of the gathering that preceded Robert's Rebellion.

Oswell Whent served in the Kingsguard and died at the tower of joy. Shella held Harrenhal when the War of the Five Kings began, but abandoned it rather than face Tywin's advancing army. The castle subsequently passed between armies before the crown awarded it to Petyr Baelish.

Baelish claims Shella is dead, though the circumstances are unrevealed. Losing Harrenhal does not by itself establish that the whole family is extinct.

### frey · House Frey ✎
A relatively young house enriched by its crossing over the Green Fork. The Twins are linked by a toll bridge; a large army cannot cross quickly without Frey consent. Wealth brought many marriage connections, though their origins and calculating habits drew contempt from older nobility. Walder ruled for decades, outlived several wives and built an enormous family already divided over its inheritance.

He reached the Trident after the battle was decided, earning the name the Late Lord Frey. During the War of the Five Kings, Robb bought passage by promising to marry a daughter of Walder's. After Robb married Jeyne Westerling, Walder conspired with Tywin and Roose Bolton. Edmure and Roslin's wedding became a trap: the Freys broke guest right, killed the king and his men, and captured survivors.

The crown rewarded them with Riverrun and other lands, while marriages secured their Bolton alliance. Some Freys still sympathised with the Starks and took no part in the plot. Afterwards, the family became a target of the Brotherhood Without Banners and covert northern revenge. Stevron's death left Ryman heir; his subsequent murder brought another struggle between the many branches closer.

### frey_of_riverrun · House Frey of Riverrun ✎
Emmon Frey received Riverrun after the Red Wedding but could occupy it only once Edmure Tully surrendered. His wife Genna Lannister directs their affairs more confidently than he does. The lordship gives him the castle, not authority over all the Trident: Petyr Baelish holds the paramount title.

A minor riverlands house whose holdings and participation in the wars have not been developed in the books.

Lucas Harroway received Harrenhal after the Qoherys line ended and became Maegor's Hand. His daughter Alys married the king. When Tyanna accused her of infidelity, Maegor ordered the family exterminated and divided its holdings among other houses.

Frey bannermen. Harys Haigh served in Roose Bolton's army, and family members participated in the Red Wedding.

A minor riverlands house whose seat and family history have not been described in detail.

### charlton · House Charlton ✎
Frey bannermen who take part in the siege of Riverrun. An earlier Lord Charlton supported Aegon the Uncrowned against Maegor. Ser Andrey died at the hands of Lannister men during the War of the Five Kings.

### shawney · House Shawney ✎
A little-known riverlands house. Neither the name nor the location of its seat has been revealed.

### erenford · House Erenford ✎
Frey bannermen. Young Joyeuse Erenford became Walder Frey's eighth wife, and Erenford soldiers serve in the army besieging Riverrun.

## Долина Аррен

### arryn · House Arryn ✎
An ancient Andal dynasty descended from Artys Arryn, who defeated the First Men at the Battle of the Seven Stars. The Kings of Mountain and Vale ruled from the Gates of the Moon before building the Eyrie. Mountains and difficult passes protect their lands. The Eyrie itself is almost impregnable but uninhabitable in winter. During the Conquest, Visenya flew there on Vhagar, and the regent Sharra surrendered without a battle.

Jeyne supported Rhaenyra in the Dance, demanding dragons to defend the Vale. Her childless death caused a succession dispute between family branches. Jon fostered Eddard Stark and Robert Baratheon; his refusal to surrender them to Aerys II began the rebellion. He later served Robert for years as Hand. His investigation into Cersei's children ended in his poisoning: Lysa acted on Baelish's instructions.

Lysa took young Robert to the Vale and kept its lords out of the War of the Five Kings. Petyr married her, murdered her and became Lord Protector. The Lords Declarant challenge him, but he weakens their coalition through private agreements. Robert's ill health makes the position of his heir, Harrold Hardyng, especially important.

### upcliff · House Upcliff ✎
A Vale house known through scattered historical references. Its present members have not appeared in the main novels.

### arryn_of_gulltown · House Arryn of Gulltown ✎
A wealthy Arryn branch closely involved in Gulltown's trade. After Jeyne Arryn's death, Isembard, the Gilded Falcon, claimed the Eyrie but eventually recognised Joffrey Arryn.

### baelish · House Baelish ✎
A recently established house of the Vale, the Baelishes began with a Braavosi sellsword in Corbray service. His son became a hedge knight; the next generation acquired a small tower on the Fingers. The old arms bore the Titan's head, while Petyr adopted a mockingbird. His upbringing at Riverrun and Lysa Arryn's patronage helped him enter royal service. Success in customs collection brought him to the small council as master of coin.

Petyr's rise gave the house titles far beyond its modest ancestral lands. For arranging the Lannister–Tyrell alliance, he received Harrenhal and the lord paramountcy of the Trident. He remained largely absent from that castle. Marriage to Lysa gave him access to power in the Vale; after her murder he retained it as Lord Protector. The Lords Declarant challenged his authority, but bribery and private bargains weakened their alliance. He also sheltered Sansa Stark as his supposed daughter, making her identity part of his plans for the northern succession.

### belmore · House Belmore ✎
The Belmores hold Strongsong. Benedar joined the Lords Declarant in demanding Petyr Baelish's removal from control of the Vale. Baelish works to divide that coalition through gifts and private arrangements.

### borrell · House Borrell ✎
The Borrells rule Sweetsister, an island associated with smuggling and false lights that lure ships ashore. During Robert's Rebellion, Lord Borrell secretly helped Eddard reach White Harbor. Godric later shelters Davos, warns him of the danger ahead and lets him continue his journey.

### breakstone · House Breakstone ✎
A Vale house represented by Ser Edmund Breakstone in a published chapter of The Winds of Winter. Its wider history remains undescribed.

### wydman · House Wydman ✎
A Vale family whose arms commemorate an ancestor's tournament victories. Little is recorded about its present rulers.

### waxley · House Waxley ✎
The knights of Wickenden are Arryn bannermen. Their lands are known for candle making.

### woodhull · House Woodhull ✎
A knightly family on the Paps, sworn to the Eleshams. It receives little attention in the main novels.

### grafton · House Grafton ✎
The Graftons rule Gulltown, the Vale's main port. Maritime trade gives them wealth and connections beyond those available to many of the mountain lords.

During Robert's Rebellion, the family remained loyal to the Targaryens and barred the city to Jon Arryn. Robert took part in the assault and killed Marq Grafton; the defeated family accepted the new regime. Petyr Baelish began his official career here, collecting port duties under Lysa's patronage.

Gerold retains the lordship in the later books, while family members appear in commercial and marriage negotiations. During the Vale's withdrawal from the War of the Five Kings, Gulltown continued to connect it with the rest of Westeros. The family's influence rests principally on the city and its revenues.

### dutton · House Dutton ✎
A minor Vale house that supported Arnold Arryn during the dispute over Jeyne Arryn's succession in 134 AC.

### donniger · House Donniger ✎
A Vale house known chiefly from lists of noble families. Its independent history has not been developed in the novels.

### egen · House Egen ✎
A Vale house represented by Vardis Egen, Jon Arryn's captain of guards. He died fighting Bronn as Lysa's champion in Tyrion's trial by combat.

### elesham · House Elesham ✎
The Eleshams hold the Paps off the Vale's coast. The knightly Woodhulls are their bannermen.

### coldwater · House Coldwater ✎
Royce bannermen who supported Arnold Arryn after Jeyne's death. Baelish later expects them to join Yohn Royce's opposition to his rule.

### corbray · House Corbray ✎
The Corbrays held Heart's Home in the Vale and traced their origins to the Andal conquest. Their early rulers took the Fingers from the First Men; the family later claimed a share in the victory over Robar Royce at the Seven Stars. Their most prized possession was Lady Forlorn, a Valyrian steel sword.

Qarl supported Aegon the Uncrowned against Maegor, then commanded the City Watch under Jaehaerys. During the Dance, Leowyn and Corwyn led the Vale's forces for the blacks. Both served Aegon III's government; Corwyn's death at Runestone drew the house into the Arryn succession dispute. Gwayne later carried Lady Forlorn against Daemon Blackfyre at the Redgrass Field.

By the War of the Five Kings the house retained its standing but had little money. Lyonel ruled Heart's Home, while Lyn held the ancestral sword. Littlefinger brokered Lyonel's marriage to a rich merchant's daughter and secretly paid Lyn, whose public opposition helped him manipulate the Lords Declarant. The marriage brought a substantial dowry and the prospect of an heir.

### crayne · House Crayne ✎
A Vale house that backed Joffrey Arryn in the struggle over Jeyne's inheritance. Little is recorded about its current members.

### lynderly · House Lynderly ✎
The Lynderlys hold Snakewood. Jon Lynderly is among the Vale lords whose backing Petyr Baelish seeks.

### lipps · House Lipps ✎
A little-known Vale house. Ser Ossifer Lipps appears in a published chapter of The Winds of Winter.

### longthorpe · House Longthorpe ✎
One of the houses of the Three Sisters, sworn to the Sunderlands. Its family history remains largely unrecorded.

### melcolm · House Melcolm ✎
A Vale house about whose lands and involvement in war little is known.

### moore · House Moore ✎
A Vale house best known through Kingsguard knight Mandon Moore. During the Blackwater, he attempted to kill Tyrion but was killed by Podrick Payne.

### pryor · House Pryor ✎
A Vale house whose individual members have not received a developed history in the books.

### redfort · House Redfort ✎
The Redforts are an ancient First Men family that survived the Andal conquest of the Vale. Horton is a longstanding associate and ally of Yohn Royce. He wanted to support the Starks after Eddard's execution, but Lysa prevented the Vale's armies from joining the war. Following her death he joined the Lords Declarant, demanding that Baelish be removed from Robert Arryn's guardianship.

His sons participate in court life and tournaments. Mychel, formerly Lyn Corbray's squire, loved Mya Stone but married Ysilla Royce to strengthen the families' alliance. Baelish hopes to break the Redforts' resistance along with the rest of the coalition, exploiting Horton's age, private negotiations and his growing web of connections in the Vale.

### ruthermont · House Ruthermont ✎
A lordly Vale house whose members have yet to receive an independent story in the main novels.

### royce_of_the_gates_of_the_moon · House Royce of the Gates of the Moon ✎
Nestor Royce's branch grew out of his long service administering the Gates of the Moon for the Arryns. Baelish secured him hereditary possession of the castle. Nestor rewarded that favour by supporting the new Lord Protector and shutting the gates against his opponents.

### royce · House Royce ✎
The Royces of Runestone descend from the First Men and remain one of the Vale's strongest houses. Before the Andal conquest they ruled as Bronze Kings. Robar II, the last of them, died fighting Artys Arryn at the Battle of the Seven Stars. The family kept Runestone as vassals of the new dynasty; its bronze armour bears ancient runes said to offer protection.

The Royces backed Rhaenyra with the Vale during the Dance, though Rhea Royce's marriage to Daemon did not create a lasting alliance with the prince. Yohn later wanted to help Robb after Eddard's execution, but Lysa forbade the Vale's lords to march. His son Robar served Renly independently and died after that king's murder.

Yohn led the Lords Declarant against Baelish, who won a year's delay and began dividing their coalition. Meanwhile, Nestor's cadet branch gained hereditary possession of the Gates of the Moon through Baelish's grant, making a united Royce opposition harder to sustain.

### sunderland · House Sunderland ✎
The Sunderlands rule the Three Sisters in the Bite. Their bannermen include the Borrells of Sweetsister, Longthorpes of Longsister and Torrents of Littlesister. Fishing, smuggling and a coast dangerous to unfamiliar ships shape island life. For centuries, wars between the Starks and Arryns brought destruction to the archipelago.

At the beginning of Aegon's Conquest, Marla Sunderland proclaimed independence. Targaryen intervention ended that attempt: her brother Steffon submitted, and the islands remained under the Arryns. Jacaerys later secured their support for Rhaenyra during the Dance.

In a published chapter of The Winds of Winter, Triston's sons attend Baelish's tournament, where the family seeks advantageous marriages. The islands' distance from the Eyrie and frequent disregard for mainland law do not remove the Arryns' claim to their allegiance.

### templeton · House Templeton ✎
A powerful knightly house whose head bears the title Knight of Ninestars. Its wealth and military strength rival those of many lords. Symond Templeton joins the Lords Declarant against Baelish.

### tollett · House Tollett ✎
Royce bannermen of the Grey Glen. Eddison Tollett serves in the Night's Watch and is known for his bleak humour. Tollett men support Yohn Royce's resistance to Baelish.

### torrent · House Torrent ✎
A house of the Three Sisters, sworn to the Sunderlands. Its holdings are associated with Littlesister.

### waynwood · House Waynwood ✎
The Waynwoods of Ironoaks are an old Andal family. Kinship with the Starks and Arryns gives them a place in the succession of both dynasties. Wild Wyl Waynwood died at Daemon Blackfyre's hands on the Redgrass Field. In the later books Anya rules the family and joins the Lords Declarant against Baelish.

Harrold Hardyng grew up under her protection and received his knighthood while remaining Robert Arryn's presumed heir. Yet the family's debts make it vulnerable. Baelish buys those obligations and obtains Anya's consent to Harrold's betrothal to Alayne, the identity under which he is hiding Sansa Stark. Anya retains her standing, but dependence on Baelish's money constrains her opposition to his plans.

### hunter · House Hunter ✎
The Hunters hold Longbow Hall. Gilwood succeeds the elderly Eon, although his brothers suspect him of murdering their father. He joins the Lords Declarant against Baelish.

### hardyng · House Hardyng ✎
Landed knights sworn to the Waynwoods. Harrold Hardyng becomes next in line to the Eyrie after Robert Arryn. Baelish plans to marry him to Sansa Stark, concealed as Alayne Stone.

### hersy · House Hersy ✎
The Hersys hold Newkeep. Their wider history has received little attention in the main novels.

### shett_of_gulltown · House Shett of Gulltown ✎
The Gulltown branch of the Shetts probably answers to the Graftons, although the narrative does not explicitly confirm that relationship.

### shett_of_gull_tower · House Shett of Gull Tower ✎
Landed knights of Gull Tower, sworn to the Royces of Runestone.

## Западные земли

### lannister · House Lannister ✎
The Lords of Casterly Rock drew their wealth from the western gold mines. They trace their origins to Lann the Clever, who supposedly gained the Casterlys' fortress through trickery. Originally of First Men descent, the family later intermarried with Andal nobility. They ruled as Kings of the Rock until Loren's defeat at the Field of Fire. He lost his crown but kept his lands as Warden of the West. Under the weak Tytos, vassals ceased to respect the Rock's authority.

Tywin restored it by destroying the Reynes and Tarbecks, then governed the realm for almost twenty years as Aerys II's Hand. His army sacked the capital at the end of Robert's Rebellion. The murders of Elia Martell and her children earned lasting enemies in Dorne. Cersei's marriage to Robert secured influence at court; royal debts increased the crown's dependence on Lannister gold.

Their alliance with the Tyrells defeated Stannis on the Blackwater and secured Joffrey's throne, but the family fractured. Tyrion killed Tywin and fled to Essos, Jaime grew distant from Cersei, and Varys murdered Kevan. Cersei inherited Casterly Rock, while the Faith Militant and Tommen's allies limit her power in the capital.

### algood · House Algood ✎
A little-known Lannister bannerman house of the westerlands. Its arms are recorded in heraldic material, but no member appears in the novels.

### banefort · House Banefort ✎
The Baneforts trace their ancestry to the legendary Hooded King. Quenten was captured in the Whispering Wood during the War of the Five Kings and held by Jonos Bracken.

### bettley · House Bettley ✎
A minor western house. One of its men-at-arms became a broken man during the War of the Five Kings.

### brax · House Brax ✎
The Braxes of Hornvale are among the principal western houses. Andros died in the rout of the camps outside Riverrun; the family lost further knights in the subsequent fighting against Robb.

### broom · House Broom ✎
An old western family represented by Benedict Broom, Casterly Rock's master-at-arms. English sources distinguish this house from House Broome, although their Russian names coincide.

### broome · House Broome ✎
Maegor burned the family's castle for supporting the Faith Militant. Alfred Broome later served at Dragonstone and betrayed Rhaenyra to Aegon II. English sources distinguish the Broomes from House Broom.

### westerling · House Westerling ✎
The Westerlings of the Crag have an ancestry much grander than their present finances. Their depleted estates made Gawen's marriage to the wealthy Sybell Spicer useful, though her family's recent ennoblement invited contempt. Gawen was already a prisoner when Robb captured the Crag. Jeyne nursed the wounded king; after sleeping with her, he married her, breaking his promise to the Freys.

The family apparently joined the Starks, but Sybell negotiated secretly with Tywin and gave Jeyne a preparation that prevented pregnancy. At the Red Wedding, Raynald freed Grey Wind, was wounded and disappeared. His body was never found.

After Robb's death, the Westerlings received pardons and promises of advantageous marriages and rewards. Jeyne remained devoted to her husband's memory. Jaime sent her and her relatives towards Casterly Rock under guard, preventing the widow from being used to revive claims to the northern crown.

### vikary · House Vikary ✎
A knightly house sworn to Casterly Rock. Suggested connections to the Reynes or Crakehalls remain unconfirmed. Lymond Vikary died during the War of the Five Kings.

### garner · House Garner ✎
A western house represented by Ser Alyn Garner, who took part in the Ashford tournament of 209 AC.

### greenfield · House Greenfield ✎
Landed knights sworn to the Lannisters. Garth Greenfield was captured in the Whispering Wood and sent to Raventree Hall.

### jast · House Jast ✎
A house of Andal origin. During Tytos Lannister's rule it feuded with the Falwells. Antario Jast later became a prisoner after the defeat of Stafford Lannister's army.

### doggett · House Doggett ✎
Joffrey Doggett, the Red Dog of the Hills, led the Warrior's Sons against Maegor. The king burned his family's castle, but he continued resisting. After Jaehaerys's accession, Joffrey reconciled with the crown and joined the Kingsguard.

### drox · House Drox ✎
A western house formed during the mixing of Andal nobles with the First Men. Its present members do not appear in the novels.

### yew · House Yew ✎
An old First Men house linked to the legend of the Blind Bowman. Manfryd Yew joined the siege of Riverrun, guarding against escape along the river.

### kenning_of_kayce · House Kenning of Kayce ✎
The Kayce branch of the Kennings originally held knightly rank and became lords after the Dance. Dalton Greyjoy's raids inflicted severe losses on the family.

### kyndall · House Kyndall ✎
A house of Andal origin. Theora Kyndall married Tybolt Lannister; their daughter Cerelle briefly inherited Casterly Rock.

### clegane · House Clegane ✎
The house began with Tytos Lannister's kennelmaster, who saved him from a lioness at the cost of a leg and three dogs. A tower and lands rewarded him, and the dogs became the new family's arms.

Gregor became one of Tywin's chief instruments of violence. He took part in the sack of King's Landing, murdered Elia Martell and ravaged the riverlands. His brutality shaped the family's reputation. Sandor rejected knighthood, guarded Joffrey and deserted the royal army at the Blackwater. After travelling with Arya, he was believed dead; other men began wearing the Hound's helmet.

Oberyn poisoned Gregor during their duel. Pycelle announced his death while Qyburn worked on his body. The later appearance of Robert Strong suggests a connection, but the new Kingsguard knight's identity has not been publicly confirmed. Succession to the Cleganes' modest holdings remains uncertain.

### clifton · House Clifton ✎
A knightly family on Fair Isle, sworn to the Farmans. Gareth Clifton's marriage to Jeyne Farman strengthened the connection between the houses.

### crakehall · House Crakehall ✎

### lydden · House Lydden ✎

### lorch · House Lorch ✎

### reyne · House Reyne ✎
The Reynes grew rich on Castamere's gold and silver mines. Most of their stronghold lay underground in enlarged mine passages, allowing the household and stores to shelter behind relatively few entrances. They entered the Lannister kingdom through marriage but long rivalled their overlords in wealth and influence.

Under the weak Tytos, Roger and Reynard borrowed heavily and increasingly disregarded Casterly Rock. Their sister Ellyn married Walderan Tarbeck after failing to establish herself among the Lannisters, linking the two rebellious families.

Tywin's demands for repayment and accountability brought resistance. In 261 AC he defeated the Reyne army. The family and its dependants retreated beneath Castamere, where he sealed the exits and diverted water into the tunnels. Their destruction became the subject of "The Rains of Castamere". After the Red Wedding, the ruined estate was granted to Rolph Spicer.

### sarsfield · House Sarsfield ✎
A western house formed during the integration of Andal nobles and the First Men. Little of its separate family history is recorded.

### swyft · House Swyft ✎

### serrett · House Serrett ✎

### spicer · House Spicer ✎

### stilwood · House Stilwood ✎

### stackspear · House Stackspear ✎

## Простор

### gardener · House Gardener ✎
The Gardeners, kings of the Reach, claimed descent from Garth the Gardener, a son of Garth Greenhand. From Highgarden they gradually united the fertile lands of the Mander through war and marriage. Oldtown entered their kingdom through a Hightower alliance; the Arbor followed after its last king disappeared.

Rather than being displaced by the Andals, they recruited newcomers and married heiresses to them. The dynasty endured while the Reach's nobility changed around it. Many houses became Gardener relatives, giving them later grounds for claims to Highgarden.

The last king, Mern IX, allied with Loren Lannister against Aegon. At the Field of Fire the dragons killed Mern and his heirs, ending the royal line. Highgarden's steward, Harlen Tyrell, surrendered the castle and received rule over the Reach. The Gardeners' surviving relatives became his bannermen, though several considered themselves better entitled to the inheritance.

### tyrell · House Tyrell ✎
The Lords of Highgarden gained the Reach after the Gardeners died at the Field of Fire. Before the Conquest, they were hereditary stewards: Harlan surrendered Highgarden to Aegon and received the former kings' lands. Older families, especially the Florents, considered their own claims stronger. The Tyrells consolidated their position through marriages to influential vassals. The fertile Reach supports vast armies, while the Redwynes and coastal lords provide substantial naval strength.

During the Dance, young Lyonel's regents remained neutral as their vassals fought on both sides. Mace supported the Targaryens in Robert's Rebellion and besieged Storm's End, then recognised Robert after the capital fell. In the War of the Five Kings, the family first backed Renly: Margaery became queen and Loras commanded his guard. After Renly's death, they allied with the Lannisters and helped defeat Stannis on the Blackwater.

Margaery married Joffrey, then Tommen; Garlan was granted Brightwater Keep, though the Florents still held it; Mace eventually became Tommen's Hand. Cersei's hostility towards Margaery and the Faith Militant complicate the alliance. Euron's invasion forces the family to defend the Reach's western coast while preserving its influence in the capital.

The fleet was sent to Dragonstone, leaving the western coast exposed. Euron's attack on the Shield Islands forced the ships to turn back towards the Arbor. Their strength remains essential to the Reach's defence, but the distance between its coasts limits how quickly they can respond.

## Штормовые земли

### baratheon · House Baratheon ✎
The house began during the Conquest. Orys, Aegon's closest companion and alleged half-brother, defeated the last Storm King, Argilac, and married Argella. He adopted Storm's End, the crowned stag and the Durrandon words, along with their border conflicts with Dorne. The Baratheons remained powerful under the Targaryens, though relations with the crown varied. Rogar helped Jaehaerys gain the throne before quarrelling with him; Borros supported Aegon II in the Dance and died in its last major battle.

Robert rebelled after Aerys demanded his execution and took the Iron Throne. Descent from Rhaelle Targaryen strengthened his claim. The royal house divided between the capital, Stannis's Dragonstone and Renly's Storm's End. After Robert's death, both brothers rejected Joffrey and declared themselves kings. Stannis revealed that Cersei's children were not Robert's. Renly died; Stannis lost the Battle of the Blackwater but continued his war in the North.

The Golden Company has now entered the struggle for the stormlands with another claimant to the throne. Tommen bears the Baratheon name, though his power rests chiefly on the Lannisters and Tyrells.

### durrandon · House Durrandon ✎
The Storm Kings traced their line to Durran Godsgrief. Legend says his marriage to Elenei, daughter of the sea god and wind goddess, brought divine storms upon him; Storm's End finally withstood their fury. Later kings expanded north and west. Arlan III conquered the riverlands, which his descendants lost to the Hoares. Their kingdom had considerably diminished by Aegon's arrival.

Argilac, the last king, offered Aegon lands and his daughter Argella's hand, hoping to create a barrier against Harren. He rejected Aegon's proposal that Orys marry her instead. During the ensuing war, Argilac left Storm's End to face the invaders and died fighting Orys.

The castle's defenders delivered Argella to the victor. Orys married her and founded House Baratheon, retaining Storm's End and the Durrandons' crowned stag and words.

### peasebury · House Peasebury ✎

### rogers · House Rogers ✎
A knightly stormlands house represented by Corwin Rogers, who competed at Ashford in 209 AC.

### swann · House Swann ✎
The Swanns of Stonehelm maintain connections on different sides of the war. Donnel served Renly, changed to Stannis and accepted Joffrey after the Blackwater. Balon joined the Kingsguard and travelled to Dorne to bring Myrcella home.

### swygert · House Swygert ✎
A stormlands house known mainly from supplementary heraldic material. The books provide no detailed family history.

### sweet · House Sweet ✎

### selmy · House Selmy ✎
The Selmys hold Harvest Hall. Barristan relinquished his inheritance for the Kingsguard and served several kings. After Joffrey dismissed him, he went to seek Daenerys. His personal service does not determine the allegiance of the whole house.

### seaworth · House Seaworth ✎
The house began with Davos's relief of besieged Storm's End. He smuggled onions and salt fish through the Redwyne blockade, saving Stannis's garrison from starvation. Stannis rewarded him with knighthood, lands on Cape Wrath and a new name, while cutting off his fingertips for his earlier smuggling. The family holds a modest estate and owes its position to his service.

Four of Davos's sons died at the Blackwater, commanding ships or serving aboard them. Devan remained Stannis's squire, while Marya stayed at home with the younger children. Davos later became Hand and went to White Harbor to seek allies.

Wyman Manderly secretly spared him and offered support in return for bringing Rickon Stark back from Skagos. That mission ties Davos's future service to Stannis's hopes in the North.

### staedmon · House Staedmon ✎
The Staedmons hold Broad Arch. Alesander, called the Pennylover, appears among Stannis's supporters. Little else is recorded about the family's history.

### tudbury · House Tudbury ✎
A minor stormlands house known from supplementary heraldic material. No members appear in the novels.

### tarth · House Tarth ✎

The family holds Evenfall Hall, and its head bears the title Evenstar. Of First Men origin, it has ancient ties to Storm's End. Alyn served in Jaehaerys's Kingsguard, and later marriages connected the house to the Targaryens.

Selwyn has outlived most of his children, leaving Brienne as his heir. Rejecting the role expected of a noblewoman, she served in Renly's guard and after his death swore to Catelyn to find the Stark daughters. Selwyn also supported Renly.

The island's precise allegiance after that king's death is not established in the later novels. Reports of the Golden Company's invasion threaten the family's lands.

### toyne · House Toyne ✎
The house fell after Terrence Toyne, a royal mistress's lover, was executed and his brothers tried to avenge him on Aegon IV. Simon later led the Kingswood Brotherhood and died fighting Barristan. Myles Toyne commanded the Golden Company in Essos.

### trant · House Trant ✎
A stormlands house represented by Meryn Trant, a Kingsguard knight who carries out Joffrey's cruel orders. Little is revealed about the family's own history.

### wylde · House Wylde ✎

### whitehead · House Whitehead ✎
A stormlands house whose precise seat and ruler's position in the War of the Five Kings remain uncertain.

### fell · House Fell ✎
The Fells hold Felwood. Willis served in Aegon II's Kingsguard and helped save Princess Jaehaera. Harwood later joined Stannis's march on Winterfell.

### foote_of_nightsong · House Foote of Nightsong ✎

### hasty · House Hasty ✎
A knightly stormlands family whose holdings have little recorded history.

### horpe · House Horpe ✎
A stormlands house represented by Richard Horpe, a trusted officer of Stannis. He fought at the Blackwater and accompanied the king's northern campaign.

### herston · House Herston ✎
A little-known stormlands house recorded chiefly in supplementary heraldic material.

### errol · House Errol ✎
The Errols hold Haystack Hall. The house still exists during the War of the Five Kings, but its position after the Blackwater is not described in detail.

### estermont · House Estermont ✎
The Estermonts of Greenstone are the Baratheons' maternal relatives. After the Blackwater, some members submitted to the Iron Throne while Lomas and Andrew stayed with Stannis. Andrew helped secretly evacuate Edric Storm from Dragonstone.

## Дорн

### martell · House Martell ✎
The rulers of Sunspear rose through Mors Martell's alliance with Nymeria, who brought Rhoynar refugees to Dorne. Together they subdued local kings; Nymeria continued after Mors's death. The house adopted princely titles and Rhoynish succession through the eldest child regardless of sex. Avoiding open battles with dragons, the Martells survived Aegon's attempts to conquer Dorne. Even Daeron I's victory proved temporary: rebellion restored independence.

Dorne joined the realm through dynastic marriages in 187 AC, retaining local laws and privileges. Old feuds with border houses in the Reach and stormlands persisted. Elia's murder, with her children, during the sack of King's Landing made the Lannisters the family's chief enemies. Doran kept Dorne out of another war while secretly arranging Arianne's marriage to Viserys. After Viserys died, he sent Quentyn to Daenerys.

Myrcella's betrothal to Trystane maintained an outward alliance with the Iron Throne. Oberyn's death intensified demands for revenge; Arianne's failed attempt to crown Myrcella nearly disrupted Doran's plans. Quentyn died in Meereen trying to claim a dragon. In published Winds of Winter chapters, Arianne travels to assess the newly arrived Aegon and his claim.

### yronwood · House Yronwood ✎
The Yronwoods are Dorne's most powerful house after the Martells. Their fortress controls the northern approach through the Boneway, while surrounding lands provide timber, iron and silver. Before Nymeria they ruled much of Dorne as kings; their head still bears the title Bloodroyal. Submission to Sunspear did not end the rivalry.

They repeatedly backed Blackfyre claimants against the throne allied by marriage to the Martells. Later, young Oberyn's duel with Edgar Yronwood ended in the lord's death from an infected wound, provoking suspicions of poison. Doran sent Quentyn to foster with Anders as reconciliation. The prince became close to Cletus and the household's knights.

During the War of the Five Kings, Anders musters troops in the Boneway on Doran's orders. Cletus accompanies Quentyn to Essos but dies on the journey. The marriage mission's failure and Quentyn's death leave the alliance without its intended reward, although no formal break between the houses has been declared.

### allyrion · House Allyrion ✎
The Allyrions hold Godsgrace. The Martells were once their vassals. Daemon Sand, Ryon Allyrion's bastard son, accompanies Arianne and assists her negotiations.

### blackmont · House Blackmont ✎
Former Dornish kings subdued by Nymeria, whose last king Benedict was sent to the Wall. Larra later heads the house and accompanies Oberyn to King's Landing.

### vaith · House Vaith ✎
The Vaiths hold the Red Dunes. During the Targaryen wars, they abandoned their castle ahead of invasion and returned after the occupiers left. Cassella Vaith became one of Daeron I's Dornish hostages.

### wyl · House Wyl ✎
The Wyls hold a fortress on the Boneway and are known for cruelty towards captives. In the First Dornish War, they imprisoned Orys and cut off his hand before releasing him. A later Lord Wyl confined Aemon the Dragonknight above a pit of snakes until Baelor rescued him.

### gargalen · House Gargalen ✎
The Gargalens hold Salt Shore. Tremond accompanies Oberyn Martell to King's Landing; little is revealed about the family's estates.

### dalt · House Dalt ✎
The Dalts are the knights of Lemonwood. Deziel accompanies Oberyn to King's Landing, while Andrey joins Arianne's plot to crown Myrcella. Doran sends Andrey to serve in Norvos as punishment.

### dayne · House Dayne ✎
The Daynes hold Starfall at the mouth of the Torrentine. Legend says their founder built the castle where he found a fallen star, from whose stone Dawn was forged. The white blade is comparable in strength to Valyrian steel but has a different origin. It is entrusted to a knight judged worthy of the title Sword of the Morning, rather than automatically inherited by the house's ruler. The Daynes were once kings before submitting to the Martells.

Arthur, the last known Sword of the Morning, served Aerys II's Kingsguard and died at the tower of joy. Eddard returned Dawn to Starfall. Ashara soon died after leaping from a tower, though much about her private history remains uncertain.

Young Edric serves Beric Dondarrion and travels with the Brotherhood Without Banners. The High Hermitage branch holds a separate castle: Gerold, Darkstar, joined Arianne's conspiracy, was accused of attacking Myrcella and fled. Doran sends men after him. Edric's whereabouts after the brotherhood's change of leadership remain unrevealed.

### dayne_of_high_hermitage · House Dayne of High Hermitage ✎
A Dayne cadet branch sworn to Starfall. Gerold, called Darkstar, joined Arianne's conspiracy and fled after Myrcella was wounded. Doran sends men to find him; the accusation against him is central to their mission.

### jordayne · House Jordayne ✎
The Jordaynes of the Tor were once independent kings. Targaryen forces occupied their castle during the First Dornish War, and Visenya later burned it. Tremond rules in the period of the main novels.

### drinkwater · House Drinkwater ✎
A knightly house serving the Yronwoods. Gerris Drinkwater accompanies Quentyn to Essos and survives the prince's fatal attempt to claim a dragon.

### qorgyle · House Qorgyle ✎
The Qorgyles hold Sandstone, where Dornish rebels killed the conquerors' governor Lyonel Tyrell by putting scorpions in his bed. A later Qorgyle commanded the Night's Watch immediately before Jeor Mormont.

### ladybright · House Ladybright ✎
A Dornish house sworn to the Martells. Alyse Ladybright manages Doran's treasury; Jayne accompanies Arianne in published chapters of The Winds of Winter.

### manwoody · House Manwoody ✎
The Manwoodys hold Kingsgrave. Nymeria defeated their last independent king, Albin, and sent him to the Wall. The house often fought the lords of the Dornish Marches.

### santagar · House Santagar ✎
The Santagars of Spottswood are knights and principal Sunspear bannermen. Aron was the Red Keep's master-at-arms and died in the King's Landing riot. Sylva joined Arianne's conspiracy and was subsequently married to Eldon Estermont.

### toland · House Toland ✎
The Tolands hold Ghost Hill. During the Conquest their lord deceived Aegon by sending a fool to face him while escaping himself. The family commemorated the trick with new arms: a dragon biting its own tail. Theora Toland later dreams of dragons.

### uller · House Uller ✎
The Ullers hold Hellholt, where a defender's scorpion brought down Meraxes during the First Dornish War. Rhaenys died or disappeared after the fall. Targaryen dragons repeatedly ravaged Uller lands without ending Dornish independence. Ellaria Sand belongs to this family.

### wells_dorne · House Wells (Dorne) ✎
The Dornish Wells branch is represented by Willam Wells. He accompanied Quentyn to Essos and died when their ship was attacked.

### fowler · House Fowler ✎
The Fowlers of Skyreach guard the Prince's Pass. Once Kings of Stone and Sky, they supported Nymeria against the Yronwoods and long fought the marcher lords. Franklyn later holds an army in the pass on Doran's orders.

## Железные острова

### greyjoy · House Greyjoy ✎
The Lords of Pyke became rulers of the Iron Islands after the Conquest. Aegon destroyed the Hoares and allowed the islanders to choose a new lord paramount: Vickon Greyjoy. The family retained the Drowned God and traditions of raiding, though some rulers encouraged mainland trade. Dalton, the Red Kraken, exploited the Dance to plunder the westerlands and capture Fair Isle.

Much later, Quellon sought closer ties with the realm, but Balon returned to the Old Way. His rebellion against Robert ended with his fleet destroyed and Pyke stormed. Two sons died, and Theon became a hostage at Winterfell. During the War of the Five Kings, Balon again declared independence but attacked the North instead of allying with Robb. After his death, Aeron called a kingsmoot. Asha offered peace and northern lands, Victarion continued war, and Euron promised dragons and all Westeros.

Euron won, seized the Shield Islands and turned his fleet against the Reach. Victarion sailed to Daenerys with a dragon horn, intending to serve his own interests. Asha and Theon are in Stannis's hands; the struggle over Balon's inheritance remains unsettled.

### hoare · House Hoare ✎
The Hoares expanded ironborn rule far beyond the islands. Some favoured trade and mainland settlement over the Old Way, drawing opposition from priests of the Drowned God. Harwyn Hardhand exploited divisions among the riverlords, defeated the Storm King and conquered the Trident. His successors ruled their enlarged kingdom from the mainland.

Harren the Black spent decades building Harrenhal, exhausting the riverlands through taxation and forced labour. It was completed on the day Aegon landed. Harren trusted its massive walls, but Balerion burned him and his sons inside the Kingspyre Tower.

After the dynasty's destruction, the riverlords received their own overlord in House Tully, while the islanders chose Vickon Greyjoy. Harren's brother, then Lord Commander of the Night's Watch, remained at the Wall and made no claim to the inheritance.

### ironmaker · House Ironmaker ✎
Erik Ironmaker, an elderly warrior, stood for the crown at the kingsmoot. After Euron's victory he became castellan of Pyke. Euron married him to the absent Asha by proxy, seeking to restrict her political choices.

### blacktyde · House Blacktyde ✎
The Blacktydes rule their namesake island. Baelor grew up as a hostage in Oldtown and adopted the Faith of the Seven. He favoured Asha at the kingsmoot and refused to recognise Euron afterwards. The new king's men captured and killed him.

### botley · House Botley ✎
The Botleys hold Lordsport. Euron drowned Sawane for challenging his rule, then passed the lands to Sawane's brother Germund over his sons. Tristifer remained loyal to Asha, while Germund supported the new king.

### wynch · House Wynch ✎
The Wynches hold Iron Holt. Waldon was among the first to recognise Euron and received half the Botley lands. In an earlier island conflict, Alester Wynch died quarrelling with Gunthor Goodbrother.

### volmark · House Volmark ✎
A Harlaw family descended from the Hoares. Maron Volmark was mentioned as a potential heir to the ancient kings, but supported Victarion at the kingsmoot. Euron subsequently granted him Greenshield.

### goodbrother · House Goodbrother ✎
A wealthy Great Wyk house with several cadet branches. Gorold brought substantial forces to Balon. He first favoured Victarion at the kingsmoot, then recognised Euron and took part in the conquest of the Shield Islands.

### goodbrother_of_shatterstone · House Goodbrother of Shatterstone ✎
The Shatterstone branch of the Goodbrothers. Lord Norne supported Victarion at the kingsmoot.

### goodbrother_of_downdelving · House Goodbrother of Downdelving ✎
A Great Wyk Goodbrother branch holding Downdelving and subordinate to the family's senior line.

### goodbrother_of_crow_spike_keep · House Goodbrother of Crow Spike Keep ✎
A Goodbrother branch of Great Wyk. Its individual members have no developed history in the books.

### goodbrother_of_corpse_lake · House Goodbrother of Corpse Lake ✎
A cadet Goodbrother branch on Great Wyk, without an independent story in the novels.

### goodbrother_of_orkmont · House Goodbrother of Orkmont ✎
The Orkmont Goodbrothers attended the kingsmoot. Their branch's origins and estates remain little known.

### drumm · House Drumm ✎
The Drumms of Old Wyk possess the Valyrian steel sword Red Rain, acquired through an ancestor's trickery. Dunstan claimed the crown at the kingsmoot and later joined Euron's campaign against the Shield Islands.

### kenning_of_harlaw · House Kenning of Harlaw ✎
Former rivals of the Harlaws, the Kennings became their vassals after defeat. The western Kennings of Kayce descend from this family. Lord Kenning supported Victarion at the kingsmoot.

### codd · House Codd ✎
The Codds' lowly origins earn them contempt from older ironborn nobility. Lucas supported Euron. Dagon served in the Moat Cailin garrison and was killed for refusing to surrender.

### myre · House Myre ✎
Once enemies of the Harlaws, the Myres became their bannermen. Some favoured Asha, while Pinchface Jon brought ships to Euron and supported him at the kingsmoot.

### merlyn · House Merlyn ✎
A principal house of Great Wyk. Meldred Merlyn supported Asha at the kingsmoot.

### netley · House Netley ✎
An ironborn house whose members appear among Victarion's supporters at the kingsmoot. Its earlier history is unrevealed.

### orkwood · House Orkwood ✎
An old Orkmont house that helped overthrow the Greyirons. Alyn Orkwood brought twenty longships to the kingsmoot and supported Euron.

### sunderly · House Sunderly ✎

### farwynd_of_sealskin_point · House Farwynd of Sealskin Point ✎

### farwynd_of_the_lonely_light · House Farwynd of the Lonely Light ✎
The Farwynds rule Lonely Light, the archipelago's remotest island. Gylbert claimed the crown at the kingsmoot and proposed sailing far west to a land without hunger or death. Most of the ironborn rejected his promises.

### humble · House Humble ✎
Descended from thralls and salt wives, the Humbles are despised by older ironborn nobility. Quellon supported Euron. Adrack accepted Ramsay's promise of safe passage from Moat Cailin, only to be flayed with the rest of the surrendered garrison.

### harlaw · House Harlaw ✎
The Harlaws dominate their populous, relatively wealthy island. Multiple castles and cadet branches support the senior lord at Ten Towers; land and trade give the family influence the Greyjoys cannot ignore. Rodrik the Reader prefers books to raiding and doubts that reviving the Old Way can avoid a ruinous mainland war.

His sister Alannys married Balon, making Asha and Theon's fate a particular concern. Rodrik backed Asha at the kingsmoot and her proposal to secure northern gains through peace. After Euron won, he submitted but continued questioning the king's dangerous schemes, including his pursuit of dragons.

Harras, wielder of the Valyrian steel sword Nightfall, helped conquer the Shields and received Greyshield. Removing him from Harlaw complicated succession to Ten Towers. Other branches retain their own interests, and submission to Euron does not mean that the family agrees on his plans.

### harlaw_of_the_tower_of_glimmering · House Harlaw of the Tower of Glimmering ✎
Hotho Humpback's branch shifted its support among Asha, Victarion and Euron. Once Harras received Greyshield, Hotho gained the prospect of inheriting Ten Towers.

### harlaw_of_grey_garden · House Harlaw of Grey Garden ✎
Harras Harlaw, a knight wielding Nightfall, heads this branch. Rodrik named him heir to Ten Towers, but Euron granted him Greyshield after the islands' conquest, drawing him away from Harlaw.

### harlaw_of_harlaw_hall · House Harlaw of Harlaw Hall ✎
Sigfryd Harlaw's branch holds the family's former seat. Its banner appeared among Asha's supporters before the kingsmoot.

Boremund Harlaw's cadet branch supported Asha before the kingsmoot.

### shepherd · House Shepherd ✎
An ironborn house whose members appear in Victarion's following at the kingsmoot.

## Королевские земли

### targaryen · House Targaryen ✎
Valyrian dragonlords who settled on Dragonstone twelve years before the Doom. Family tradition says Aenar left Valyria after Daenys had a prophetic dream. A century later, Aegon and his sisters conquered most of Westeros; Dorne joined much later through a marriage agreement. The Targaryens made King's Landing their capital and ruled for almost three centuries. They retained the Valyrian practice of marrying close relatives; Jaehaerys won the Faith's acceptance through the Doctrine of Exceptionalism.

Dragons underpinned their supremacy, but the succession dispute between Rhaenyra and Aegon II led to the Dance, killing many family members and almost all their dragons. After the last dragon died, kings depended more heavily on marriages and vassals. Aegon IV's legitimised bastards brought another dynastic conflict: the Blackfyre Rebellions. Robert's Rebellion and Aerys II's murder ended the ruling line's hold on the throne.

Viserys and Daenerys escaped to Essos. Viserys died; Daenerys hatched three dragons and conquered Meereen. Meanwhile, a young man claiming to be Rhaegar's surviving son Aegon landed in Westeros. His identity remains unconfirmed, but the Golden Company is already fighting for his claim.

### bywater · House Bywater ✎
A crownlands family represented by Jacelyn Bywater, called Ironhand after losing a hand in the assault on Pyke. Tyrion put him in charge of the City Watch. At the Blackwater, his own men killed him when he tried to prevent their flight.

### buckwell · House Buckwell ✎
The Buckwells hold the Antlers. Their lord fled to Pentos as Maegor's rule collapsed. Jarmen later served in the Night's Watch and died during the expedition beyond the Wall.

### bar_emmon · House Bar Emmon ✎
An Andal family of Sharp Point. The Bar Emmons supported Jaehaerys against Maegor and later served Dragonstone. Young Duram swore allegiance to Stannis in the War of the Five Kings.

### baratheon_of_kings_landing · House Baratheon of King's Landing ✎
Robert's royal branch arose from his victory in the rebellion and marriage to Cersei Lannister. It held the Iron Throne, capital and crownlands, while his brothers received Storm's End and Dragonstone. Robert relied on the victorious coalition and left much of government to Jon Arryn.

After his death, Joffrey succeeded him. All three of Cersei's children were actually Jaime's, and Stannis rejected their claims. Eddard's execution and competing kingships brought war. The Lannister-Tyrell victory at the Blackwater preserved Joffrey's throne, but he was poisoned at his wedding. Tommen inherited and married Margaery.

Under the boy king, Cersei, the Tyrells and the Faith Militant compete for power. Tywin and Kevan's deaths remove the court's former leadership. The branch retains the Baratheon name while depending on Lannister strength and its allies, with the disputed succession still driving the war.

### baratheon_of_dragonstone · House Baratheon of Dragonstone ✎
Stannis received Dragonstone after the Targaryens' fall, along with its island bannermen and responsibility for the former dynasty's stronghold. He considered the grant a slight because Robert gave their ancestral Storm's End to Renly. After Robert died, Stannis declared Cersei's children illegitimate and claimed the throne. Renly refused to yield; his death at Melisandre's shadow gave Stannis part of his army. The Blackwater then destroyed most of Stannis's fleet and forces.

Instead of renewing the assault on the capital, he relieved the Night's Watch, defeated the wildlings and sought northern allies. Restoring Deepwood Motte to the Glovers strengthened his position before he marched on Winterfell. Selyse and Shireen remained at the Wall.

Shireen is the branch's heir. Dragonstone's fall leaves the family without its original stronghold or most of its island possessions.

### byrch · House Byrch ✎
A crownlands family whose lord remained among Maegor's last supporters before Jaehaerys's accession.

### blount · House Blount ✎
A crownlands house best known through Kingsguard knight Boros Blount. Restored after a temporary dismissal, he became Tommen's food taster.

### blackberry · House Blackberry ✎
A little-known house associated with service to Stannis. Whether its lands belong to the crownlands or stormlands has not been established.

### blackfyre · House Blackfyre ✎
This Targaryen cadet house took its name from Blackfyre, the Valyrian steel sword Aegon IV gave Daemon before legitimising his bastards. Daemon's supporters treated the gift as a sign of preference over Daeron II; resentment of Dornish influence helped rally rebels.

The first rebellion ended at the Redgrass Field in 196 AC, where Bloodraven's archers killed Daemon and his eldest twins. Aegor Rivers took the survivors to Essos and founded the Golden Company to preserve the exiles' strength. Daemon's descendants made four further attempts to return, from the Whitewalls conspiracy to the War of the Ninepenny Kings. None won the throne.

Barristan Selmy killed Maelys in the Stepstones in 260 AC, ending the known male line. The fate of female descendants remains unrevealed. The Golden Company outlived its former claimants and later backed a youth calling himself Aegon Targaryen, but that support does not establish Blackfyre descent.

### boggs · House Boggs ✎
A Crackclaw Point house loyal to the Targaryens. Its men fought for Rhaegar at the Trident. This family is distinct from the Boggs of the Neck.

### brune_of_brownhollow · House Brune of Brownhollow ✎
The Brunes of Brownhollow are landed knights on Crackclaw Point. Lothor serves Baelish and hopes for a riverlands castle. His personal service does not place all Brunes under Petyr's authority.

### brune_of_the_dyre_den · House Brune of the Dyre Den ✎
The Brunes hold the Dyre Den on Crackclaw Point. Their ancestors swore directly to Visenya, without a local overlord. They fought for Rhaegar at the Trident, and Eustace's descendants later participated in the War of the Five Kings.

### velaryon · House Velaryon ✎
A Valyrian house of Driftmark, settled in Westeros before the Targaryens. The Velaryons became known for seafaring rather than dragons: their ships supported the Conquest, and family members often served as master of ships. Intermarriage brought the houses closer. Their greatest power came under Corlys, the Sea Snake, whose voyages yielded immense wealth and funded High Tide and trading towns on Driftmark.

Corlys fought beside Daemon in the Stepstones. His marriage to Rhaenys and those of their heirs made the family Rhaenyra's main naval support during the Dance. Their fleet blockaded Blackwater Bay. The Battle of the Gullet cost them nearly a third of their ships; the Triarchy sacked High Tide and Spicetown, and Jacaerys died. Afterwards, Alyn rebuilt their naval strength, helping recover Fair Isle and campaigning in the Stepstones.

Later Velaryons lacked that wealth but continued serving the crown. Monford backed Stannis in the War of the Five Kings and died on the Blackwater, leaving a young heir. The family's bastard Aurane Waters received command of Cersei's new royal fleet. After her arrest, he fled with the ships. A pirate styling himself Lord of the Waters appears in a published Winds of Winter chapter, but his identification as Aurane remains unconfirmed.

### wendwater · House Wendwater ✎
A crownlands family associated with the Wendwater river. Its estates are described in supplementary material, but its members do not appear in the novels.

### gaunt · House Gaunt ✎
A crownlands house represented by Gwayne Gaunt, a knight of Aerys II's Kingsguard. Symon Hollard killed him when the king was taken captive at Duskendale.

### dargood · House Dargood ✎
A Duskendale family possibly descended from the Darklyns. It survived the reprisals after the Defiance and remained in the town; its subsequent allegiance is not explicitly named.

### darkwood · House Darkwood ✎
Distant Darklyn relatives of Duskendale who survived the senior house's destruction. Their precise position after the Defiance has not been revealed.

### darke · House Darke ✎
A possible Darklyn cadet family. Jonquil Darke served as Queen Alysanne's sworn shield. The family's kinship and later history are incompletely known.

### darklyn · House Darklyn ✎
The Darklyns ruled Duskendale and repeatedly supplied knights to the Kingsguard. Denys imprisoned Aerys II to obtain concessions for the town. After Barristan rescued the king, the family was executed and its lands given to the Rykkers. The Defiance greatly deepened Aerys's mistrust.

### cargyll · House Cargyll ✎
The house is best known through the Kingsguard twins Arryk and Erryk. They chose opposing sides in the Dance and killed each other at Dragonstone. The family became extinct later, sometime between the Ashford tournament and the main novels; the exact date is unknown.

### cave · House Cave ✎
A Crackclaw Point family that, like its neighbours, considers itself sworn directly to the Iron Throne rather than through an intermediate lord.

### kettleblack · House Kettleblack ✎
A sellsword family raised by Cersei's favour. Osmund entered the Kingsguard, Osfryd commanded the City Watch and Osney participated in the plot against Margaery. Their father Oswell was already serving Baelish. Osney's confession brought the brothers into custody.

### cressey · House Cressey ✎
A minor crownlands family whose independent history is scarcely described in the books.

### crabb · House Crabb ✎
A Crackclaw Point house whose former stronghold, the Whispers, lies ruined. Nimble Dick Crabb guides Brienne there and is killed by Shagwell. The family's men had earlier fought for Rhaegar at the Trident.

### langward · House Langward ✎
A crownlands family represented by Harrold Langward, a knight of Maegor's Kingsguard. Gyles Morrigen killed him during the transfer of power to Jaehaerys.

### largent · House Largent ✎
Luthor Largent's loyalty to Rhaenyra brought the family its prominence. As commander of the gold cloaks, he helped her take King's Landing but later died in its riots. Whether he was the family's last member is unknown.

### longwaters · House Longwaters ✎
A small family descended from Jon Waters, the bastard son of Alyn Velaryon and Elaena Targaryen. Jon's legitimate son added "Long" to distinguish his surname from a bastard's. Rennifer Longwaters serves as the Red Keep's chief undergaoler and likes to recall his royal ancestry.

### mallery · House Mallery ✎
A crownlands house whose members partly changed allegiance to Jaehaerys during Maegor's fall. Lothar Mallery later drowned in the Battle of the Mummer's Ford.

### massey · House Massey ✎
The ancient Masseys of Stonedance were once kings. They supported Aegon against their overlord Argilac during the Conquest. Justin Massey later serves Stannis and receives orders to sail overseas to hire soldiers.

### manning · House Manning ✎
A little-known crownlands family whose members do not appear in the novels. Its maritime arms are insufficient evidence for a precise location of its lands.

### pyle · House Pyle ✎
A crownlands house represented by Howard Pyle at the Ashford tournament of 209 AC. Few other details of its history are known.

### pyne · House Pyne ✎
A Crackclaw Point family whose members and holdings have no developed history in the books.

### rambton · House Rambton ✎
The family resisted the new religion on Dragonstone. Hubard Rambton and his sons defended the sept against Selyse's men and were killed. Two surviving sons were later burned as traitors.

### rykker · House Rykker ✎
The Rykkers received Duskendale after the Darklyns' destruction in 277 AC. Jaremy fought for Aerys but was sent to the Night's Watch after King's Landing fell. A northern army later suffered a crushing defeat while attempting to attack the town.

### rollingford · House Rollingford ✎
As Maegor fell, Lord Rollingford fled to Pentos and later sought Jaehaerys's pardon. Herman Rollingford subsequently died in a duel in Braavos.

### rosby · House Rosby ✎
A wealthy house near King's Landing that submitted to Aegon without battle. Gyles Rosby served as master of coin and died without a settled inheritance. His ward and several claimants dispute the estate, while the crown hopes to take its revenues.

### sunglass · House Sunglass ✎
The Sunglasses of Sweetport Sound backed Stannis until the burning of the Seven's holy places. Guncer opposed Melisandre, was imprisoned and later burned. His brother fled to Volantis; the family's galleys remained in Stannis's fleet.

### celtigar · House Celtigar ✎
The Celtigars of Claw Isle are of Valyrian origin and have longstanding ties to Dragonstone, though they were not dragonlords. Island estates and trade supplied their wealth. Stories of the castle's treasures include a Valyrian steel axe and a horn said to summon sea monsters. They claim Crackclaw Point, whose lords reject their authority and insist on direct allegiance to the crown.

Bartimos supported Rhaenyra in the Dance. As her master of coin in occupied King's Landing, he imposed heavy taxes; a mob killed him and close relatives during the unrest.

Adrian supported Stannis in the War of the Five Kings but was captured at the Blackwater and recognised Joffrey. He later testified against Tyrion. His defeat did not bring the island's plunder: Stannis rejected his followers' proposal to seize its wealth.

### scales · House Scales ✎
A minor family associated with Dragonstone. Its independent history is scarcely described.

### stokeworth · House Stokeworth ✎
The Stokeworths hold a castle near King's Landing that supplies the capital with food. Lollys inherited after Tanda's death. Her husband Bronn eliminated Balman, while Falyse sought Cersei's help and disappeared into Qyburn's custody. Bronn's position in the household became much stronger.

### staunton · House Staunton ✎
The Stauntons hold Rook's Rest. During Maegor's fall they helped transfer the Red Keep to Jaehaerys. A later lord and his family died of the Shivers, but the house survived.

### thorne · House Thorne ✎
A crownlands family represented by Rickard Thorne, who died trying to save Prince Maelor during the Dance. Alliser defended King's Landing for Aerys, chose the Night's Watch after defeat and became an opponent of Jon Snow.

### farring · House Farring ✎
A house serving Stannis. Gilbert holds Storm's End while the king campaigns in the North. Godry Farring accompanies that campaign and bears the name Giantslayer.

### follard · House Follard ✎
A crownlands family represented by Dick Follard, killed defending Castle Black, and Perkin, who serves among Stannis's queen's men. Their personal service does not establish the whole family's allegiance.

### hardy · House Hardy ✎
A Crackclaw Point house associated with local tales of Lucifer Hardy, a warrior who sought peace among neighbouring lords.

### harte · House Harte ✎
A crownlands family whose lord was executed for refusing to support Aegon II. Elwood Harte later fought for Joffrey at the Blackwater.

### hayford · House Hayford ✎
A house sworn directly to the Iron Throne. An earlier Hayford served as Daeron II's Hand and died in the first Blackfyre rebellion. During the War of the Five Kings, the young heiress Ermesande is married to Tyrek Lannister. His disappearance leaves the management of her inheritance unresolved.

### hogg · House Hogg ✎
Landed knights serving the Hayfords. Jon Hogg fought for Maegor against the Faith Militant. During the War of the Five Kings, both sides attacked Roger Hogg's lands.

### hollard · House Hollard ✎
Longstanding Darklyn allies, the Hollards were almost entirely executed after the Defiance of Duskendale and their castle destroyed. Barristan obtained mercy for young Dontos. He became Joffrey's fool, helped Sansa escape and was murdered on Baelish's orders, ending the family.

### chelsted · House Chelsted ✎
A crownlands house represented by Qarlton Chelsted. As Aerys II's Hand he tried to stop the plan to burn King's Landing. Aerys burned him instead and gave the office to the pyromancer Rossart.

### chyttering · House Chyttering ✎
A crownlands house that supported Stannis during the War of the Five Kings. Its family history is scarcely recorded.

### edgerton · House Edgerton ✎
A little-known crownlands house whose members have not appeared in the published novels.
