## «Дом Дракона»: Таргариены

### jaehaerys1 · Jaehaerys the Conciliator

### viserys1 · Viserys I
House of the Dragon makes Aegon the Conqueror's prophetic dream a secret Viserys passes to Rhaenyra. Alicent mistakes his dying references to Aegon for a wish to crown their son. Fire & Blood contains neither that exchange nor that explanation for her decision to support Aegon II.

### rhaenyra · Rhaenyra
The adaptation makes Rhaenyra and Alicent childhood friends of similar age; in Fire & Blood, Alicent is nine years older. Their clandestine meeting in a King's Landing sept in season two is also a television invention, bringing their former friendship into the struggle over the crown.

### daemon · Daemon
The show makes Daemon responsible for Rhea Royce's fatal fall from her horse. In the book she is injured while hunting and dies nine days later, while he is away in the Stepstones. His season-two visions at Harrenhal are another addition to the adaptation.

### aemma · Aemma Arryn
The adaptation shows Viserys authorizing an operation to deliver Aemma's child without her consent. Fire & Blood records her death in childbirth but does not describe that procedure or decision. In both accounts the infant Baelon survives her only briefly.

### aegon2 · Aegon II

### helaena · Helaena
The show gives Helaena prophetic visions. Its Blood and Cheese scene concerns the twins, whereas the book forces her to choose between Jaehaerys and Maelor. Season three places her pregnancy before her fatal fall from the Red Keep, changing the chronology of the youngest child's story.

### aemond · Aemond One-Eye
At Storm's End the show presents Lucerys's death as the result of Vhagar escaping Aemond's control. Fire & Blood records his pursuit without revealing his thoughts. At Rook's Rest, the adaptation also has him deliberately turn Vhagar's fire on Aegon and Sunfyre.

### daeron_d · Daeron the Daring

### rhaenys_q · Rhaenys, the Queen Who Never Was
Rhaenys's escape through the Dragonpit floor during Aegon II's coronation is invented for the show. The Great Council is simplified too: the adaptation presents her as Viserys's rival, while the decisive book vote is between Viserys and her son Laenor.

### baela · Baela

### rhaena_d · Rhaena
The adaptation merges Nettles's dragonriding story into Rhaena's and connects her with Sheepstealer. In Fire & Blood she stays in the Vale, where Morning hatches from a Syrax egg. Giving her a wild adult dragon changes her role during the Dance.

### jacaerys · Jacaerys Velaryon
The show stages Jacaerys's agreement with Cregan Stark at the Wall. In Fire & Blood he visits Winterfell and concludes the Pact of Ice and Fire, including a promised future marriage between the houses. Mushroom's account of Sara Snow is absent from that television meeting.

## «Дом Дракона»: Веларионы

### corlys · Corlys Velaryon, the Sea Snake
The adaptation makes Vaemond Corlys's brother rather than his nephew. It also identifies Corlys as Addam and Alyn's father. The book leaves their parentage disputed: Marilda names Laenor, while Mushroom attributes the boys to Corlys.

### vaemond · Vaemond Velaryon
The show makes Vaemond Corlys's brother and has Daemon kill him before Viserys during the succession hearing. In the book he is Corlys's nephew; Rhaenyra orders his death after he challenges her sons' legitimacy, and Syrax receives his body. Daemon carries out the killing in both accounts.

### laena · Laena Velaryon
The show gives Laena a death by dragonfire at her own request after a disastrous labour. Fire & Blood instead has her succumb to childbed fever. She tries to reach Vhagar but collapses on the steps, and Daemon carries her back to bed.

### laenor · Laenor Velaryon
The adaptation has Laenor escape overseas with Qarl Correy after a staged death arranged with Rhaenyra and Daemon. In Fire & Blood, Qarl kills him during a quarrel at Spicetown. Daemon's involvement in a hired murder is one chronicler's theory, not an established fact.

### addam · Addam Velaryon

### alyn · Alyn Velaryon, Oakenfist
The show openly makes Alyn and Addam Corlys's sons and develops their fraught relationship with him. Fire & Blood preserves competing accounts of their father: Marilda names Laenor, while Mushroom claims Corlys. The chronicle does not settle the question.

## «Дом Дракона»: другие дома

### alicent · Alicent Hightower
The show begins with Alicent as Rhaenyra's childhood friend. It adds her relationship with Criston Cole and her season-two proposal to surrender King's Landing to Rhaenyra. Those scenes are absent from Fire & Blood, whose account of the queens' political opposition develops differently.

### otto · Otto Hightower

### gwayne · Gwayne Hightower
The adaptation sends Gwayne on campaign with Criston Cole and later places him with the Hightower army at Tumbleton. In Fire & Blood he serves the City Watch in King's Landing and is killed when Rhaenyra's supporters take the capital.

### ormund_h · Ormund Hightower
Fire & Blood has Roderick Dustin kill Ormund at the First Battle of Tumbleton. The season-three finale instead ends their fight with Ulf and Silverwing burning both men. The show keeps their confrontation but changes how it ends.

### jason_l · Jason Lannister
Fire & Blood has Jason receive his mortal wound at the Red Fork. The season-three premiere instead introduces the aftermath of the western army's defeat through Roderick Dustin presenting Jason's severed head. His death is relocated and shown through a different encounter.

### borros · Borros Baratheon

### cregan · Cregan Stark

### jeyne · Jeyne Arryn, the Maiden of the Vale

### alysanne_bw · Alysanne Blackwood
The show gives Black Aly the three arrows that kill Criston Cole in season three. In Fire & Blood, Cole dies under the archers' fire at the Butcher's Ball; the killing is attributed to Robb Rivers and his bowmen, rather than Alysanne.

### oscar · Oscar Tully
The adaptation makes Oscar Grover's grandson and successor as Lord of Riverrun, responsible for rallying the riverlords behind Daemon. In the book he is Grover's great-grandson, Elmo's younger son and Kermit's brother. The show combines responsibilities held by several Tullys.

## «Игра престолов»: Таргариены

### aerys2 · Aerys the Mad King

### rhaegar · Rhaegar
Game of Thrones confirms an annulment of Rhaegar's marriage to Elia and a secret wedding to Lyanna, making Jon their legitimate son, Aegon. The published novels have not confirmed that annulment, wedding or Jon's parentage directly.

### viserys3 · Viserys, the Beggar King

### daenerys · Daenerys Stormborn
The show takes Daenerys to Westeros, through the war against the dead and the destruction of King's Landing, before Jon kills her. The published novels stop in the Dothraki sea after her flight from Meereen on Drogon; those later events remain outside the book narrative.

### jon · Jon Snow
The show identifies Jon as Rhaegar and Lyanna's legitimate son, named Aegon. After killing Daenerys he is sent to the Night's Watch and leaves with the free folk beyond the Wall. The novels have not directly resolved his parentage or the outcome of the attack by his sworn brothers.

### aemon_m · Maester Aemon

### brynden · Brynden Rivers, Bloodraven
The show calls Bran's mentor the Three-Eyed Raven without explicitly identifying him as Brynden Rivers. He is killed in the Night King's attack on the cave. In the novels Brynden is alive and teaching Bran; the show's Night King has no equivalent role in the published story.

### aegon_r · Aegon
The show omits Young Griff, who claims in the books to be Rhaegar and Elia's surviving son. It treats that child as dead in the sack of King's Landing. The name Aegon is instead given to Jon, Rhaegar and Lyanna's son, who is a different child.

## «Игра престолов»: Старки

### eddard · Eddard Stark

### catelyn · Catelyn Tully
Game of Thrones ends Catelyn's story at the Red Wedding. In the books, Beric gives his remaining life to bring her back. As Lady Stoneheart she leads part of the Brotherhood Without Banners and pursues those involved in the massacre, a storyline omitted from the adaptation.

### robb · Robb Stark
The show replaces Jeyne Westerling with Talisa, a healer from Volantis. Pregnant Talisa accompanies Robb to the Red Wedding and is killed there. In the book Jeyne stays at Riverrun and survives him, leaving a different history for their marriage and its aftermath.

### sansa · Sansa Stark
The show gives Sansa much of Jeyne Poole's role by marrying her to Ramsay. The books keep Sansa in the Vale as Alayne Stone, while Jeyne is passed off as Arya. Sansa's later coronation as queen of an independent North is a television outcome the published novels have not reached.

### arya · Arya Stark
The show brings Arya back to Westeros, where she kills Walder Frey and the Night King before sailing west. In the published novels she is still training with the Faceless Men in Braavos. Those killings and her voyage are not part of her published book history.

### bran · Bran Stark
The television ending makes Bran king of the Six Kingdoms after Daenerys's death, with the North independent. The books still have him learning from Brynden Rivers beyond the Wall. Neither his coronation nor that political settlement has occurred in the published narrative.

### rickon · Rickon Stark
The show has Ramsay capture Rickon and kill him before the Battle of the Bastards. In the books, Wex reports that Rickon and Osha went to Skagos. Wyman Manderly sends Davos to recover him, but the result of that mission remains unknown.

### benjen · Benjen Stark
The adaptation brings Benjen back after the children of the forest save him, then has him sacrifice himself to help Jon escape the dead. The books leave him missing after a ranging. They do not establish him as Coldhands, whose assistance to Bran partly passes to Benjen in the show.

### lyanna · Lyanna Stark
The show reveals Lyanna's secret marriage to Rhaegar and the birth of their son at the tower of joy. The books give Eddard memories of her death and his promise, but have not directly disclosed that promise's content or Jon's parentage.

## «Игра престолов»: Баратеоны

### robert · Robert Baratheon

### stannis · Stannis Baratheon
The show has Stannis authorize Shireen's burning, lose to the Boltons and die at Brienne's hands. In the published novels he is still campaigning near Winterfell, while Shireen remains at Castle Black. Ramsay's letter claims that he is dead, but the claim lacks independent confirmation.

### renly · Renly Baratheon

### shireen · Shireen Baratheon
The adaptation has Shireen burned as a sacrifice with Stannis's consent in his army's camp. The published novels leave her at Castle Black with Selyse and Melisandre. Her death has not yet occurred in the book narrative.

### joffrey_b · Joffrey Baratheon

### myrcella · Myrcella Baratheon
The show has Ellaria poison Myrcella with a farewell kiss before she leaves Dorne. In the books she survives Arianne's failed attempt to crown her, losing an ear during Darkstar's attack. She remains alive at the end of the published narrative.

### tommen · Tommen Baratheon
The adaptation has Tommen kill himself after the destruction of the Great Sept and Margaery's death. The much younger book Tommen remains king. The sept's destruction and his suicide have not occurred in the novels.

### gendry · Gendry
The show gives Gendry part of Edric Storm's story, taking him to Stannis for his royal blood. Daenerys later legitimizes him and grants him Storm's End. In the books he works at the inn at the crossroads and receives neither that title nor formal recognition.

## «Игра престолов»: Ланнистеры

### tywin · Tywin Lannister

### kevan · Kevan Lannister
The adaptation kills Kevan in Cersei's explosion at the Great Sept. In the book Varys murders him in the Red Keep because his competent government threatens the plans for Aegon's return. The circumstances and the person responsible are different.

### cersei · Cersei Lannister
The show has Cersei destroy the Great Sept, take the throne after Tommen's death and die with Jaime beneath the Red Keep's ruins. The published novels leave her facing trial after her imprisonment by the Faith. The explosion, reign and death belong to the adaptation's later story.

### jaime · Jaime Lannister
The show sends Jaime to fight the dead at Winterfell before returning to Cersei and dying beside her in King's Landing. In the novels their relationship has deteriorated. His last appearance has him follow Brienne towards a meeting with Lady Stoneheart.

### tyrion · Tyrion Lannister
The show makes Tyrion Daenerys's Hand and later Bran's, after he helps secure Bran's election. His book journey includes Young Griff, captivity and service with sellswords. By the end of the published novels he has neither met Daenerys nor become her Hand.

### lancel · Lancel Lannister
The show has Lancel try to prevent the Great Sept's explosion and die in it. The novels have him abandon his former life and join the Faith Militant, but leave him alive. The explosion is not part of his published book history.

## «Игра престолов»: Аррены, Талли, Мартеллы, Тиреллы

### lysa · Lysa Tully

### robert_a · Robert Arryn, Sweetrobin

### hoster · Hoster Tully

### brynden_t · Brynden Tully, the Blackfish
The adaptation has the Blackfish stay at Riverrun and die when the castle falls. In the book Edmure helps him escape before surrendering it to the Lannisters. He remains at large, although his subsequent whereabouts are unknown.

### edmure · Edmure Tully

### doran · Doran Martell
The show has Ellaria murder Doran and seize power in Dorne. In the books he is alive and directing the Martells' secret plans, while Ellaria argues against further vengeance. The adaptation changes both the family's leadership and its political aims.

### oberyn · Oberyn Martell

### trystane · Trystane Martell
The show has Obara and Nymeria kill Trystane aboard a ship in King's Landing after the Dornish coup. The published novels leave him alive and betrothed to Myrcella. That murder does not belong to the book narrative.

### sand_snakes · Sand Snakes
The adaptation centres on Obara, Nymeria and Tyene; the books identify eight daughters of Oberyn. The three television sisters participate in Ellaria's coup. Euron kills Obara and Nymeria, while Cersei takes revenge on Tyene. Their book stories follow different political missions.

### arianne · Arianne Martell
The show omits Arianne, including her attempt to crown Myrcella and her struggle with Doran over the succession. Ellaria and the Sand Snakes take the political initiative in television Dorne, but their coup is not a direct adaptation of Arianne's plan.

### quentyn · Quentyn Martell
The adaptation omits Quentyn's mission to Daenerys, his failed marriage proposal and his death after trying to claim a dragon. Without him, the show does not give Doran that attempt to secure a Targaryen alliance through his son.

### margaery · Margaery Tyrell
The show kills Margaery in the Great Sept's explosion after she recognizes the danger but cannot get the congregation out. The books leave her alive, released into Randyll Tarly's custody pending trial. Her death has not occurred in the published narrative.

### loras · Loras Tyrell
Television Loras never joins the Kingsguard; the Faith prosecutes him for his relationships with men, and he dies in the sept's explosion. In the books he serves Tommen's Kingsguard. Reports say he was gravely wounded taking Dragonstone, although the assault and his condition are not shown directly.

### willas · Willas Tyrell
The show omits Willas and makes Loras Highgarden's heir. It replaces the proposed marriage between Sansa and Willas with a plan to marry her to Loras. The Tyrell succession and their marriage negotiations consequently differ from the books.

## Тиреллы и Грейджои

### leo_t · Leo Tyrell (Longthorn)

### olenna · Olenna Redwyne
The adaptation has Olenna confess her role in Joffrey's murder to Jaime, then drink his offered poison after Highgarden falls. In the books she remains alive, and her involvement is disclosed through Littlefinger's account. The show also substitutes Loras for Willas in her plan for Sansa's marriage.

### mace · Mace Tyrell
The show omits Mace's sons Willas and Garlan, often uses him for comedy and has him negotiate with the Iron Bank as master of coin. He dies in the Great Sept's explosion with Margaery and Loras. In the books he remains alive and serves as Tommen's Hand.

### balon · Balon Greyjoy
The show has Euron personally throw Balon from a bridge on Pyke. The books also record Balon's fatal fall from a bridge, but do not directly reveal the circumstances of his suspected murder.

### euron · Euron Greyjoy
Television Euron kills Balon himself, allies with Cersei and dies after fighting Jaime. The books give him a dragon horn, his captive brother Aeron and a campaign against the Reach. His mission to Daenerys is carried out by Victarion, who is omitted from the show.

### aeron · Aeron Greyjoy
The show gives Aeron only a small priestly role around the kingsmoot. It omits his conflict with Euron and his imprisonment, revealed in the published Winds of Winter sample chapter, "The Forsaken".

### asha · Asha Greyjoy
Renamed Yara in the adaptation, she attempts to rescue Theon, escapes with him after the kingsmoot and allies with Daenerys. Her marriage to Erik Ironmaker and capture by Stannis are omitted. Those events remain central to her book situation.

### theon · Theon Greyjoy
The show has Theon escape Winterfell with Sansa; in the books his companion is Jeyne Poole, disguised as Arya. Television Theon later dies defending Bran against the Night King. The published book story leaves him a prisoner of Stannis, with his fate unresolved.

### nettles · Nettles
The adaptation merges Nettles's role into Rhaena's dragonriding story. Fire & Blood gives Nettles her own history: she wins Sheepstealer's trust by feeding him sheep, fights for Rhaenyra and accompanies Daemon before escaping an order for her execution.

## Фреи

### forrest_fr · Forrest Frey
The adaptation has the Freys offer Jacaerys passage and support in exchange for Harrenhal. Fire & Blood records no such bargain. Forrest fights for Rhaenyra and dies in the Battle by the Lakeshore.

### lothar_fr · Lothar Frey
The show has Lothar murder the pregnant Talisa at the Red Wedding, later join the siege of Riverrun and die at Arya's hands. She serves his remains to Walder in a pie. The book Lothar is alive and takes no part in that siege; Talisa is a television character.

### merianne_fr · Merianne Frey
The show places Merry beside Walder, who forgets her name, and later at the feast where Arya poisons the Frey men. That feast does not occur in the books.

### roslin_fr · Roslin Frey
Television Roslin largely disappears after the Red Wedding. During the season-six siege Jaime tells Edmure she has borne a son and threatens to launch the child into Riverrun. In the book Roslin is still pregnant at that stage.

### ryger_rv · Ryger Rivers
The show includes Ryger during Catelyn's negotiations with Walder at the Twins. It gives him no separate storyline beyond that appearance.

### sabitha_fr · Sabitha Frey
The adaptation has Sabitha join the Frey lord in offering Jacaerys support for the promise of Harrenhal. Fire & Blood gives no account of that agreement.

### shirei_fr · Shirei Frey

### stevron_fr · Stevron Frey
Stevron tries to moderate Walder's insults during the television negotiations at the Twins. The adaptation does not show his subsequent death after the Battle of Oxcross.

### walda_fr_merrett · Walda Frey
The show has Walda bear Roose a son, then has Ramsay kill Roose and set his hounds on mother and child. In A Dance with Dragons, Walda is alive and pregnant.

### walder_fr · Walder Frey
At the television Red Wedding, Catelyn kills Walder's young wife rather than his grandson Jinglebell. Arya later serves him a pie made from his sons, kills him and uses his face to poison the Frey men. The published novels leave Walder alive.

### walder_fr_ryman · Walder Frey
The show combines Black Walder Frey with Walder Rivers as Black Walder Rivers. That character kills Catelyn at the Red Wedding, helps besiege Riverrun and is later killed by Arya.

### walder_rv · Walder Rivers
The adaptation combines Walder Rivers with Black Walder Frey. The resulting character is called Black Walder Rivers and takes actions assigned differently among the book Freys.
